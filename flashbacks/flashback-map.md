## HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

Every flashback is grounded in a specific sensory or emotional trigger in a present-day scene. The trigger should feel inevitable in retrospect — the flashback earns its place by being the only thing that could follow that specific moment.

Triggers can be: objects, smells, sounds, a word someone says, a gesture that echoes an earlier gesture, a silence that matches an earlier silence.

## NOTES ON TRIGGER DESIGN

- The trigger should be small and specific — not a grand parallel, but a texture that matches
- Objects work particularly well: the sticky note, the BB-8 mug, the GoT fleece, the mango ice cream
- Amy's stillness when frightened is a reliable trigger — it's the tell that opens her
- Sound triggers (Amy's Tantrum Theme, a particular laugh) can work for Nick and Miles scenes
