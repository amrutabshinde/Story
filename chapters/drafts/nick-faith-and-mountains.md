# Nick-Faith and Mountains..

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 5*

---

I hid behind the door so that she wouldn't see me when she walks in with Leo. Miles was sitting in the living room. Leo had called her on the pretext of meeting Miles first time after I was supposedly dead. I was nervous. What would i say when she see me.. 'Hi Amy, m not dead after all!'  would she punch me in the face for all I made her go through? I must say I deserve to be punched.

She walked in through the door quietly , slowly making her way toward Miles. All of a sudden she gasped, "Oh my God, He is back!" she said softly as she put her hands on her mouth. How could she know I was here!!

I looked across the room. Everyone looked shocked as well. Amy stood there ,she kept  repeating 'Oh my God. Oh my God' covering her face. trembling as she cried. I rushed to her. turned her around to face me. Tears rolling down from her eyes she fell into my arms.

' Nick, I knew you were alive'.. she sobbed.. ' No one believed me.. They thought I was crazy'

'I know , I know" I said rubbing her back as she shivered.

And then she wailed! She had forced herself believe I was alive. She had fought the world trying to prove otherwise. All the weeks of bottled up despair and desperation was finally flowing out with her tears. Her cry of anguish would haunt me forever. I had caused all this pain.

'I am sorry' I said.

Miles hugged us both. Leo joined in. Amy was only sniffling now. Something was still bothering me.

I held her by her shoulders.

'Amy,  how did you know I was here when you walked in!" I asked.

She gave a smug smile and said, ' your mobile is charging next to the TV'
