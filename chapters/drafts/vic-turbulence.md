# vic- Turbulence

*POV: Vic | Timeline: year 1*

---

I was managing the event for my college reunion that night. The job wasnt as glamorous but the money was good. Plus the band I organise tours for was on a break for a few months. Every thing was running smoothly so I decide to help the guys at the busy bar. 
I saw familiar faces around from my college days a few years back. I saw Amy walk through the door. I did not know her personally but she was the one of the few who had scored a very good package in campus placement and had become somewhat famous overnight. She looked a bit out of place. While people were meeting and greeting each other she came and sat down on the bar stool in front of me.
' one virgin mojito, please' 
' sure' I said as I began mixing. 
' hey, I know you! ' she said good naturedly. 'You set up the dance that won us the first prize in that inter college competition. I don't think we were ever even shortlisted for it before you.' 
' thanks!' I said beaming with pride. I had set up the stage lightings and the audio for the dance. It was that dance when I knew this is what I wanted to do. ' well everyone knows you of course. The one who got paid the biggest buck.' 
' unexpectedly ' she added smiling.
' I did not think this was your scene.' I remember she would hang out with people who would plan treks and tree plantations. 
' it isn't, honestly, but I had to get out of a lousy dinner. This was my best excuse' she said. 
' that lousy , huh' I said.
'You have no idea ' 
She sat there the entire evening even when the shows were going on. We talked what she was doing now. What I was planning to do. She talked about how here ship load of family lives in Pune and she was so fascinated by my childhood in Dalhousie. She mentioned how she loved travelling and adventure sports. And we spoke about all the places we have traveled. I was having a good time. No strings attached. I did not have to flirt. Since we both had zero chances to hook up. Our worlds were different. And some how that took the pressure off the conversation. I did not have to impress her or try to make her like me. My assistant Tej thought differently it seems. He was making sure no one came to me for any issues or problem , any light that was out or drink that was getting over. He was fending them off cause he thought I was finally giving a girl a chance. He was a romantic. We sat there almost till pack up time. 
' it's getting late. I should get going' Amy said looking at her watch.   
' I can drop you somewhere. Or want me to call a cab.' i asked.
' I live close by. I will just walk.' she said as she got down from the bar stool.' this was fun!' she held out her hand.
' unexpectedly.' I added smiling and she laughed.
Tej called out to me saying- ' we will wrap it up here boss. You don't want to send the lady back home alone so late.' he winked at me.
Amy rolled her eyes. 
' friends trying to set you up?' she asked.
' yeah, sorry' I said awkwardly.
' relation ships are so overrated. You think you found the one and then he goes off with the first shinny bimbo he comes across.' she said angrily.' what's wrong in being single! ' maybe it was the mule talking. She was flushed. And fuming like a angry panda walking towards the door.' you coming or not' she asked bossily turning around.
' yes yes' I replied. I picked up a mango wine and 2 glasses and ran to catch up with her. 
She was already at the gate with her sandals in her hand.
I handed her a glass and she took it. 
' so I guess you are single.' I mocked.
'ha ha' she said as I poured the wine and handed her a glass. ' what is it?' she asked curiously.
' you'll like it . It is a mango flavor liqueur' 
' it is nice' she declared.
Soon she was a tipsy and had to hold on to my arm for support while I held her sandals.
' it is like the world keeps telling you to find someone and that there is a problem if you don't. And it tells you this so many times that you start believing this bullshit.' she said.
' yeh, f  them' I said.
' yeah ' 
' I don't even have time for a girlfriend now. I am working almost 18 hours a day and I am happy. I don't really want to carry the load of any expectations.' I said.
' we should be able to meet someone have a good time and say bye bye'
' agreed.' 

I stood by the door till she unlock it. As she opened the door and threw here sandals in. 
' do you want to come in?' she asked tilting her head. 
' Amy , i think I will leave' I said .
' cmon' she slurred.
