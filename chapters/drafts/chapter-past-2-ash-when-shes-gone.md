Amy had gotten into the habbit of cooking for us and making us sit at the dinning table to eat together. And it had been fun to sit with your best buds and laugh after no matter how the day had been. But now that we are sitting here i realize, Amy never let us talk much! I had no idea what to sy to Leo right now.
