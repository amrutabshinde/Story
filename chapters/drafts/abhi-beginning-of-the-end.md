# abhi- beginning of the end

*POV: Abhi | Timeline: Unknown*

---

I was on a flight to Mumbai from London. The reason was a text form Amy that said -' Abhi , could you please come here urgently, I need you.' I was worried and angry all throughout the flight. Why couldn't she just tell me why she needed me. 
She opened the door to her and Leo's house as I reached there. She hugged me. I hugged her back distractedly trying to figure out if she was alright. Leo was no where to be seen but the house looked just as cozy as before. Amy looked a bit tired but she had tea and snacks ready on the coffee table next to the sofa. Things looked normal. 
' how are you?' she asked as she pulled out of the hug and made me sit.
' why am I here , Amy ?' I said cutting to the chase. 
She looked a bit uncertain as to what to say. She opened her mouth a few time as if deciding what her answer would be. 
' Abhi.. I don't know if I have the right to ask this of you..' she started and I cut her off.
' All you did was just texted me that you needed me and here I am flying from half the world away to you.. you have the right to ask for anything ,Amy. Stop scaring me and just spit it out, please' 
She gave a small smile and took both my hands in hers. Her smile quickly vanished and my heart was filled with an unnamed fear. 
' Abhi, I got diagnosed with ovarian cancer last year...' , she had and they had operated and it was over. But why was she bringing it up now. My ears started ringing. Please God , no! I did not want her to continue.
' They fixed it. But it is back...and it is bad. We tried everything , but it isn't helping.. ' her eyes  were welling up as she bit her lips. ' and I trying to stay positive. To hope it will be fine. But it is not working..it is still spreading fast.' she said desperately. I was numb. I clutched on to her fingers as she spoke. 
' I have been trying to be brave, Abhi, Leo is devastated. I have to be strong for him. But I can't anymore. I am so tired. I want to break down and cry. I dont want to die....Abhi ',she struggled to continue. I pulled her into a hug and she cried loudly in my arms. 
I felt like the walls were closing in on me. Amy, my Amy. I gave her up so she could be happy. This just couldn't be true. I hugged her tight - I couldn't let her go again.  
'Amy, I got you. ' I said as I held her till she calmed down. She needed me to be strong now. She called me here cause she knew she could be weak in front of me. Not to fix things or give her any hope. But just to be there when she was her at her lowest. And she wanted me to assure her that she could let go. I took a deep breath and said, ' Amy, Leo will be fine. I will make sure he is. I promise you.' she looked at me for a few seconds like she was searching my face for answers ' And you?' she asked.  I fought back tears. what a cruel question, Amy. 'what choice do I have?' I said.
