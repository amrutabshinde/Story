# Abhi- what are friends for

*POV: Abhi | Timeline: year 3*

---

I was sitting by my window watching the torrential Mumbai rains late that evening. I had bought a few vada pavs from the little restaurant across the street. Generally Sunday nights were always spent with Amy. But ever since amy started dating Leo , she would be busy spending her weekends with him. I knew I should have been happy for them. They were my best friends after all. They were happy with each other n blah blah blah.. But I couldn't help feeling I was losing her. I did not feel like eating those wada pavs anymore. 

My mobile phone rang. It was Amy, Ok she remembers I am alive, I thought sourly. 

' hello Amy' 

' Abhi... ' she sounded worried.Instantly my bitterness was replaced by concern. 

' Amy, what's wrong'

' abhi, Leo... We had an argument. He stromed out.. He just wouldn't answer my calls now.. He left hours back. I don't know where he is ,Abhi.. I am  scared'.. And it is raining so hard...'

' amy , calm down , he will be just fine,OK.. Let me try calling.' I said as I locked my place and walked towards their house.

I tried calling Leo as I walked to Amy and Leo's place. His mobile was switched off. I did not believe  he was in any mortal danger. It was raining heavily today. He could have just gotten stuck in traffic on his way to anywhere close too. But Amy being Amy must have already imagined every worst possible scenario.
When I reached her house. She was sitting on the sofa sobbing.

' Amy, Leo is going to be just fine. '

' I shouldn't have argued . What if somethings happed to him!' she brust into fresh tears.
She was overreacting. But she always does. She will always worry when she doesn't know where we are. Leo should have realised that. I felt angry at Leo. I sat next to her and put my arms around her.
' Amy, Leo is going to be fine.' I assured her. ' the roads around are jammed with traffic. '
' it is all my fault.. ' she squeaked. 
If Leo died today. I would revive him and kill him again I thought.
' ok, he probably went to his office. Google maps show it will take about 2 hours for him to come from there. Here's what we will do- we wait for another hour. If Leo doesn't come, i''ll drive down to search for him on the way to his office.' 
She did not seem convinced.
' if you want I can drive down there now.' 
I could see almost feel the gears in her head working. Should she send out her best friend in danger to rescue her boyfriend. It was fun at times to see how worked up she gets with these things. There was a lighting and thunder just in time to add to the dramatic effects of the scene.
' no.. I can't have you both our there.' she said desperately. 
' We can send Ash' I joked,' he is depensible'
She smiled.
' cmon , Amy' I said as I saw an opening from here guilt laden tears , ' Leo's gonna be fine.' 
' yeah' she said, ' I am so worried , Abhi.' and she put her head on my shoulders and sighed. 
' Amy, you got me , ok- he is going to be fine. I wouldn't be here if I thought Leo was in any sort of danger. You trust me.?'
She nodded.
We sat there for a while watching the rain and the thunder. I had never seen her cry so much. And I hated Leo for it.
About 30 min later  the door bell rang.

I got the door. It was Leo. Soaking wet from head to toe. He pushed me aside and ran towards Amy. 

'Leo ,' she screamed and ran towards him. ' where were you!!'

'  I am so so so sorry ' he said as he held her hands.

' I was so worried. Thank God you are ok.' she cried.

' My cell died, my car broke down. I hitched hiked all my way here. ' 

' I had no other way of contacting her.. ' he explained as he looked at me .' I am just so sorry.. ' he hugged Amy.
'if something would have happed to you..'  she said crying in his arms. he wiped her face with his wet hands.

' I am fine, I am completely fine..' Leo said and looked at me apologetically as he held Amy. He had sensed I was angry. 
' I should get going.'  I said as I cleared my throat. Amy broke out of Leo's hug and turned towards me.
' don't be silly. You are staying for dinner.'  she said.
' next time' I said and I hugged her.' take care '. 
I walked towards the elevator and Leo followed.
' Abhi, cmon, stay for dinner.' he said as I got into the elevator
' she has cried all evening for you, Leo. Take care of her.' I said angrily as the elevator doors closed. I was furious at him. He should have known better than to walk out without any contact.
 I reached my place. Ash was there eating the vada pavs I had got.

' ssup!' He said his mouth full of food. There gg oes my dinner I thought as my stomach rumbled.
My clothes were wet because of the rains. Umbrellas are practically useless in Mumbai rains. When I changed into dry ones and came out Leo was waiting for me in the living room. He and Ash were talking about something and stopped when I came out .
Leo stood up. ' Abhi, I am sorry...' he began. Now I don't consider my self a violent person. I guess till now I had never hit any one or felt so angry at someone so as to hit them. But Leo hurt Amy! 

Smack! I punched him in the face. 

'you know she deserves better, Leo' I bellowed to him. 

Leo fell back on the sofa his hand over his face. Ash got up from the sofa and ran towards him ,' Abhi, what's wrong with you!' he screamed at me. Leo's nose was bleeding. His hand was now covered with blood. I hit far more harder than I intended to.

' Abhi , step out in the balcony.' Ash ordered. 

I obeyed. I watched Ash get his first aid kit and cleaned Leo's blood off his nose. It wasn't broken- just bleeding. I might have overreacted. Maybe I shouldn't have punched him. But all my frustration of not getting enough time with Amy just to have Leo make her cry had been too much to bear.
It was sometime later that lAsh allowed me to come in. Ash stood in between both of us ,just in case he needed to stop anyone from fighting. I half- expected Leo to hit me back. Leo stood up as I walked in. 

And he came and hugged me. Not at all the reaction I was expecting. 

' Abhi, thanks for being there, dude. ' he said smiling at me with his bloody face and cotton stubs up his nostrils and all. ' I am just so glad that Amy and I have you.'
I was hard to stay mad at Leo. 'Sorry, I punched you.' I said. 
' Next time I mess up, just please hit me anywhere but the face.' he laughed.
