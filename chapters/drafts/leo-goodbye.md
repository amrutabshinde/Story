# Leo- goodbye

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 2*

---

I was back from a dinner with a few friends on a Friday evening. Abhi was watching TV as he ate his pasta. His phone rang. It was Amy. Abhi frowned before he answered it. 
Since Amy had started going out with Vic , Amy and Abhi were not on the best of terms. Amy and Vic insisted that they were not dating and were only in a 'casual relationship', what ever that meant, but Abhi felt they were wasting their time with each other. To be honest, though they made a nice couple Abhi was right - you could never imagine them growing old together. Amy had always been a settle in one place type of person, I could imagine her in a house with kids and dogs hosting people for Diwali dinners, staying close to family and meeting them every weekend. Vic on the other hand couldn't be tied down to a place. He was in event management , he would be touring for events and he loved every bit of it. Since they started dating they have both been giving up on their dreams. Amy had started going on tours with Vic missing wedding nd birthdays at home and Vic had started cutting down on longer tours. Even they knew this wasn't going to work for long. Abhi confronted them, asked them why they were fooling around when they knew this had no future. Amy has been cross with him ever since. So when she called, Abhi wasn't sure if it was good news or bad news.
' hello Amy' he finally answered.
Amy was screaming so loudly that I could hear her across the room.
'Vic broke up with me! Do you hear, we are finally over. Are you happy now!?' she sobbed over the phone.
 Oh, this was bad. I had never heard Amy sound so miserable. 
'Amy,' Abhi said as all the color left his face. ' Are you alright?'
' how can I be alright! I loved him! And you couldn't let me have that. Just because you have the perfect love life doesn't mean you get to judge mine!'
' that's not fair ' Abhi began but Amy cut him off.
' Fair! Don't you dare talk to me about being fair! For the first time I was finally happy! But you had to ruin it. You were my best friend! ' she cried. ' my best friend!' she said and she hung up.
Abhi kept the mobile aside and sat there his covering his face with his hands.
'give her some time , Abhi, she'll calm down'
Abhi did not answer. he just sat there his face in his hands. 
Amy wasn't being fair - Abhi cared for Amy. He was just being protective. He was trying to make sure Amy wasn't going to end up being hurt.
'She is in a shock, let it settle down. ' I tried again.
' she shouldn't be alone' he finally said. 
' I will go' I offered . 

And that is how I found myself knocking on her door - not knowing what to say or do. Amy was a regular at the house. But I was used to a happy Amy at the most a silly Amy. I have never had to handle a crying Amy. To be honest, even when she started tearing up at movies, Ash and I always made way for Abhi to deal with the situation. But Abhi was right, she shouldn't be alone right now. She hadn't realized or just failed to accept how much Vic meant to her. Just as Abhi had feared , the deeper she got in the more it hurt when it was finally over. 

Yes, she was a wreck. Blood shot eyes. Messy hair. And a dejected look that broke my heart. 
'I heard what happened' I said ,'How you holding up'  I added softly.
'I am fine ' she lied as a tear rolled down her cheek. 
'Of course you aren't ' I said feeling sad for her. I walked in and  gave her a hug and she cried softly in my arms. 
'It hurts, Leo' , she mewed. 
'I know ' I said hugging her tightly. 'It will be better soon.' 
She broke out of the hug 'Thanks for coming' she said with a slight smile.
'Of course' I said smiling back. 
'Do you want some tea.' I asked.
' yes , please.' she said.

We ended up making the tea together since I did not know where half of the things were in her kitchen. We sat down drinking the tea on a little table in the kitchen. 
'Did you have dinner ' I asked. 'I can make something if you are hungry'
'Vic got dinner earlier' she said and she closed her eyes to stop the tears.
He loved her as well. These foolish kids thought they could fool around and not get attached. I felt a spark of anger for how stupid they had been. I imagine that was how Abhi had been feeling all this time Amy was with Vic.
' we have tried breaking up before , you know ' she said tearfully. ' ever since Vic met Abhi, he had decided we shouldn't be together. But it did not work. tonight Vic said he took up a contract for a band touring Europe for 2 years.' 
Ouch, that was blunt.
' when does he leave'
'tonight , his flight leaves in a few hours.' she cried. ' I don't know how I am going to face tomorrow, Leo. It hurts so much to even stay awake. To even breathe.'
I put my arm around her.
' we will get through this.' I said. There was no point in telling her to stop crying. ' I promise you , we will' 
 She felt so tiny in my arms. and so vulnerable. No wonder Abhi gets so protective of her. It is heartbreaking to see her in despair. Ever since I have know her she always hopeful even in face of failure. 
' I never even said good bye!' she continued. ' I just shut the door to his face. I thought if I did not he won't go. it was like, we sat there finishing our dinner when he said he is going away for 2 years and I asked him to get out of the house. it is over just like that!' 
Abhi was going to kill me for what I did next. 
' Amy, get ready, let's go and see Vic off. '
She looked up at me confused.
' you deserve closure. You will always regret not saying a proper goodbye' I said putting on my coat.

Amy and I waited outside the airport. Vic's friend said they were on the way. About 30 min later, Amy spotted a familiar van. It was Vic's friends dropping him off. Amy ran towards it while Vic was still getting his luggage off. His friends saw her first and stopped talking. Vic looked at Amy, first confused and then he gave her a smile.
' I should have known you'd come' he said shaking his head. He gave her a quick hug and gave me a nod. They pulled out of the hug but Vic did not let go of her hands. 
Amy seemed a lot calmer than she was at home. 

' I couldn't let you go like that. ' she said biting her lips. ' I am going to be fine, Vic. I really am. It might take some time. But I will be fine.'
' Then I will too.' Vic's smile broadened. I felt a bit of pride at how well composed she was. 
He pulled her into another hug. This time they took their time. I could see him hugging her with his eyes closed like he would just never let her go. Please God, just don't make Vic change his mind now. Let him leave. Else Abhi will kill me for sure. 
' you should leave now. ' Amy said pushing him a bit away. They were just four words but what they meant was she had accepted it. 
Vic understood it too. He sighed. His eyes glistened. Somewhere Amy's relentless positive attitude gives others mad hopes too. He knew if she accepted the end of a season it is the end. 
I moved closer to Amy as Vic moved apart to pick up his bags. 
'Take care of him." Amy said to his friends. 
'Of course', they said and gave her quick hugs. 
I heard Amy breathing slowly ,trying not to cry as she watched them load the bag on a cart. I put my arm around her.
Vic did not turn back. I guess if he did he would have no courage to get on board that flight. Amy watched him till we could not see him any more. And then she hugged me and cried. 

She was quite while we drove back. The sun had started to rise.  When we reached her house she got out of the car , 'I'll be fine, Leo- go home and get some rest' 
'I don't mind. .' 
'I'll be fine, honestly' 
"Ok" 
She sat there without moving for a while 
"Thanks for taking me to meet Vic." she said. "I needed it" 
'Anytime.", I said ,"Now that you are taking advice of a wiser human being " she smiled at that " can i offer you some more wise words' I just realized how much I had missed her smile. I generally find it mildly irritating that she is laughing and jumping up and down for little things butterflies and rainbows.
But last few hour taught me I prefer the foolish Amy to the sad one.
'You will give it to me even if I refuse.' she said smiling now.
'well ..yes.' I resisted the urge to make another joke to make her laugh. I tried to make my face a bit more serious. 'Abhi just had your best interests at heart' 
'I know ' she said frowning.
'And he was right' 
'Of course he was right. He is always right. But couldn't he just be right a few years later' she said her eyes brimming up with tears again.
'It would have never been enough. ' feeling sad for her.
' I know it isn't fair to be mad at him. But right now the only way I am not falling apart is to focus on being angry at him. '
' amy.. ' I began but she cut me short.
'Trust me but I don't know what else to do. It is like every time I start feeling the pain I start thinking about how angry I am with Abhi. It is the only thing keeping me going. It isn't fair. But only way I got to hold it together. I don't want to face him right now. He doesn't deserve me lashing out at him anymore. I know I will regret whatever I say to him. I already feel bad about yesterday night.' she spoke without taking a breath and tears rolling down her cheeks.
'I already lost Vic, I cant lose any one else now' she added softly.
'You are never going to lose us , Amy. At least not Abhi. Even if you stab him right now with a knife he would still die worrying if you are alright' 
She gave a weak smile.
 
Back home Abhi had fallen asleep at the same place on the sofa where I had left him. He woke up when I walked into the room. 
' how is she?' he asked rubbing his eyes.
' she is stronger than we think, Abhi'
