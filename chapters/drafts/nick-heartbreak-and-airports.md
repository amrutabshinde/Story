# Nick - heartbreak and airports

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 3*

---

Here's the thing about airport delays: they're either the worst thing that's happened to you all week or they're fine, depending entirely on who you're stuck with.

Amy had found us a corner. She always finds the corner — I've noticed this about her, the way she locates the wall, the pillar, the edge of any space and installs herself there like she'd planned it. I had chips. My backpack produces things when needed; Amy finds this unsettling and I find her finding it unsettling very funny.

The board said *delayed.* It had been saying it for forty minutes with the confidence of something that intended to keep saying it.

Her phone buzzed.

I watched her look at it. I've got good at watching Amy look at her phone, specifically at messages from one particular contact, because there's this thing that happens to her face. It's not a big thing. Just a softening. Like something in her exhales without her permission.

"Who is that," I said.

"Leo."

I ate a chip.

"What," she said.

"I didn't say anything."

"You made a face."

"I'm eating chips. This is my chip face."

She showed me the screen. *Have you eaten.* No question mark. I looked at it for a second and then I looked at her, still doing the face she didn't know she was doing, already typing back.

I've been watching Leo text Amy from Mumbai for three months. About eating. About sleeping. About whether the auto driver seemed trustworthy. Once — once — at eleven at night, a message that just said *is your door locked* and then immediately *sorry, ignore that.* Amy had stared at her phone for a full minute. She had not ignored it.

"Amy," I said.

"He worries about everyone." Already, before I'd finished her name. Which meant she'd had this argument with herself enough times that she'd gotten fast at it. "He once drove Ash to a hospital at two in the morning because Ash thought something looked weird about a mole. The mole was fine."

"That's not—"

"He got me home. When my back went. I barely remember most of it but I remember him telling me which step we were on going up to my flat." She'd tucked her phone away. Done with it. "He brought me tea once. In the office because  missed my morning cup. "

I looked at her.

"He's come to Bangalore three times this year," she said, in the same flat tone. "He told me it was work each time. I looked up the company he mentioned."

"Amy—"

"I know." She said it like a door closing. "Nick, I know."

"Then—"

"Because." She stopped. The departures board reflected in her glasses. "Last year I let myself believe. For a few weeks I just — let myself think about it. I thought maybe—, when he asked me out for dinner , I though it was a date and all. Turns out we were meeting his new girlfriend  Nat."

I put the chips down. "Ouch" That must have hurt.

"So that Leo for you." Amy's voice was so even it hurt to hear it. "And I am not falling for it again."

I know what she meant by not getting her hopes up. I have had my fair share of disappointments to know that if u expect disappointment you cant be disappointed. To be totally honest , something in me disagreed. Firstly , Leo is not dating anyone right now.  And he constantly make sure Amy isdoing ok. And all the excuses Leo makes up to just come to Bangalore are just a ruse to see her. I mean it almost feels like he misses her.   

"Okay" I shrugged.

"You," Amy said, in her changing-the-subject voice, "don't get to lecture me about this anyway."

"I wasn't lecturing—"

"Nick." She gestured at me. Comprehensively. "I am sure you have had girls falling at your feet since you were 15. That face and those eyes.. you can get any one you want. the girls at the airport are ogling at you and giving me stink eye because they are jealous of me!" She handed me the chips back and laughed. "You don't understand what it's like to like someone who doesn't see you."

I took the chips.

Didn't eat any.

"Nick?" Amy noticed, I had not laughed with her.

I never said it out loud. Even to myself. 

"There's someone," I said.

Amy went very still. 

"We grew up together when we lived in Mumbai. Like she is my dad's best friend daughter." I was looking at the carpet. Airports have the worst carpets. This one was a brown that had given up. "I think I have always loved her. We are very close. Even when my family moved to Bangalore, we keep in touch. Like it was obvious for me there could be no one else I could care about.." I paused. "I've known her my whole life. But she doesn't feel the same about me. Last year she got engaged to someone else."

Amy's hand found mine

"What's her name," Amy said. Very quiet.

"Miles."

"But on the positive side Miles broke up her engagement." , I said brightly. 

"Really!" Amy said smiling now , her eyes shining with optimism she reserves for everyone else but herself.. 

"Then she went to London to study because of all the drama. And she came back to Mumbai last month. And I am going to meet her this weekend" I announced.  

"Ah, I though you came to Mumbai for me."  

"Yeah, I lied." I said sheepishly.

"Are you going to ask her out?", Amy said, putting both her arm around mine and grinning. 

"Maybe, Lets see.. " ,I looked at Amy this time. I used to be scared to ask Miles out because I did not want to make things weird between the only best friend. But since i have met Amy things have gotten a bit less lonier. So i really might ask Miles out on a date 

Her phone buzzed.

She looked at it. Turned the screen toward me.

*Chips are still not food. Please eat something.*

I laughed. Actually laughed — not a performance, just the real thing, the five minutes of pressure in my chest finding the only way out available.

Amy typed back. I watched.

*I'll eat on the plane.*

*The plane is delayed.*

*Then I'll eat when it's not delayed.*

*That's not a plan.*

*Goodnight Leo.*

*Eat something.*

She locked the phone.

" See if fhis doesnt work out , maybe we should get married" she said. 

" Are you proposing to me right now?' i asked laughing.

' ewwe. , no' Amy said wrinkling her nose. ' we can just be like each others back up."

" No thank you" i said. 

" Come on, like given all my failures in love dept, there is no other person I would rather live with for the rest of my life.' she said excitedly. 

" We ont have to get married for that! We can just be there for each other."

" Ok , or we can have an open marriage you can sleep with anyone you want" 

I was shocked at what track her mind run on. ' you seriously need to stop watching television sit com. It has ruined your brain"

She sat cris cross on the seat facing me and put both hand on my shouldes turning me to face her. Her eyes filled with wild imagination.

'Nixk please, like when i post our wedding pic on facebook . All the firls form my school with die of envy. What is the point of having those amazing blue eyes and those greek god like cheekbones if you cant help a friend out!' 

"Wil you leave me alone now  if I agreed."

"Yes. Yes" Amy said making a sign to show promise 

' Fine.' I sighed. ' i will be your backup' 

She lecked me on my cheek. ' husband, we should changethe curtain in our house to some bright floral ones. The ones you have now are very boring"

I put my face in my hands.

My shoulders were shaking. So were hers. Maybe it was not such a bad idea after all
