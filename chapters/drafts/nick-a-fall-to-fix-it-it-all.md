# Nick- a fall to fix it it all

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 3*

---

Ami came limping to office that day. Seems he had sprained his leg while running on the thread mill yesterday evening. He leg hurt so much that he had trouble driving his car. While all of us were concerned about Ami I couldn't help noticing that urmilla seemed downright angry that Ami had hurt himself. 

'Which leg?' She asked sternly.

Ami seemed to be avoiding her. 

Which leg she repeated.

' left one ' Ami said quickly and started looking at the files in front of him.

' did you visit the doc?' She asked.

' I will see him later' 

' did you book a appointment?'

' I will call up and book', he looked like he wanted to avoid being confronted by miles. 

' I'll call up and get one' 

' no need i'll do it myself.' 

' do it not' she commanded.

'I'll call up when I see fit'  a bit loudly now.

' OK m calling up' 

' urmilla just mind your own...' And he stopped in mid sentence.

' yeah tell me its none of my business' she threatened.

Ami glared for a while and let out a sigh. ' OK get one for today if you can' 

The day till evening proceeded as usual. For me the conversation was completely bizzare. I knew Ami n miles had a history. But I never knew what. Ami told once they used to be friend and friends strictly but something hadd happed they weren't so close anymore. Still one could see urmilla making an effort to talk and Ami pushing her away. This bothered me a bit. I did not want it to be I love miles , miles loves Ami and Ami hates miles love triangle. But I had no guts to ask miles what's up with them. Not even before I asked her out in the first place. So here we were going through our normal day after a super bizzare start.  Ami couldn't drive and I had driven miles to office today. I actually waited in behind a tree to spy on when she would come down her house to leave for office. This way I was driving her to office out of coincidence not desperation as Amy was teasing me of today.So I was driving them both to the doctor being the only one with a car. Ami sat in the front passenger seat. And miles sat behind. The tension in the car was a bit disturbing. We reached the doctor in complete silence, except for Ami humming ' kya hua tera wada' . 

The doctor greeted Ami n miles as old friends.I sat on a sofa behind their desks , my existence forgotten.

' where have you been ' asked the doctor.' Last checkup was nearly a year back ' he said consulting the medical history the receptionist had pulled out. Miles glared at Ami, ''  very careless of you two though, urmilla , you of all people know the pains we took to get Amit back on his feet.' 

' she was in London past 11 months, it is my fault' 

The doctor looked at Ami for a while and said,' any ways ,how is it going. ?  ' he got up and motioned Ami to sit on the examination table. 

' it is hurting today, I overdid my exercise on the thread mill yesterday.' 

The doctor touched Ami's ankle. 'Hmm, i need to get an xray, I will give you some medicine so that the inflammation reduces. And a painkiller. Urmilla can I trust you to get him back in 3 days.' 

Miles nodded. 

As we got down to the parking in elevators, miles said to Ami, ' you haven't seen the doc for almost a year. ' she said sadly.

' I had no need to see him. I could walk perfectly' 

' you need to work more Ami, how else can you dance again' 

' there is no point dancing without a partner.' Said Ami rudely.

And he sat in the car . something about that comment hurt miles. She looked like she would cry. Ami looked determinedly on the car dashboard not meeting our eyes. 

We drove to Ami's place in silence again. Ami was still staring at the dashboard. I could see miles in the rear view mirror looking out of the window. Was she anbout to cry? Should i say something. Or should i leave it to ami.I felt like an intruder. I had no idea what was wrong and these ppl took no pains in explaining. 

' Ami, we are home' I said when we reached his place. 

He looked like I had shaken him out of his thoughts . ' thanks guys' he said and got off. Miles got down and sat in the front seat. 

' I can drop miles and come back dude' I said, 

' I am fine ,m fine ' he said and moved backwards. 

It happened in a split of a second. Ami moved a few more steps backward , and tripped on a stone on the pavement. One moment he was waving good bye and next he was on the floor clutching his leg. 

Miles and I rushed towards him. his left ankle clearly in pain. He tried to stand but his left leg gave way and he nearly fell if it wouldnt have been for miles holding him. Miles and I held him by his shoulders and helped him in the elevator. Ami's eyes were watering with the pain. 

Miles made Ami sit on the chair . he struggled a bit against her making him sit but miles proved to be stronger. She pushed him on the chair. she took off his shoes placed his foot on her thigh. She asked me to get a bag of medicines kept in the drawer under the TV. She sprayed some medicine on his ankle and massaged his foot. She did these things like she had done it a million times before. Ami meanwhile had rested his back on the chair's back rest and sat with his eyes closed and watering. ' better now Ami' miles asked. He nodded. She tied his leg in a crepe bandage and pulled out a foot rest and placed his foot on it delicately. I got him some water. And pulled out a soda for myself from the fridge. He gulped down the water as well as my half finished soda. 

' Ami , you seem to really hate your leg.' I said pulling out another soda. 

' yeah yeah' Ami answered bitterly. ' I love breaking it every now n then.' 

' I'll drop miles and come back.  We want no more adventures tonite.'

' m fine dude. U guys can leave honestly.'

' miles you are a pro ' I said ' u fixed him up like a medic'

' I have had a lot of practice on Ami's leg itself' she said and smiled. ' he always kept hurting himself' she looked at Ami who did not return her smile and she stopped smiling.

'What's wrong with you two. ' I finally asked. 'You have been acting so weird all since morn now.' 

' ask Ami, he just refuses to let go of his bloated ego'

' my ego!' Screamed Ami. 

' why are you just not ready to let it go and forgive me. I said m sorry and I mean it sincerely'

' I don't want to forgive you., I just don't want you in my life at all..' 

Miles stared at Ami speechless. 

' you left me alone miles,' continued Ami, ' when I needed you the most. For no fault of mine.' 

' you told me that you can get to your feet without my help. ' miles barked.

' your dumbass boyfriend told me that you guys having trouble because you are spending all your free time tending me' said Ami, hold on urmilla's boyfriend?!

' he what!?!' Exclaimed Miles, it was news to her.

' I was trying to protect your relationship. I knew how much you loved Raj. ' 

' I would never want someone who had promblems with my friends.' 

' anyone would have a problem with what you were doing for me , miles. It is more that even what Anu would do for me' 

' and now you are talking like everyone else. ' she said bitterly.

' that was no to reason run off like. I would have never believed you would leave like that without a word. Specially when I had come to depend on you so much.' 

' you said you did not need me.'

' did I? ' Ami smirked and pointed to his bandaged foot.' I fell 2 days after you left, and I did not walk without a stick for next whole month. My foot did not hurt but I just did not believe I could walk. I never had. I had just believed you. I needed you to tell me I could walk again. But you never called , you never cared , miles. ' 

' Ami, you can't mean it. ' said urmilla ,' You asked me to leave. You said I was smothering you! '
' I did not know you run off like that without a word or fight! We always talk , we always stay and fight.' 
' I tried to!' screamed back Miles, ' I tried to tell you Raj raised his hand to me!'
' he what' asked ami surprised.

' we fought the evening before I left. He said he did not like that I was spending so much time on you. And I said will just never stop till you could walk and he could go to hell of he had a problem. He got so angry he tried to slap me. I loved this guy! And I came to you but you asked me to leave, you never tried to hear me. You were supposed to me my friend!' She sat there silently for some time. 

Ami was trying to get up from his chair. I tried to lend a hand but he refused saying , ' m fine' he took his walking stick and paced the hall for a few min. Then He sat next to miles looking forlorn. 

' that so*** hit you' he said finally as he touched Miles' cheek. 

'Hmm,' said miles.

' I could never believe raj could do that. '

' neither could i, m not sure if he was trying to punch me or slap me, I gave him a classic urmilla defence blow' 

' ahh' that must have hurt ' Ami winced mockingly.

' must have , he was screaming on the floor even before I kicked him.' 

' you kicked him!?'

' twice!'

Ami looked at her smiling proudly. ' that's my girl!' 

And he looked at me as if to say do you still want to date her. 

'We should have had this argument months back.' Miles said.

' true, we would have had , if you wouldn't have run away' 

' don't start it again ,Ami' I said.

'Okay okay' Ami said.

We ordered dinner. These guys caught up on months they had lost touch. I listened and ate most of the time.  I was too happy to get bored.  So  Ami n miles were friends only. And miles had broken up with her boyfriend. I was feeling more n more cheerful by the min.

' ma'am,'  I said to miles, ' if you guys done with your happy reunion, may I have the pleasure of driving you back home' 

' the pleasure's all mine ,sir' she said and laughed. She was happier today than all the days i have been working with her. Ami looked at me with a look that said , ' good start' . should I ask her out today. I have never had trouble asking girls out before. But with miles I was tongue tied. She made me nervous specially when we are just two of us and m thinking about asking her out. 

' can I tempt you for an ice cream, miles' I asked as we reached her house. 'Sure' she said. The parlor was close by so we walked. She talked about Ami and her other friends. How they had a dance group. How Ami had always been her partner. We took the ice creams and walked back, miles still on about her friends. I was wondering the entire time if I should ask her out. But she wasn't giving me any time to speak anything at all. We had almost reached her house again. I was running out of time. She said goodbye, I think I replied. I watched her walk towards her door, and I called out.

' miles,' she turned. ' I ..  can I ask you out for coffee sometime.' 

She looked amused. ' sure nick'  she said.

' Sunday, evening, say 6 o clock' ,

' sounds good' 

' I promise I won't ever try to hurt you... Not after I have heard what you did to ur last boyfriend' . and she laughed. I did it?! Did I just ask the girl of dreams out on a coffee date.
