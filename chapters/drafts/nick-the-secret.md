# Nick - the secret

*POV: Nick | Timeline: Unknown*

---

"Did you leave for the follow-up with the doctor" I asked Amy over the phone.
"I am on my way. The train is just pulling up on the station.' She said
'Isn't Leo driving you?' I asked surprised.
there was a pause and she replied 'He has some meeting this morning, I told him I could handle it"
"You did not tell him" Leo was thinking this was just a follow up to Amy's surgery.
"Nick, no point in getting him all worried for nothing." I did not agree. He had the right to know.  I hated it when Amy 'protected us' from things like that.
"Ok, let me know how it goes, ok?"
"Yep" she said before hanging up.

2 weeks back I had driven Amy to the ER because of severe stomach ache. The whole way she was writhing in pain till the nurses gave her painkillers. Leo was on some event out of town , took him hours to get there. Meanwhile the doctors assumed I was the husband and told me that Amy had a mass in a stomach which needed to be removed -  and they added that it could be malignant. the surgery was done. Amy was home in a few days and  the sample was sent for testing. 15 days after the surgery Amy still hadn't told Leo. Today was her follow up appointment with the doctor. She would know today if it was cancer or not.
I called Amy once I reached there.
' Are you done', I asked.
' not yet, I am next'
' I am waiting outside in the parking.' 
' Nick...you didn't  have to come.' she said , but she sighed with relief  as she hung up again.
I paced the parking while I waited.
