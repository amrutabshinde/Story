# Abhi - lovable enemy

*POV: Abhi | Timeline: year 3*

---

I was at Amy's place that afternoon. Leo was busy putting up curtains in their extra bedroom. It seems Amy was gifting this room to nick .' I prefer him messing up this room than the usual mess he makes in the hall' Leo said when I asked him why was he allowing Amy to do so. 

I did not feel like helping at all. I just can't understand what these guys love so much about Nikhil! Was I jealous of him. Of course I was. I wanted to be what he was in Amy's life. Nick was almost a family member to Amy n leo . his weekend he spent at their house. He and Amy have filled up the house with all the object like star wars swords, lotr rings, harry potter wands and other objects. There are little super heroes all around the house. Most of which nick has gifted her and others he helped her acquire. For Amy it was always Nikhil this , Nikhil that! I hated that guy.

Leo had finished with the curtains and locked the room. Amy had been out for her painting session. 

Just then Nikhil came in through the door . he had the keys to their house! He had four boxes of something in his hand. 

' hey Leo ' he called out. ' I didn't know you were home. ' 

' what's in the boxes nick' Leo asked narrowing his eyes.

Nick smiled stupidly. ' wait a moment. He went in the bedroom and came out with one of the boxes in his hand. He opened it to show a sword of some kind. He flicked on a button and the sword glowed. Those swords in star wars. Amy had been wanting them for ages. 

'What's this' Leo asked clearly unimpressed.

' light saber! ' nick answered. ' it even hums!' 

Leo was not happy. Nikhil couldn't care less. He was moving around the house swinging his sword. 

' nick ' Leo called out , ' how much did they cost'

' no price tag on happiness, dad' nick said still fighting.

' that much!' Leo exclaimed.

' cut it off my pocket money ok'' nick said looking more n more stupid with that sword now.' Let Amy see them , she'll love it.' 

As if on the cue , there was noise of someone opening the door. Amy must be home. 

Nick quickly dump his sword under the sofa and sat down. 

She came in with her painting. 'Hi Abhi' she said and went it to keep her things. Nikhil picked his sword up held it high. ' now listen to the reaction ' he smiled smugly. 

' Àaaaaaaaaaaa' amy yelled from inside. She ran out with a half opened box of her sword jumped around the room. ' light saber !! Nikhil you are the best friend ever , ever .' she screamed and hugged him. In 2 sec she was dancing again. She drew it out and started it. ' it hums!!!' She screamed. ' obi wan Kenobi, ' she yelled, 'fight let us' 
And both of them started fighting in the air.

I couldn't help smiling at how happy they looked, like little kids with toys. After sometime Nikhil had stopped playing while Amy still kept swinging 2 swords in her hand. Leo n Nikhil stood next to each other, Nikhil with his elbow rested on Leo's shoulder, both watching Amy n smiling. ' i got one for you n one for miles too' nick said. Leo just shook his head laughing.

' look what I painted today. ' she took out her painting and sat next to me with it in her hand. it was a peacock feather. It had bright beautiful colors. She stared at it for a moment and smiled.' Isn't it beautiful!' 

' yes ' , nikhil had crawled up on the sofa besides me. Leo besides him. Amy moved the painting towards them. ' awesome' leo said. ' Nikhil looked at amy and smiled.
 'U like it' she asked him. 
' love it' he said . 
' I made it for you, the minute I saw we were going to make a peacock feather, it had to be for you.'  she said . 
Nick looked at her for a few seconds and then said softly ' thanks' .

Amy got up saying to make us some tea.' M sure abhi, these guys wouldn't have offered you anything.' 

Nikhil sat there staring thoughtfully at the painting in his hand. Leo called out , ' nick clean up the mess ' Nikhil wasn't listening. Leo called out to nick twice , nick was still staring at the painting. Leo shook nick and asked, ' dude, u okay?'  Nikhil looked at leo 'she always outshines my gifts' he said. Leo just patted Nikhil's back in response. 

' nick, ' called out Amy on top of her voice from kitchen. ' that fish u cooked yesterday is making my oven stink. You better clean it up.' 

' yes ma'am , just keep the oven door open for a few days the smell will drift off' I heard him say as he moved to the kitchen.
