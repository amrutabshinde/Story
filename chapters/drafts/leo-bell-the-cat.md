*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 3*

---

'but I want to pick you up from the airport, alone ' I complained to Amy over the video call. Amy was coming to Mumbai from Bangalore. It had been 2 weeks since we started officially dating and I still hadn't had the courage to tell Abhi. Even now I had locked my bedroom and lowered the phone volume so he would not know I was talking to Amy. 

' Abhi always picks me up, Leo.' she said.
' i know, but it has been so long since I held you' she blushed. ' how are you going to keep your hands off me!' 
' i will manage ' she laughed. 
' lets just tell Abhi your flight is delayed. Then I can pick you up. We can have lunch and I can drop you back at the airport for him to pick you up.'
' all this cause my boyfriend is too scared of our best friend to tell him' 
' I am not scared , I am just waiting for the right moment.' 
' wuss, let me talk to him.' 
' please Amy, let me break it to him. He is going to go all Caesar on me.'
' how have you broken his trust.' 
' he had told us to not mess with you when he first got you home. ' 
' he did' she said frowning.' how dare he. He is not my dad.' she said but she was smiling. 
' frankly talking to Abhi scares me more than if I had to talk to your Dad.' 
' oh come on. ' she rolled her eyes.
'cause it is him and cause it is you. His precious cat like thingy. In his mind no one is going to be good enough for you.'
' relax , Abhi is like a big cuddly bear' 
' you know grizzlies can tear you apart in a second?' 
' Are you worried he won't approve. '  she asked.
' I am worried that if he doesn't he will be right. He knows us more than we know oursleves. What if he has a perfectly valid reason why we wont work.' 
'oh ye of little faith.  Have a bit more trust in us, Leo'
' I do, Amy, I love you more than life itself. But I trust him more.' 
She smiled. 

that was our moment. Amy glanced at me with a hint of sadness inn her eyes. and I realized I have been feeling sorry for myself this entire day that I never paused to think about Amy. She was missing me as well. And she could not hug her boyfriend because he was too scared of their best friend. What a wuss!
She deserved better. 
'Abhi, I need to talk to you,' I said suddenly getting up.
'ooo..kay' said Abhi looking confused.
