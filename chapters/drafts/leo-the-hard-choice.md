# Leo - the hard choice.

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 3*

---

The more Nick seems to be supportive of Amy moving back, the more it seemed to trouble Amy. Nick handled literally everything with the move. He booked movers, he got Amy's rental lease terminated for less penalty. He helped look for houses for us to live till our house was complete. 
"Leo, did you call the person I told  about tiles. He is good." ,Nick said as he was eating 
"Why does it feel that you cant wait to get rid of me" Amy said frowning before I could answer.
'I am just trying to help' nick said. 
' you just seem so eager to help, like you want me out of here as soon as possible'
Nick took his time to answer. he put down the apple he was chewing on. sighed and replied.
"Do I have a choice.' he paused for a few seconds. Fixing his bright blue eyes on Amy. 'You decided to move. You made this decision on your own like it did not affect me at all. Like it is not going to turn my life upside down. I am just playing along"
' do you have any idea ,how difficult this is for me. To choose between you and Leo. Every night i keep thinking how was I going to tell him that i cant leave you here alone. What if Leo broke up with me.' Amy raised her voice. What was going on! 
My girlfriend, nay, my fiancé was thinking about breaking up with me for her best friend!
' you were going to break up with Leo!?' Nick asked raising his eyebrows so high that they disappeared under his untidy hair. He looked amused and smiled at me as he added, 'for me? For real?'
Maybe I should be hurt. But to be honest I knew Amy loved me with everything she had. For her sacrificing us would be the ultimate sacrifice. Her loyalty did not allow her to leave Nick and be happy. She would have instead stayed back and been miserable. 
I had no idea she was stressing out about moving back. I always assumed her living in Bangalore was temporary. 
 "Since when do 'we' not talk, Amy' I asked. 
'Or we' added Nick shrugging. 'we could have saved a lot of stress just by talking, Amy, like I could have told you I have been exploring moving to Mumbai a few months back.'
' what?!' said Amy still crying.
' Well, miles is there. If you move there is nothing keeping me here.' he said casually tossing some nuts in his mouth.
Amy was too shocked to speak. She just stared at him with her mount hung open. 
' why didn't you tell us this before!' I asked.
' you decided to move.' Nick answered looking at Amy. ' Without even giving it a single thought of what it would do to me! I was like - here I am relocating for you and you did not even care to ask me before planning a move.'
' so you aren't moving with us because you are angry!' I  asked frustrated. 
' no, I am still moving.' said Nick sheepishly, 'I just wasn't going to tell you yet' 
' what are you? 8?!' I screamed.
