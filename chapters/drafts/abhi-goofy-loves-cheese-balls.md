# Abhi - goofy loves cheese balls

*POV: Abhi | Timeline: Unknown*

---

I was waiting for amy outside her office building

, she seemed to be in no haste to come out. I could see her talking to the watchman outside. Typical amy, she takes me for granted always,. If this had been a few months ago I would have been fuming . she would come and I would yell at her, not that she cares. But today, I loved every bit of waiting there for her. Watching he stroll her way out of office , talk to every other person on the way. It was good to know that months of hard feelings had not changed the most precious thing in my life, Amy's friendship. It was the usual Friday night dinner plan, I pick amy up from office, tanu would be home already, leo would be joining in. Just like old times!. She came out of the security gate and ran toward the car. ' hi, ' she chirped, ' u were waiting long?'  ' nah , couple of hours'  I said. She stuck her tongue out at me. As we drove she continued talking about what happed in the day, some book she had read, some sites she had seen, an all myriad of topics. I couldn't help smiling. Amy was back! Life is going to be awesome again. It isn't like I haven't been good when she wasn't there but it is impossible not to miss her. My pet dog goofy misses her too. She usually got these big bag of cheese balls. Goofy used to pounch on her for it. She would throw a cheese ball across the room n goofy would hunt it down. A few weeks back I got a big bag too thinking he'd be happy, he saw the bag and ran out of the door searching for amy. 

It was sad. He wasn't interested in eating them at all. 

Amy made me a stop at a store to get cheese balls. She got 2 big bags. 

I held them as we climbed stairs of my apartment.

As we came close to the stairs, I could hear goofy barking out loud. I have never known him to bite a stranger or chase people, but I still did not want to take my chances, amy hasten to ring the door bell. 

' amy just hold on, let me call tanu n check if goofs alright, he is barking a lot,' 

' cmon abhi, he won't hurt me,' but she knew I won't budge.

Tanu picked up the phone on the third ring.

' tanu , m outside the door, whats wrong with goofy. He is barking a lot'

' oh that explains, he must have seen u enter the main entrance, he is just gotten so excited, he is jump in front of the door barking, u got him something?'

I couldn't help smiling as I said' I got him amy... , can you hold him or tie him, I don't want him pouncing on her' 

' give me a moment' and she cut the call, I could hear tanu talking to goofy over the phone, he barked in response. She scolded him and the barking reduced, tanu open the door. Amy hugged her. ' it is so good to be back' she said, amy grabbed the cheese balls bag and ran to where goofy was tied. He was wagging his tail, leaping as his lease would allow. She dropped the bag and hugged goofy . he licked her face, her hands. His tail wagging wildly. Amy kept hugging him for long, tears rolling down her eyes.the cheese balls lay forgotten.  Tanu reached for my arm and placed her head on my arm. I couldn't help smiling. This was my family complete once again.
