# nick- and then she remembered.

*POV: Nick | Timeline: present day (year 5)*

---

I was about to finish my dinner when I heard my mobile ring. It was Leo. I hesitated. Last time we had spoken was almost 2 weeks ago- we had not left things on the best of terms.I was still in two minds as to whether to be nice or rude to him when I say hello.
But all my doubts vanished when I heard him on the phone.
' Nick, ' he said with such desperation in his voice that all my emotion turned to worry. 'Nick, it's Amy....' Was she alright! 'Amy just remembered your accident. Please just come home' he pleaded. 
' on my way, is she ok?' I asked as rushed out of the house.
' just please come here, she thinks you are dead. '

I heard her cry even before I entered the house. I realized why Leo was distraught. It was heartbreaking. She was screaming my name and howling. The pain in her voice was unbearable. It  was taking all the courage I had to just move towards the door. I walked to the bed room where it was coming from. And there she was lying on the ground , her knees and arms pulled towards her stomach, calling out my name and crying. Leo was trying to console her in vain. Leo looked at me and stood up sobbing , ' she just wouldn't listen that you are alive! Help me!' She had no idea of her surroundings. She was completely lost in the sorrow. Lost in the agony of losing me- her best friend. I really wished I was dead rather than face her now. I spoke her name as I walked to her but she showed no indication she heard. ' Amy , I am here ' I said shaking her , loudly over her screams of ' Nick ' and ' what am I going to do now' I shook her again and again shouting that I was there, still no use.  I held her arm and propped her up to face me , ' Amy , I am not dead' she looked at me but  continued crying ' you left me , Nick, Alone, ' 
I pulled her into a hug ' I am not dead- I am here' she still cried. I did not know why to do. I could feel tears of desperation filling up my eyes. Why won't she believe me! 
I shook her bit harder than I wanted to.' Amy, I am not dead- do you understand! I am alive and I am here!' I screamed. It was as if she suddenly realized where she was , who I was. She touched my cheek with a finger. And her eyes rolled up and she collapsed unconscious in my arms.
I hugged her and cried. 
I did not let her go all evening. I just held on to her unconscious body, while Leo called the paramedics. We both stood watching helplessly while they checked her vitals and treated her.  I couldn't meet Leo's eyes. I was so ashamed of all things I said last time we met. 

This happened a few weeks ago.Amy had made a miniature coffee shop last year. I had loved it. she took almost 6 months to build hers and she had promised me to build one for me too. But since her amnesia I was a nobody. She got around to completing it a month back but she gave it to Abhi. It hurt. I was already losing hope that she would ever remember me and this seemed like the  final straw. It was childish but I was really tired of waiting. 
' I can't do this any longer ,Leo!' I had declared angrily that night as i walked to my car. ' I can't, I just can't pretend things are fine. And wait and hope she will remember me.'  
' Nick, please , she is still the same Amy. She is going to get her memory back and she will never forgive me if I let you walk out of our lives.'
' I am already out of her life!' I lashed out,' Did you not hear how she talks to me! Like I am some stranger.'
' For once, Nick, can you not make this about you! Just stop and think about her and not just yourself.' he spat out angrily.
' How dare you! I let everyone think I died just to keep her safe! And of all the people she knows I am the one she doesn't find important enough to remember.'
'That's not fair ! ' Leo said sadly. I should have stopped from what I said next. But I did not.  
'It never mattered to her then that I was not in her life and it doesn't matter even now' 
Leo just walked away. 
I couldn't believe I had said that! I had been feeling so bad all this time. And now that she was lying unconscious in front of me, I did not know how I could be absolved of my sin. 

When they put her on the bed I sat down at the feet of the bed with my head in my hands. I was ashamed  of myself. She fought the whole world ,even Leo, for me. And I gave up on her. I did not want to face her or Leo. 
Leo was talking to the paramedics. He came and sat on the bed next to Amy. 
' she will be fine' he said.
'I wish I was dead , Leo' I said not looking up.
'I wish you were too,' he replied,' for all the trouble you have put me through' but he patted my shoulder. He forgave me. It felt like a huge weight off my shoulders.
'Is that why you were crying so hard on the floor when my plane crashed' I said smiling.
That got me a kick on my back.   
' Aaah man, that hurt! Ok maybe I deserved it. But Leo, ' I said seriously, ' I am really sorry, for what I said that day , and for all that Amy went through for me, for all that you went through.' I said facing him.
'Shut up you idiot',  he said rolling his eyes, 'Do you need any more proof how important you are to us. ' he pointed at Amy. ' Amy would happily go bear it all again if that is what it needs to be your friend. You have had your share of trauma too. Would you want to wish it all away if that meant not knowing her'
I took Amy's hand and understood what he meant 'Never'. I wouldn't change a single accident or a single heartache or  a single fight if that was what it took to end up here holding her hand and being kicked by Leo. 'Never' I repeated more firmly this time.
