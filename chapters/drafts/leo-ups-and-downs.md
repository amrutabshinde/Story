---

' I cannot bear this anymore , Amy' I said tearfully. ' every time you are out and I don't hear I am so worried I can't breath. '
' I will give it up.' she said softly. 
'i know, but I can't make you give up something you love because I don't have the strength to stand by you.'
' I love you' she voice faltering.
'I know, but I am not strong enough for you.' 

I saw her walking towards my house in the rain. My heart skipped a beat. Was I dreaming or was it actually Amy walking down the driveway in her ridiculously red trench coat? I hadn't seen her in 3 weeks. I missed her. I missed how it felt to hug her. Maybe I was hallucinating. Or maybe she was here because she had forgotten something. Or I must have left something at her place. She is just here to return it. I tried to calm my heart which was beating painfully fast against my chest. You broke up with her, remember? I reminded myself. We are not together anymore. 
The doorbell rang. There she was. Amy. My sunshine. There were drops of rain water on her face. 'Hi' she said softly. 
I tried to say hi but I felt a lump in my throat. All my logic went out for a toss. I pulled her into a hug. she hugged me back. She had one hand on the back of my neck and one on my back as she held me tightly  I could feel warmth enter my body where her hands were touching me -  I felt overwhelming  peace. I did not want this moment to end. I wasn't strong to break away. I wasn't strong enough for her- a voice in my head said. I lacked the will to do what is right for her.
I broke our of the hug with great effort. 
' you should not have come, Amy!' I said I as walked away inside. ' she followed me looking confused. It was breaking my heart. ' I am not strong enough to let you go all over again.' I said as I sank into the sofa. I put my face in my hand to avoid looking into her hurt eyes. 
I felt her hand in my hair and then felt her sit next to me. Our arms touched and she suddenly pulled back. I looked up. Was she hurt?
' Amy? What's wrong?'
She took off her trench coat and her hands were bruised.
'Nothing much.' she said.' Almost healed' she added looking at my horror struck face.
' Amy, what happened ?' I asked. But I knew what it would be. This was why we had broken up. This was what I couldn't be strong enough for her. Last time she went through so much trauma and she ended up taking care of me. 
'i went hiking to snow mountain last weekend.. ' she said.' we got stuck in a snow storm. ' 
I was having trouble breathing. Every time she goes for her adventures I fear for her safety. 
' I tumbled a bit in the snow that is how I got bruised. But it is superficial. Almost healed.' she repeated.

' But something else happened out there , Leo. I fell for a distance and landed in thick snow. I was hurting , couldn't move. It was very cold for a few minutes and then my whole body went numb. I knew if I did nothing I was going to freeze the death. And I almost considered not getting up.'
'Oh my God, Amy' I said and clutched her hand in horror. 
'I hadn't realized  until that moment how much our break up had weakened me. I had just lost all my will to live. It was so easy to just let go then to face another day without you.' she bit her lip.
What had I done! I could feel tears on my face. I did this to her. 
'But I cant die, not like that!' she continued. 'There are a lot of people for whom I have to live for. So I have to fight for my life. For my happiness -' she took my hand in hers. ' Leo it doesn't matter to me what else I have to give up for you. I would do it in less than a heartbeat. I have made my choice . It is hard on you when I go for adventure sports so they got to go. You are my priority! '  she looked at me with challenge in her eyes. She had trapped me. It was the same reason I had given when I left a job opportunity to be with her. I never regretted it. ' you are my priority , Amy. Nothing even comes close.' I had said. And if I deserved to choose then , she should have a choice now. 
'I have been a fool, Amy' I cried. '

 'But then I couldn't do that to you. You'd be so crushed. And who will take care of you. I couldn't die leaving you alone, Leo. Suddenly I was scared of dying. I got up with what ever strength I had to join the group setting up flares to get help.' 
'You were right , Leo,' she continued ' you are my greatest weakness. I have never been scared of dying ,now I am because  of you. But Leo , you are my greatest strength, you are my will to leave. If I don't have you in my life, nothing matters' she said tears filling here eyes. 
'Amy..' I said but she cut me off wiping off her tears.
'Leo it doesn't matter to me what else I have to give up for you. I would do it in less than a heartbeat. I have made my choice . You are my priority!'  she looked at me with challenge in her eyes.
