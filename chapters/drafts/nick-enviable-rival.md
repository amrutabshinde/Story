# Nick- enviable rival

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 3*

---

Leo , abhi n I were standing in  Leo and Amy 's bedroom. It was her bday in a few days . miles was taking her with her girl gang to some resort a day before. we were trying to figure out if we could decorate the room as a surprise when comes back that night.

' I am giving her a raspberry pi as a gift' declared Leo. 

' we could keep the gifts on the bed and decorate the room. She'll be surprised when she walks in' I said.

Abhi was looking around quietly for a while. He visited the other bedroom and came back here.

' I think you should move your bedroom to the other bedroom, Leo..' Leo looked at him with raised eyebrows. ' I mean , this isn't the room Amy would have chosen willingly , there is just one window which opens to south. She can see no sun set nor sunrise.' 

I looked around and felt maybe he was right. This room wasn't exactly fitting Amy's tastes. I remember she had mentioned she wanted the room facing east to be her bedroom when we were setting up the place, but she never pressed it, so I had never considered it again.

' oh man , abhi , you are right! No wonder she has her desk set up in that room '  Leo said..

' yeah, there is plenty of light there too, this room is a bit gloomy for her'

He was  spot on again I thought with a bit of envy. 

It was decided that we set up the new bedroom next Saturday. Amy was going to be out the entire day. That should give us enough time.

Along with miles i had got her multiple gifts, I got her some PSP titles. We found the pendnt of arwen evenstar from lotr fame. Miles had got her a purse. I was thinking if I could get anything more, something that will impress abhi the mighty too. May be I could search for that hexabug she wanted.

That Saturday I came in with my bag of gifts. Leo had moved the bed to the new bedrooom . I helped him move the clothes to the new cupboards. Abhi came in at noon. He carried a carton with him.

' dude you were supposed to be here in the morning' Leo scolded.

' it took me sometime to get her gifts.plus I figured out you two can move the furniture without my help' he said smiling sinisterly.

' what's in the box' leo asked giving him a look of loathing.

' I had collected some gifts for her in past few months. Nothing much , but just a few things she'd like.' 

He pulled out curtains first, ' these I got yesterda, they fit the room decor.' 

The curtain had beautiful bright flower printed on them. The background was light lavender, which made the flowers look brigter. I couldnt help noticing this definitely was something Amy would love. We put them on the rods before abhi pulled out more items from his magic box.

He had got her a wooden box to keep her earnings, there was delicate wooden carvings on the box. Next was a perfume that had a lemon + strawberry type smell. Then a bunch of plastic flowers for her desk. 

This guy almost could think like Amy. She never toldme she loves flowers but her wall papers , her kitchen magnets, the table clothes were all bright flowers. I never realized how much she adored them. That perfume, did Amy not always pick up scents like those, tangy and sweet.  And yes now that I think she loves carvings on wood and stone. Even Leo seemed surprised. Finally he pulled out a little teddy bear and I thought evilly,  he went wrong there. Amy isn't a fur toy person. But I lifted it up and realized why Amy was gonna love it. It was the softest fur I have ever touched. I could almost imagine her rubbing it on her cheeks. She loved soft things. This guy was a god when it came to buying Amy gift! 

We decorated the room. Put on some lights . Amy was to come any minute now. 

' where do we keep the cake ' I asked ,' here or out in the living room' 

' what cake' said Leo instantly. ' oh man , I forgot the cake. ' he exclaimed.

' I'll get it. '  I said.

' wait , let me call and ask if they have those ice cream cakes at sugar shack, I'll go n collect.'

' she'd love it ' Leo said.

Of course she would. How could abhi be wrong I thought as he moved towards the door. He stopped at the lightings we had put up. They we blinking  wildly. He adjusted the mode to make them change colour gradually. Just like Amy does!

I must have been staring at the door for long cause Leo said, ' I know, he just knows Amy like no one else does' 

' if somebody were to ask me , who loves Amy the most,' Leo continued, ' I wouldn't name myself. I'd name him'
