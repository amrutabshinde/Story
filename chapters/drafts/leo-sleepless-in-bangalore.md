# Leo- sleepless in bangalore

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 2*

---

The only furniture in the house was this L shaped sofa that came with the apartment. We both took on side each of the sofa to sleep. As romantic as it might seem sleeping with our heads almost touching each other, I was so exhausted but the early morning flight and moving the boxes around during the day that I instant fell asleep. I woke up around midnight to find Amy missing. A light coming from the kitchen led me to Amy sitting on a table and building her miniature house. She had taken up this new hobby on painstakingly building miniature houses and coffee shops and all kinds of miniatures in the last few weeks. each kit must took days to complete and she had already filled up her room in Mumbai with these little rooms. Even Ash, Abhi and I had been gifted a few. 
'Amy, sup? I said yawing.
'Couldn't sleep'  she replied as she continued tinkering on. She wouldn't meet my eye. Why was she hiding. And then I realized 
' how long have you not been sleeping.' I said sitting on the chair opposite to her. 
' I sleep. Just...late.' 
'Amy, how long ?' I asked again though I already knew the answer. she had been struggling after her breakup.  

She looked up her eyes glistening slightly. ' Nights have been difficult. ' she shrugged and her eyes welled up. ' I need to keep my self busy , Leo, else I will lose my mind... Every night I go to bed and I keep thinking how foolish I have been. ' and now she was crying,' Abhi kept warning me to not get involved with Vic but I did not listen. I was so stupid and look where it landed me. And then I tried to run away form it all .. to this city where I know no body.. and it doesn't seem to be working either. I left you guys and now I dont know if it was a big mistake.' she sobbed. ' my life has become a series of one wrong decision after the other.'
' We are not going anywhere , Amy, you think this isn't working , just walk back and it will be same as always. Take as much time as you want , we will wait. '
'What if it just never gets better..' . Where was my insanely happy Amy!
' Give it time , Amy. ' 
' If it did not work out with Vic. I am scared it wont work out with anyone else. ' 
Please dont say, Amy that , I thought, you have no idea how head over heels in love I am with you. 
' Amy' I said as stared into her eyes. 'You are hurt. And it feels like the end of the world. But this will pass... trust me. Some day you are going to love someone so much and you will realize that they are you first priority always . Nothing even comes close to being with them. Nothing is too big to give up.
