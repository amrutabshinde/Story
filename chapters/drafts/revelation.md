# Revelation

*POV: Unknown | Timeline: Unknown*

---

I was almost at my best friend Ru's door step when she messaged that she is late and will be home in an hour.  She was leaving for her college in less than a week and she still had not started packing. I was here to help.  
Her brother , Bud, was helping their mom cook in the kitchen. 
' hey dude, Ru's out' he informed.
' typical, she just wants me to get most of the stuff done. " , I sighed as I made my way to her room.
As I climbed the stairs like I have been for the past 10 years ,I tried not to think about how scared I was to live a life where I don't see Ru everyday. I think I have lived here more than I have lived in my own house. I even have my own futon bed and a dresser in a den next to Ru room. It was all going to be over just like that.  But now was not the time to wallow in grief. I was happy for my friend's success and I will be supporting her. Hence here I was to help her pack.

I could start with the books, those should be easy. She wont need a lot of comics there anyways I thought as I picked up a huge pile from the book case to put it on the bed for sorting. I took a few steps trying to balance the books and they toppled over all across the floor. A small fancy leather bound book fell out too. Ah, it was her dairy. It fell with a written page face up. She had been maintaining a dairy since last 3 years. I don't think she writes in it everyday, but I have noticed her writing in it on special days.  Not to brag, but I must have had millions of chances to read it without her knowing it but I believe in boundaries or at least I have until today. It was actually the words like 'love him' , 'heart's burden' that caught my attention on the open page. I always had a doubt that she was having a crush on someone. She would have that look when we listened to love songs like she was thinking about an actual person. I felt hurt that she never accepted it. That she felt she couldn't confide in me. I thought I knew her more than her dairy did. ' not really' , the dairy must be saying right now. I did not pick it up but I titled my head to be able to read it better.

' Now that we are going separate ways, should I tell him how much I love him. Will it lessen my heart's burden? If I tell him how much it hurts to love him- will it hurt less? '
 
This wasn't a very happy entry. She had written it a few days back.

' shall I tell him how much it hurt to listen to all the songs with him that make me think of him and say yet nothing. When we were together yet not together. All those times we hugged but I don't see love in his eyes. All those night when he falls asleep on the couch and I sit there looking at him all night. So close yet so far.' 

My heart was beating fast against my chest now. So hard I could hear it thumping. It couldn't mean anyone else but me. But how could that be! And then I heard a scream.
' what the hell! Rock. You are not supposed to read it!, shrieked Ru. she looked terrified. ' get out of my room' she pushed me towards the door. But my legs had turned to lead. I fell to the floor clutching my chest. 'I … Can't .. breath' I gasped. Her fury quickly transformed to worry.
She sat me upright against the foot of her bed.' I will get some water' ..
She handed me a bottle from her purse. ' drink' ,she commanded.' and breath' I gulped down half of the bottle. My heart was still pounding . I rested my head  against the leg of the bed staring at the roof , not knowing what to say or think. One minute past, then two. We sat there in silence.  ' since when?' I croaked finally managing to get the words out.
'Since always' she said with a shrug, her voice daring me to pity her.  Always! But I never even thought about her that way. ' but.. I never..' I said trying to say so.
' I know ' she said and sighed ' you can't help it nor can I,' she said like she was explaining an algebra problem.' It is what it is.' she said calmly but her eyes were brimming with tears. She looked away.  I closed my eyes to stop my tears. This was Ru , I loved her more than any girl friend I have ever had. If I knew she loved me I would have dated her, married her , had kids with her , grown old and died faking that I also loved her. I would do that just to keep her happy. And she knew it too. Probably that was why she never let me know.  She was a fighter. She has always been my rock. I always thought she never needed me as much as I needed her. I have been so wrong! 

Before any of us could say something Ru's brother came in.' what are you two love birds doing!' he mocked us. 
'shut up, Bud' I said . I generally got along with Bud. But he could really be very insensitive at times.
' ah, Are you crying! I can understand. Now you will have to do all your homework by yourself.  '
'what do you want , Bud' Ru said dangerously.
' Ma asked me to check on you. Why haven't you started! What were you doing, making out?' 
' Enough' I said angrily. But Bud might have missed it since Ru had pounced on him and they were wrestling on the floor- hitting each other. I sat on the bed with my feet up to avoid being hit. Soon their mom was in the room scolding them. She then made us start sorting things in Ru's bedroom into different piles- stuff to pack, stuff to throw, stuff to donate. Ru and I had been avoiding each other's eyes while packing. There was this weird tension in the air between us. Ru's mom must have sensed it too, cause she asked- ' Are you two alright?' Bud started to joke about something but his mom silenced him with a look. ' Of course, Ma, we are perfectly fine. Just a bit overwhelmed' said Ru casually.

She had been acting as if nothing had changed. She had been doing it for so long  it must come naturally to her. Hiding her feelings from me , from everyone. But I was still having trouble processing it. ' I am not fine.' I said looking at Ru. She stared back with a hint of panic in eyes.  ' I am not fine, Ru. I am losing my best friend. I am not at all fine with it.' 
' dude it is a bit late for that now , she leaves next week' said Bud mocking me.
His Mom looked daggers at him and he stopped. 
' We will give you two the room' Ru's Ma said. And then she left the room dragging Bud with her.
' We have to talk' I said to Ru.
' What is there to talk about ! ' 
'Why are you going away, why did you take a course so far from home'
'what do you mean' she lashed out, still angry with me for creating a scene in front of her family.
'It is just a way to move away from me ' I asked raising my voice too. 
She stood there staring at me for a few moments. 'I don't know ' she said softly and uncertainly. It stung.
'It will be easier without me around?' I asked feeling tears in my eyes.
' Probably ' she said 
'That's it then!' I cried. 'you are just going to cut me out of your life!' 
She looked alarmed by the thought. 'Obviously not!' she said more certain than before as she moved towards me.
I pulled her into a hug and cried on her shoulder. 
'I am sorry , Ru! I have been such a terrible friend. But I can't live without you. It scares me that we are already not going to meet , if you don't talk to me , I don't know how I will survive.' I hugged her tightly.
She pushed me away. ' stop being over dramatic ,ok' she said but she was also crying. ' we are stronger than this' she said looking me in the eye.
I was so proud of her. 
' I love you' I said and instantly regretted saying it 
' no you don't ' she said and sighed.
' well, you know what I mean.' I said. ' more than I know what I mean' I added.' I can't think of changing what we have. ' I said earnestly 
' ouch,' she said with a wry smile.
' honestly, I mean I can't take a chance on us. I don't know what I'd be without you!'
She smiled her first true smile I had seen that day. 
That evening at dinner. Ru's mom and dad both were in tears how their little girl had grown up and was leaving home. Her mom had taken to cooking her favorite dishes for the past few days now. Both Ru and I ate without interrupting the drama. The only time Ru looked up was when Bud said he was proud of his little sis. Even my mouth fell open at his words. After dinner when we walked back to her room I realized every one always spoke of how much of a support Ru was in their lives. No one ever asked her what she felt. Everyone , including me, were just fooled by the brave façade she put up behind which hid a normal teenage girl - with her crushes and dreams and heartbreaks. I have always been coming to her with problems. Never once did I stop and ask if she was ok. 
' are you ok, you seem quiet.' she asked. 
' I am , are you?' I said. Which seem to surprise her. 
'of course, m great' she shrugged.
' I used to think I know when u are upset but after today I can't really say I trust my judgement any more.' I said softly as we sat on the bench in her balcony.
She took time to answer. She sat there breathing in the cool summer night air.
' but after today I am really fine. It is like a heavy burden is off my shoulders. I suddenly feel so free not having to lie to you!' She placed her head on my shoulder. I kissed her head .
' you think our friendship can survive this' she asked not moving her head.
' If any friendship could  it is ours!'
