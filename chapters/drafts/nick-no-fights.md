# Nick - no fights.

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 3*

---

I was in my backyard with few of my favorite friends on this fine cool summer morning. We were having a nice summer barbeque. My fiancé Miles was sitting across the table with my best friend Amy and Amy's husband Leo. 

"They never fight" Miles said pointing at Amy and me. 
"Or at least not like normal people." Leo teased.
"You remember that time when Nick was not talking to Amy for some reason but he was picking her up from her painting class as usual. They would drive in complete silence.  How weird is that!"
"And that time at some party " , said Leo. "they fought and I was so sure now we finally get rid of Nick from our lives. But there he was still sleeping on our couch and eating our food. Amy was fuming how she never wanted to see Nick again but still cooking food for him." 
"You still married her!' laughed Miles.
"Well I did , didn't I.. I love a crazy adventure" Leo said putting his arms around Amy. 
Amy smiled and looked at me.  We were both thinking the same thing - that one time we had a real fight. This was almost 5 years ago. We had been friends for about 6 months then. 

I had never thought that Amy would believe someone else over me. 
'Get out ' I said, pulling my hair in despair. 'I never want to see you again'  .When she did not move. I screamed 'Get out!'
I was angry at her for ruining this. Just when things were going so well- she sides with the first person she finds who accuses me of misbehavior.

The next day I woke not remembering why I felt so miserable. It took a while for it all to come back to me. How Amy easily wrote me off. I felt betrayed. 
Every time I looked at something that reminded me of her like puzzles or her painting - I had the urge to break things. I hated myself for caring so much for her, for letting her affect me so much. I hated her for thinking so less of me.

She kept calling me every few days. I never answered. She never left any voice mails, except once. After trying to reach me for more than 3 weeks I once got a call late at night and then she left a voice mail. I was so desperate to hear her voice by then, that I immediately heard it.
'Hi Nick,' she said. 'h-how- how are you?', she croaked.' I tried calling you before...  . ' she was crying  I could hear cars in the background. Was she crying on a street! 'I know you don't want to talk to me. I just wanted to say  I am very sorry. I was wrong and I deserve this. But I really miss you. I really do. '.She was sobbing throughout the call.
I missed her too! But she had hurt me. It was difficult for me to forget- to forgive. And yet I still loved her no less. Hearing her cry broke my heart. 

Her house was on the way to my office and I would generally pick and drop her off. I had been avoiding that road ever since our fight. But today I took it , and there she was waiting in the corner for me. Did she know I would come by today or did she wait here for me everyday?
I stopped the car in front of her. I was not looking at her determinedly looking at my steering wheel. She got in. 
We drove in silence.I could feel her eyes on me - pleading for me to talk.  I stopped the car in the parking. 
'Amy, ' I said finally looking at her. ' I am angry and hurt and I can't forgive you.. yet. But I am also not ready to lose you. ' She nodded got out of the car.
"Take your time. I will spend my entire life making it up to you!'  she said as she walked away. 

In the evening I went down to the coffee place where we usually had coffee everyday. I had not asked her to be there but she was waiting at our window bar table with her coffee. She saw me and gave a little smile. I took my coffee and sat down next to her. We had our coffee in silence, together, looking at the cars passing by on the road. Later I drove her home again without talking.
That night for the first time in weeks I slept well. It was like some stitch had dissolved in my chest and I was able to breath again. 
For the next few days that is how we continued just talking what was necessary but still driving together, having coffee. And I don't remember when we got back to talking but that was an important lesson in our friendship- we are miserable without each other. We don't have to talk every day. We don't have to meet frequently. But we need to know that we have each other and no fight or argument is bigger than that. Since then we have fought a lot ,haven't spoken with each other for months, but I know no matter how angry she is if I show up at her door, she will open it for me and I would do the same for her.

Later that day Amy and I sat with our feet in the pool. She asked playfully, 'Did I make it up to you?"
"Hmm, given that you once almost died saving me, were almost attacked because of me, had to fight off the world for me when they said you were crazy..."I smiled," I don't give a damn if u think I am an fucking pirate or blame me for robbing a bank!"
' if you were a pirate , which would be cool , by the way,.. like Jack Sparrow.
' Captain Jack sparrow' I corrected.
' Captain Jack Sparrow,' she nodded and smiled. ' like if you were anything I would still be sitting right here by your side. I love you too much to care.' 
' I know. ' I said .
