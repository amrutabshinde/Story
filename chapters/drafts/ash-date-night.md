# ash- date night

*POV: Ash | Timeline: year 1*

---

We waited in the car for Amy near her apartment gate. Leo was driving us somewhere for a surprise dinner. He had not told us what the occasion was or where we are going or even if we were going to pay for our own food or it was his treat. All Abhi and I knew was Leo had asked us to keep the evening free. I assumed the same went for Amy.

I saw her first, she was walking down the stairs. Abhi and Leo were in the front discussing about some movie. She was wearing a pretty dress and jewelry and some bracelets on her hand. Point is she was a bit more decked up than usual. And I realized with horror what had happened. 
' Leo, did you tell Amy where we are going?' 
' Nah, it is a secret . Just asked her if she was free for dinner tonight.' 
He was smiling ear to ear and I felt like punching him in his perfect teeth. Amy had a huge crush on him and he was a blind fool to not have seen that. She thought he was asking her out on a date, I thought with despair. Stupid ,stupid , stupid Leo. She has no idea we were in Leo's car. I opened my side of door wide and stepped out to spare her the shock when she comes closer.
She saw me and she realized her mistake. She shook her head at her folly and smiled at me. 
'wow Amy , looking good!' Abhi called as she walked closer. 
' stunning' Leo whistled. 
'ah,' she smacked her lips and shrugged. ' what if you took us to some place fancy.' she smiled unusually wide even for her.
' you look nice ' I whispered. 
' thanks' she blinked back. 
I don't think she knows that I know the she likes Leo. Leo is a easy to fall for. He is extremely friendly and generous and he flirts with Amy all the time. Plus he made Amy live with us when she hurt her back and he genuinely took care of her. And Amy is just walking around with her heart on her sleeve. 
Meanwhile in the car Amy was not in too bad a mood. She was still shaking her head slightly and smiling at her mistake from time to time as she tinkered with her jewelry. We were all pestering Leo to tell us what this was about.
All was okay till Leo dropped the bomb.
' ok people. First of all this is a self paid dinner and drinks. 'oh man!' Abhi said.
' and there is someone I want you to meet' Leo continued.
I did not want him to continue. It was like watching a movie scene where you know something bad was going to happen and you don't want it to. I glanced at Amy. She had turned to stone.
' I met someone a few weeks back. Her name is Natasha. And I want to introduce her to my bestest friend in the whole wide world.' Leo blew Abhi a kiss.
' that was the surprise?!' Abhi said disappointed. But he was smiling as Leo puched him on the shoulder.
Amy still wasn't moving. 
' great news , Leo' I said and shut up. 
' awesome, ' she managed to say and she felt silent. I wanted to hug her , poor thing. The whole journey she was quiet looking outside the window which she had opened wide. 
When we reach the restaurant I got down first and held out a hand for her. She looked suprised but took it. I wanted to be as supportive as I could. I even held out my arm for her to hold.she quickly grabed it and her smile returned. It is generally hard to keep her spirits down. Leo came up from behind and offered his arm as well. 
She slapped it with her spare hand.
' what would your lady love say if you walked in with another woman.' she teased.
' let's not start with love and all guys ' Leo started saying. As Abhi pushed him away and held Amy's hand. 
' Mine won't mind' he said. 
And Amy walked in holding both our arms into the battlefield. 
The dinner was uneventful. We liked Nat. Amy left early saying she had a school reunion to attend. As I walked her out with Abhi who was trying to book cabs for her and setting curfew on when she should be home- I wanted to hug her and tell he that things would be alright.
