# Nick - walking back

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 4*

---

I sat in front of the sofa where amy was sleeping, snuggling to the shawl I had placed in her hand.Leo was out in the balcony having just yelled at me for my decision to move to germany. worst was i had not had the courage to tell urmilla nor amy about moving. it had been difficutl breaking up with urmilla, but i could tell by the way she looked at me that she felt i was overreacting, and things would be ok in some time. i couldnt tell her i am moving away making our break up absolute. and i thought the worst was over when i left urmi's house. all i planned to do was meet amy and leo and leave.

And then amy gave me a taste of what I was leaving behind. She had saved me meatballs she made, given that leo was a vegetarian, they were definite made for me. She made me a painting since she finds my house doesnt have enough. As I sat on the floor resting my back on the sofa on which she was sitting she kept blabbering about her life last week before she fell asleep on the sofa. I almost forgot why I was here. Now when the moment to leave finally arrived, It was breaking my heart.To go without even letting her know why. The entire week I had tried to be strong. But at this moment all my resolve was giving way. I did not want to go. I forced myself to think of Miles in my damaged car with blood all over her face. I forced my self to remember how amy was hurt,the scar on her leg still visible. It was safer for them that I left. ' I m sorry amy' I whispered as I took my painting. Suppressing the urge to hug her tight I walked to wards the door. Leo came to the door, ' u r hurting her. At least give her the reason' he said, ' I'll call her once m there, ' I promised.

Amy and Miles learned about me going from Leo even before I could land. There was a msg from Amy that just said. ' you should have told me everything ' 
We never kept secrets , Amy and me . For the past few weeks keeping from Amy that I was the reason Miles was hurt had been the hardest thing I had to do. I had even tried to avoid her but she would keep pulling me into her life again. I knew she would never leave my side even if it got her killed. ' what would you do if you were in my place ' I replied. 

It was harder than I thought, living away from Urmilla. I had called her up on reaching Germany,

'I arrived a few minutes back'

'How was the journey' she said.

'Fine'

'The weather'  

'Is good too, how are you Miles'

'M good.'

'Miles, m sorry. I love you too much let anything happen to you'

'Somethings are worse than dying , Nick'

 I woke up every morning wondering what Miles was doing. She generally left me messages that she has woken up or going to doctor etc. She was on my mind 24/7. Sometime I wondered if she was missing me too. Should i be happy if she did or if she did not.  Urmilla started going to work in about a week's time.

'u sure it is a good idea' I had asked when she told me she would be joining office the next day.

'I will go mad sitting here in my room all by myself doing nothing', she had said.

  

I worked long hours in office and spent rest of the time in the gym working out. now that Urmilla was back to office we had little time to talk. I started putting in more hours. Life was miserable. Time I kept telling my self. Give it sometime and things would be just fine. Amy wasn't helping either. She kept sending me details of how she almost got run over by a truck. Or how she almost fell on the stairs. In her effort to try and prove that there are other dangers too.  

  

I had no message from amy that afternoon, neither of her dare devil tricks nor any random joke. A thread in the back of my mind kept polling Facebook, watapps and cell to see if she updates anything. I turned to Google news to distract myself when I first saw the news of floods in Mumbai. I tried calling Amy's cell , then Leos cell both were unreachable, I tried their landline.. Leo answered. ' hello' he said . 

' leo , it nik, u guys safe home? I heard about floods' 

' m home ,waiting for amy' he said desperately. ' she sent a message when she left office an hour back , can't reach her now.'

My heart started beating faster, she was going to be fine I told myself. 

' leo, how bad is it' I asked finally. ' I tried to drive to her office from east here. Abhi tried west. It is flooded nik. The water is higher than my head too.' 

My heart sank. She is fine I told myself. 

' she can swim' I said more to console myself. ' she'll be fine' 

I kept telling myself the same thing for the next two hours.

This isn't how it was ending I told myself, amy deserved more than the 
friend I have been to her. She deserved all those stories she has cooked
 up with urmilla n me and she and leo grow old together. God please keep
 her safe, I would never let her go again.  
I called up urmilla, ami and ashu were trying to see if police can help.  It felt helpless to sit there not being able to do anything. All her friends are doing their bit looking for amy while I am here calling people waiting for them to call. The TV showed images of flooded areas, people sitting on roofs of houses . at least they were safe. I tried to imagine amy on a building like that. Unable to reach us but alive. 

Then finally the phone rang. Leo was on the call, 

' she is back.. ' he said ' safe and alive' sighing with relief, ' she waddled through waist deep waters, her body has a rash now because of it. But she is safe'

It took me some time before I found my voice, ' where is she' 

' having a bath right now' 

Later it was urmilla who called, ' she is safe' urmi said half laughing half crying. 

' We are driving me to Mumbai' she declared. I wanted to stop her but somehow I knew it was useless to try. I myself had booked the next flight to India.

I tried to talk to Amy before I got onto the flight.she was sleeping leo said, she was running a temperature and the rash on her body had worsened. 

' u sure right she is fine'  , it made me uneasy that I haven't heard her speak 

' she is ill, but she is alive- that's all that matters' he answered.

To my utter frustration, it took me more than 20 hours to finally reach mumbai. Urmilla called saying she'd pick me up. She came alone in her car. She ran towards me and hugged me when she saw me. How I had missed her. For a moment I forgot where we were , all that mattered was she in my arms. She had grown thinner than I last saw her. The scars on her facewere almost gone

' how are you? I said finally.

'Not bad.' She said smiling. 

' and hows amy?  I haven't been able to speak to her at all!'

' she is sick.. But she is alive' urmi said.

'Is it that bad' 

' her face is all swollen. She is running high temperatures. But frankly I am just so glad she is alive. Leo hasn't slept for 2 nights in a row now.. He won't leave amy's bedside.'

' it was traumatizing for everyone.' I agreed.

'But she seems glad this happened , she knows you will be back.'  Urmilla continued .

' I am going back though, once she is ok..' 

Urmila looked at me for a while and said, ' I promised I won't make things difficult for you... ' 

 We drove to Amy's place in silence. Urmilla isn't a person to start weeping, or start pleading thankully,. She understood logic. She loved me but she understood why I was doing what I was doing. Amy on the other hand would be difficult, rules and laws and logic never meant anything to her. There was no way she was going to willingly let me return.

Urmilla must be thinking the same , she wished me ' all the best' as we knocked on Amy's door. 

Ami opened it. ' hello nik' he smiled. 'where is she' I asked. 

Leo came out of the kitchen with a tray, ' nik' he boomed, ' good to see you man' 

' good to see you too , leo' I was going to ask where amy was when she screamed from the bedroom, ' is that Nikhil?' I rushed towards the bed room.

'How are you' I went and I hugged her she pushed me back. I did not care. I was just happy to care. She had a rash on her entire body. Her hands and feet looked swollen too. Was she running a temperature. True I was happy she was alive but it did look like she had had a reaction due to the water she walked in. 

' why did you gave to come' said my sarcasm queen, ' haven't I proved to you that I can very well get myself killed without your help!' 

' I am just so happy that u r alive that I don't give a damn to all your taunts' I tried to hug her again, got pushed again.

' ya right like you give a damn.I don't want you in my house..what if somebody bombs my place right now because of you.' 
 I sat on the bed next to her. I took her swollen hands in mine. The rash looked terrible from this close. 

'Amy come on now let's fight about it later. ' I tried to calm her.

' later! ' She yelled, ' later?! You leave for Germany without telling me, I almost get drowned! How were you going to live with it Nikhil! With the last words I had left with being a quarrel between us!'
' Amy..shut up..' I began . ' I almost lost you! 

' you owe me an apology, and a promise that you will never do it again' she declared. 

' yes you are right' I could be dramatic too.I knelt on the ground , folded my hands and said, ' I owe you an apology, I should not have left without letting you know. I could just not bear to look you in the eye and say good bye. I am sorry , plz forgive me'

' and you'd never do it again?' Amy said. Oh she is so demanding.

' and I'd never do it again' I added. ' forgive me please'  Amy now had big tears brimming up in her eyes. ' do I get a hug now' I said. She hugged me tight. I must have wept there I cant remember. Coming so close to losing her again was more than I could handle. 

'Thanks for coming back' she said.

' thanks for being safe.' I replied. 

' dude keep your hands off my beautiful puffy wife' leo said pushing me away from amy. He had walked in carrying coffee for me. 

We set up the dinner in the hall. Amy couldn't walk. I carried her outside to the sofa. She was heavy, I almost broke a sweat. ' skipped gyming , haven't you' she joked. ' moti,' I said, ' what have you been eating' she pulled out her tongue and laughed. I was getting plates from the kitchen when leo pulled me aside,

' dude, thanks for coming' 

'How could I not come' I said honestly. 

Sometime during dinner amy said

' can you take ami n urmi to ur place tonite. Not really sure I can accommodate all you guys' 

 ' of course you can , we'll just crash on the sofas n carpet' 

She looked around uneasily, ami n urmi avoided her eyes. 

'It is uncomfortable, you guys have had a long journey , sleep well tonite.'

' I will drop them home and come back, I am back from Germany to be with you. I will stand all night in ur house if I have to.' I snapped.

Leo cleared his throat and said' actually , nik, we don't want you guys to stay here. We aren't sure what sickness it is that amy has got , it would be safer for you to stay away for a while till we know.' 

'What rubbish'   I said, 

' nik, I don't want you all ill because of me . '

' are you doing this to get back at me!'

' how dare you! ' she screamed,and i knew I was in trouble from the look she gave me ' m not eating unless you promise that you will go to ur place tonite'

Ok she won, I could not say anything against that. ' fine I'll go,  tonite, but once ur reports come and the doctor says it is ok to be around u, m staying here until I return back!' I said.

 Leo was looking daggers at me.oh I realized my mistake,but it was too late.

' so u are going back' she said softly, and she was going to cry again.oh man. I can't take it now. Mnnn

Just then the doorbell bell rang, God loved me! It was abhi and akki and their wives. 

I finished my dinner quietly. Amy had gone all quiet too.  That is her way of making me feel guilty. And it worked. I felt terrible. She had looked her usual perky self before the argument despite her illness. Now she looked close to tears. 

' all ok sweetheart,' abhi asked as he stroked her hair. 

' yep, just tired' she smiled. 

' why don't you sleep ' tanu added.amy nodded. 

'I'll carry you to bed' I said before Abhi or Leo could move to do the same . I lifted her up. She clung to me like a baby. Wiping her now  pouring tears on her sleeves. I took as long as i could . She had been holding up tears for long since dinner.

'Don't cry ' i said.

'It is just ' she said sobbing ' all this trouble of almost dying seems in vain , if you go back' .  

'Amy, darling, please try to understand.'  She did not answer. 

' take care amy ' I said as I put her on her bed. 

' nick ' she said almost in a whisper, ' i understand why you want to go back'  I sat beside her bed, my face level with hers.

' I don't want to , amy, but I can't stay here. You have no idea how much i want to come back here. '

She nodded, ' i guess I can , no matter how much I want you to stay here, I don't want you getting into any trouble because of me. Just like you don't want to see us hurt.' 

' I still think my case is fairer than yours'  I smirked. She raised her eyebrows.' cmon amy don't make me go away, we'll take all precautions right, you sneeze and cough in handkerchiefs, we'll have no one eating from you plate, no mosquitoes, I'll make sure. Don't make me go please. Who will carry you around, I'll keep worrying all night long if Leo accidentally dropped you on your way to the wash room .Or if that rash has gotten any worse' 

' we managed without you until now' she said sourly .Then she said imitating me, 'C'mon Nick, Don't go back to Germany, We'll take all precautions we can, u can get commandos or body guards to keep you safe. Dont go back please.I keep worr ying if you are alive or someone actually killed you. Or if you are missing us all and crying at nights'

'forget it Amy, there is just no point in trying to explain it to you, you guys treat me like i love leaving you people behind here. you of all people, Amy. Miles and you are the two people in the world I cant think living without. How can I let you guys get hurt for me. "

'Some people are worth melting for , Nick . ' , she said like Frozen's snowman,' You are not keeping me safe by moving away, isn't yesterday enough to prove it,'

''what happened yesterday was beyond my control, but what happened last year with you and now with Urmilla could have been avoided."

'Why are you thinking about the two bad incidents we have had to face. why don't you think of all the good time you are taking away from me. And Miles, look what you have done to her.why doesn't she get to decide if she wants to be with you or not." 

"She will never choose to leave me, even if it cost her her life"

"So she deserves it then, if she chooses to stand by you then she deserves all the risk that come along the your love . We are not kids Nikhil, that you need to keep us safe. you are a friend i refuse to live without..if you still dont listen i will find other ways to get you back"

'Like hell you will, Amy!" i said angrily almost yelling. She was threatening me.  'Just shut up and sleep."

"Like i can do anything else, " she screamed back and closed her eyes pretending to sleep.

She is always this stubborn. If she doesn't get what she want she'll blackmail her way into it. It was frustrating.

People outside definitely heard us scream. Leo raised an eyebrow,  "It is always difficult to put her to bed" he mocked. "Somebody forgot to give me my shawl" she screamed from inside.

Leo answered back "At your service your highness." and ran in with her shawl that was lying outside on the sofa. Amy amy amy, I couldn't help smiling.You cant stay angry at her for long.

Abhi and Akki left after sometime. 

Leo said, "It is getting late guys, u should make a move, Nikhil stays far off on the other end of the town"

Urmilla and Ami gave him identical looks as if to say, 'u r kidding right' .

"Guys i am serious , just get going ok" Leo said , abit uncertainly now thta Ami and Urmilla had folded up their sleeves and stood right in fron of him.

 'U will probably have to throw me if you wan tme to leave this place" Urmilla said dangerously

"And you would have to fight me to throw her out" Ami added.

"It is three against one now Leo" I said. Leo considered the situation for a moment.

"Eh, what ever.. " he shrugged  "I knew you wouldnt leave, was worth a try though . I kept extra blankets under that sofa there. Make yourself  comfortable."

Leo went in to sleep. I crashed on the the sofa, my spot! It felt so good to be back here. Felt like home.I hummed to myself as i adjusted the pillows. I found Urmilla staring at me and smiling ," What? " i asked, "

"U look happy to be back" Urmilla said. As she laid down on the other sofa.

"Spending night at Amy's place , you by my side . Could I ask for more" I said. I might have to leave in a few day, but just now felt just perfect. Urmilla fell asleep instantly, my ring still on her finger. I felt better seeing it.  Did what amy blabber in her room actually make sense, or was it because I wanted to believe her so desperately. This beautiful woman in front of me loved me inspite of the risk it brought along with it. But should I be letting her take the risk...

' dude, stop drooling over miles' ami winked, he caught me staring at her. 

' don't go back nick, ' he continued. I stared at him a bit surprise. Given that he was the one who had told me to stay away from urmilla. ' I hate to see miles hurt nick, we have always watched each others back every since school days. I would never want her to date a guy who can get her killed... But I can't bear to see what you moving away has done to her. She is dragging day after day, nick. Her spine still hurts but she spends late nights in the office.she is avoiding her friends, avoiding parties. I thought it would get better with time, but it keeps getting worse. I have never seen her so miserable, even when she broke up witOwn h suraj, she fought with everyone of us when we siad she was wrong.When I had broken my ankle , everyone gave up on me being able to walk, she was determined to make me walk again. And now she is lost, she has no plan , no direction that she wants to follow. ' 

The day we broke up came to my mind, i had made her promise to not make it difficult for me to leave.she had kept her word. Maybe I was wrong to do so. I hurt her and did not even let her fight back. Some things are worse than dying she had said. Yes urmilla they are, loving and and having to let you go is one of them. Watching you covered in blood is another. 

' I can't live with her in danger for my sake' 

' can you live without her' ami asked,  ' I can't believe I m telling you this but she has the right to fight with you and die for you if such a time comes.' 

I had no answer. Was I being unfair to her. Ami lay down on the carpet and slept. These people had not slept since yesterday. I couldn't sleep. Sometime after midnight I could hear leo in the kitchen. He was talking on the phone. 

' she is sleeping right now. .. A bit warm.. About 100.. 101. Yep, I'll call you , good night sweetheart, I love you too" 

' who you talking to' I asked raising my eyebrows. 

' abhi' he smiled wickedly. ' he keeps calling me to check on amy. I m sure amy is happy that this happened. It got abhi back, it got you back.' He said as he made us coffee. 

' hmm, if it wasnt for floods I would have been sure she got this on herself on purpose... Thx' i said I he passed me my cup. 

He sat on the chair opposite to me. Just like he had done the night I had left for Germany. He was thinking the same 

' so Nikhil , we are back to square one.'

' hmm, I guess so' 

We sat there sipping coffee for a while.

' aren't you going to yell at me or advice me leo' I asked.

' just one, if you want to go back you were right last time to leave without telling amy, she'll never let you go.and once u r there forget her, coz as long as you love her she will keep pulling you back here.' 

'I will have to keep coming back then, if I did not love her I wouldnt go away' 

' baCk to back to square one' leo said.

' leo, what would you do if you were in my shoes' 

He thought for a while before answering, ' I would do just what you did, but that is what makes a difference- not being in your shoes , I can judge the situation much better without letting emotions blind me.' 

' and what is your judgement'

' I think u r being immature by running away' 

Hold on, me immature! How did he come to a conclusion like that.

 leo continued, ' the probability of someone trying to kill you and hurting someone else is small, are you willing to spoil, your life , urmilla's life and amy's n my life for it? Childish of you to bury your head in the ground like that.' 

' I want to believe you leo, I really do'

Leo just smiled. And yawned. He had not slept for 2 nights in a row now. 

I was going to tell him to sleep and that I'll keep a watch on Amy's fever when he said,' dude, I should sleep, take care of Amy for me now, check her temperature ever 2 hours. Call Abhi every one hour and wake me up if it increases.' 

Shamelessly he had put all his responsibilities on me. If I was quiet it was because I was too shocked to speak. He took it as a yes and went in the hall to lie on the couch where I had been lying. Scoundrel!! 

' you dog , I shouted back' 

' love you too' he replied and slept.

I made myself another cup of coffee, and sat on the bean bag next to Amy's bed. 

Leo made sense. He always did. Right here in this house was everything I cared about. And all was well.
