## STRUCTURAL GAPS — CRITICAL

### Abhi and Tanu — Full Scene
**Status:** Unwritten
**Why it matters:** Tanu is currently defined almost entirely through her relationship to Abhi. The most significant gap in the novel. A real conversation between them — where both are present and complicated — is essential before the London arc can carry its full weight.
**Open questions:** Does she know about Amy? What did she do with that knowledge? What did she give up to move to London? What does she want for herself?

### Ash — A Scene About Ash
**Status:** Unwritten
**Why it matters:** Ash is the best emotional radar in the story and is currently used almost entirely as truth-teller and comic relief for other characters' arcs. He needs at least one scene that centres him.

### Miles — Independent Interiority
**Status:** Insufficient
**Why it matters:** Miles exists almost entirely in relation to Nick. What did she do before Nick? What does she want? What makes her angry?
