# THEMES — FORGET ME NOT

---

## CORE THEMES

### Love as Action, Not Declaration
The governing principle of the entire novel. Characters demonstrate love through doing — keeping vigil, driving under false pretexts, bringing tea, staying awake, moving furniture, adjusting fairy lights. Declaration arrives late, if at all.

Scenes must earn their emotional weight through specific, domestic, physical gesture — not through speech that announces feeling.

---

### The Cost of Optimism
Amy's relentless optimism is a choice, not a default. It costs her more than anyone sees. The reader should understand this cost even when the other characters don't.

The amnesia arc is, among other things, a story about what happens when the person who held everything together doesn't remember that she was doing it.

---

### Chosen Family
The friend group is a chosen family — assembled by accident and kept by choice. The recurring Friday nights. The permanent comforter on the sofa. The recliner that is Amy's now. The BB-8 mug. These are the rituals of a family that made itself.

---

### The Right Choice Made Badly
Abhi's arc is the clearest expression of this theme: the decision to move to London was correct. The way he made it was not. He gave Amy up so she could be happy. He has not entirely forgiven himself the execution.

The theme extends: Vic made the right choice (to leave) and executed it badly (ambush). Nick made the right choice (to fake his death) and executed it alone (only Miles knew). The novel is full of people who were right about the big thing and wrong about the small one.

---

### Memory and Identity
The amnesia arc makes explicit what the novel has always been about: we are, in part, the people who remember us. Amy without her memories is not less Amy — but she is Amy without the six years that made her Leo's wife and Nick's best friend. What does she owe those years? What do those years owe her?

---

### Silence and Omission
What characters don't say is as important as what they do. Abhi at the airport. Leo at the door. Nick wearing the peacock feather. The story lives in what is withheld as much as what is given.

---

## TONAL PRINCIPLES

- **Humour as structural tool:** Comic relief releases tension after vulnerable moments. It does not undercut them.
- **Restraint over sentiment:** Emotional beats are quieter than they could be. The louder the moment, the shorter the sentence.
- **Specificity over generality:** A BB-8 mug. Tomatoes and basil. The low, warm setting on the fairy lights. The specific detail is the emotion.
