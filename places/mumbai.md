# MUMBAI — PLACE FILE
*Primary setting of Forget Me Not*

---

## ROLE IN THE STORY
Mumbai is home. The city where the friend group forms, where Amy and Leo build their life, where Nick and Miles live. The place characters leave (Bengaluru, Germany, London) and return to. Its texture should be present but not performed — assumed rather than described.

---

## KEY LOCATIONS

### Amy and Leo's House
- Leo bought it months into their relationship, said nothing
- The proposal is a mortgage, delivered at a construction site
- Friday nights here are sacred — Nick always present
- The east-facing bedroom: Abhi noticed it was wrong. They moved everything for Amy's birthday.
- Amy's fairy lights: low and warm. Abhi adjusts them to her exact setting on the way out, in passing.
- The garden: Leo grows tomatoes and basil. Not a throwaway — it is character.
- Amy's flowers: everywhere, never announced, visible in every choice she makes about a space.

### Nick and Miles's Place
- Amy's spare comforter on Nick's sofa: permanent fixture, not moved on weekdays
- The recliner: Amy has permanently claimed it. This is accepted.
- The BB-8 mug: Amy's, always present here too

### Leo and Abhi's Old Flat
- Ash lives here too
- Where the early group scenes happen
- Where Ash delivers his truths plainly and without drama

---

## THE CITY AS TEXTURE
- Monsoon: present in Year 4–5 — Leo goes missing for a night in the floods
- The corner where Amy waits every morning hoping Nick will drive past — Year 4–5, when Nick is in Germany
- Mumbai is not described as spectacle. It is assumed — the heat, the rain, the traffic, the specific weight of a city that everyone in this story has always lived in.
