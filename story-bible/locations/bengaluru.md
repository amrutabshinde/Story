# BENGALURU — PLACE FILE
*Secondary setting*

---

## ROLE IN THE STORY
Bengaluru is where Amy goes when she leaves — and where Leo follows. It is the city of the transfer, the heartbreak recovery, the meeting of Nick, and the kiss that changes everything. It is a city of becoming rather than belonging.

---

## KEY EVENTS HERE

- Amy moves here for a work transfer (Year 2–3)
- Still working through the Vic break-up. Leo helps from Mumbai.
- Amy meets Nick here. He works in defence research.
- Amy and Nick's first fight.
- Nick tells Amy about his crush on Miles.
- Amy brings Nick to meet the group in Mumbai.
- Leo flies here on a fabricated work trip. He stops Amy's date with someone else. They kiss.
- The Bangalore airport scene: Amy and Nick, delayed flight, Leo texting from Mumbai. Early emotional dynamics. The parallel between Amy's and Nick's respective unrequited situations.

---

## TEXTURE
Bengaluru is where Amy is slightly untethered from the life she knows. Nick arrives into that space. Leo arrives into it and refuses to let her stay untethered. The city itself is not described in detail — what matters is its distance from Mumbai, and what that distance makes possible.
