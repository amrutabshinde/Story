# MASTER TIMELINE — FORGET ME NOT
*All events in real chronological order*
*Cross-reference: story-order.md for narrative sequence*

---

## YEAR 1–2 — BEFORE EVERYTHING

- Amy hurts her back. Leo brings her home to recover — first extended time alone together.
- Leo makes tea for Amy. Amy makes tea for everyone else. This distinction matters.
- Ash and Leo notice Amy's absence after she goes home. Neither says it plainly.
- Leo invites Amy to dinner. She arrives expecting the two of them. It is to meet his new girlfriend, Nat.
- Amy meets Vic at a school reunion. They begin dating slowly.

---

## YEAR 2–3 — VIC, BENGALURU, THE BREAK
- Leo and Amy are both seeing other people — Leo with Nat, Amy developing things with Vic.
- Amy and Vic are together properly.
- Vic takes a European touring contract. Amy finds out at dinner, hours before he leaves. He chose ambush over conversation.
- Airport goodbye: Vic hugs Amy with his eyes closed. He does not look back walking into departures — he knows himself well enough to know he couldn't board if he did. *Then I will too* — when Amy says she'll be okay.
- Leo realises he loves Amy the moment she says she's leaving for Bengaluru. *I could almost hear my heart break.*
- Amy moves to Bengaluru for a work transfer.

---

## YEAR 3–4 — NICK, THE HOUSE, THE PROPOSAL
- Amy is still working through the Vic break-up. Leo helps from Mumbai — calls, texts, presence at a distance.
- Amy meets Nick in Bengaluru. Nick works in defence research.
- Amy and Nick have their first fight.
- Nick tells Amy he has a crush on Miles.
- Amy brings Nick to meet Abhi, Leo, and Ash — first group meeting.
- Leo flies to Bengaluru on a fabricated work trip. Real purpose: stop Amy's date with someone else. They kiss.
- Leo and Amy begin their relationship.
- Leo buys a house months into the relationship. Tells no one. This is how he loves — action before language.
- Nick and Amy: a burglar breaks in. Amy is stabbed. Nick is hurt. Amy unconscious in Nick's arms — he holds on, doesn't let go.
- Miles and Nick get together.
- Leo proposes at a construction site. The mortgage is the proposal. *We can even get married if you want.*
- Amy keeps Leo's sticky note — *call me before attempting any more stunts — Leo* — for years.

---

## YEAR 4–5 — LONDON, GERMANY, THE MONSOON

- Abhi begins pulling away. His feelings for Amy are complicating his ability to be present as a friend. He doesn't name this to anyone.
- Abhi moves to London with Tanu. Airport goodbye: cold pat, doesn't meet Amy's eyes, doesn't look back. The right choice, made badly.
- Miles gets into an accident. A rogue group is targeting Nick because of his defence research.
- Nick moves to Germany to keep Amy, Miles, and Leo safe. Tells only Miles the full truth.
- Amy waits on the corner every morning hoping Nick will drive past.
- Leo goes missing for a night in the monsoon floods. He comes home.
- Nick comes back after the threat is managed.
- Amy and Leo marry.

---

## YEAR 5–6 — THE FAKE DEATH, THE WEDDING

- Amy is threatened by the rogue group — an attempt to pressure Nick away from his research.
- Nick fakes his own death. Sits in a room and listens to Amy refuse to believe he's gone. *In Amy's hope. It's the most indestructible thing I know.*
- Amy collapses hearing Nick is dead — then calls Miles to say: *there's no proof.*
- Leo: wailing on the floor when he thinks Nick is dead.
- Nick comes back after the threat is neutralised.
- Nick and Miles push their wedding — originally scheduled earlier, delayed for Amy's pregnancy.
- Nick and Miles marry. *I'm not getting married without Amy* — no hesitation, from both of them simultaneously.

---

## PRESENT DAY — THE HOSPITAL

- Amy's car accident.
- Amy wakes with amnesia — six years of memory lost. Her marriage to Leo, her friendship with Nick, everything from Year 3 onward is gone.
- First waking concern: Abhi missed a flight because of her.
- Leo is a stranger to his own wife.
- Nick keeps vigil at Amy's hospital bedside. Peacock feather pendant worn every day as an act of hope.
- Memories begin recovering in parallel with present-day scenes.
- Abhi flies from London when Amy texts two sentences.

---

## OPEN / UNPLACED

- Amy's early illness detection — quiet, fully resolved, handled alone for too long. *(Separate from the later recurrence — do not conflate.)*
- Amy's later recurrence — the real collapse, different emotional register entirely.
- Nick: *on floor screaming* when Amy calls about the cancer diagnosis.
- The miniature coffee shop: Amy builds it, gives it to Abhi. This nearly breaks Nick.
- The east-facing bedroom: Abhi notices it is wrong. They move everything for Amy's birthday.
- Abhi adjusts Amy's fairy lights on the way out the door — her exact setting, in passing, without being asked.
- Abhi's box of gifts: curtains, earring box, scent, flowers, the impossibly soft teddy bear. Collected over months from things she never announced but which are visible in every choice.
