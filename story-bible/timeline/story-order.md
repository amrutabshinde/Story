*Order events appear in the narrative, not chronological order*
*Cross-reference: master-timeline.md for real chronology*

---

## STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

The novel opens in the present day — Amy in hospital, amnesia, six years gone.
Present-day scenes run in forward linear time.
Flashback scenes are triggered by present-day moments — a smell, an object, a voice — and recover in parallel with Amy's memory.

The reader discovers the past at roughly the same rate Amy does.

## NOTES ON STRUCTURE

- **Reader knowledge vs. Amy's knowledge:** The reader learns the past through flashbacks. Amy is also learning it — but her emotional relationship to these memories is not the reader's. Track what Amy has recovered vs. what the reader knows.
- **Leo's position:** He knows everything and can say almost none of it. His POV chapters are the most constrained — full of what he cannot do.
- **Nick's position:** He knows Amy better than almost anyone in the room, and she doesn't know him at all. His vigil chapters carry enormous weight in silence.
- **The flashback trigger system:** Every flashback should be grounded in a specific sensory or emotional trigger in the present-day scene. Document triggers in flashback-map.md as they're written.
