# NICK — CHARACTER PROFILE
*Cross-reference: voice.md, timeline.md*

---

## ROLE
Amy's best friend. Miles's husband. Narrator for several key scenes.

---

## VITALS
- **Age at story start:** Early-mid twenties
- **Location:** Mumbai (→ Germany briefly, returns)

---

## PHYSICAL
- Tall — Amy is significantly smaller beside him
- **Batman t-shirt:** recurring identifier, practically a character detail in its own right
- **Peacock feather pendant:** worn continuously during Amy's amnesia as an act of hope

---

## VOICE & MANNER
- Leads with performance, means everything underneath it
- Loud where Abhi is quiet, demonstrative where Leo is focused
- Makes jokes when things are serious — humour as deflection and armour
- Delivers real things quickly and sideways (parallel quality to Amy — the sharing is part of the love)
- Fiercely loyal, almost recklessly so

---

## CORE TRAIT
Generous with everything he has, anxious underneath the confidence. The gap between who he performs being and who he actually is — **this is where his best scenes live.**

---

## KEY FLAW
Makes himself indispensable, then struggles when not needed. Can turn fear of abandonment into aggression before he's noticed. Struggles to receive care — extraordinary at giving it.

---

## LOVES
Star Wars, Doctor Who, GoT, LOTR (shared with Amy — the sharing is part of the love). His Batman shirt. The recliner Amy has permanently claimed. Miles.

---

## ARC
Nick's arc runs in parallel to Amy's — they are each other's answer to a specific kind of loneliness. His unrequited feelings for Miles develop slowly, through his own perception of her, over years.

The fake death arc is the structural and emotional peak of Nick's story: he sits in a room and listens to Amy refuse to believe he's gone. This is the most Nick scene in the novel.

**Critical canon note:** Nick tells **only Miles** about the fake death plan. Not Leo. Not Amy. Miles is the sole confidante. Do not write this otherwise.

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- Fakes his own death — sits in a room listening to Amy refuse to believe he's gone
- *In Amy's hope. It's the most indestructible thing I know* — said as a joke, meant completely
- On the floor screaming when Amy calls about her cancer diagnosis
- Amy unconscious in his arms after the burglar attack — held on, didn't let go
- *I'm not getting married without Amy* — no hesitation, said simultaneously with Miles

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Amy:** The friendship that defines both of them. The answer to her prayer. Never romantic — do not write with subtext.
- **Miles:** His home base. She knows him pre-language. She chose him with her whole self.
- **Leo:** Chosen through shared love of Amy, became genuine.
- **Abhi:** Respect more than intimacy. Nick recognises in Abhi what he himself isn't.

---

## OBJECT CONTINUITY
- Batman t-shirt: recurring, almost a signature
- Peacock feather pendant: worn through the amnesia arc
- The recliner: Amy has permanently claimed it; this is accepted
- Amy's spare comforter on Nick's sofa: permanent fixture, not moved on weekdays
- The GoT fleece: given to Amy
