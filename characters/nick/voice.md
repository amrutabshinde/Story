# NICK — VOICE GUIDE
*Cross-reference: profile.md*

---

## HOW NICK SOUNDS

Nick **performs first, means everything underneath.**

His internal voice is louder than the others. More interruptions. More jokes that land wrong. The gap between what he thinks and what he says is where his best material lives — and he knows this, which makes it worse.

His humour is performative: deflection, armour, real things said sideways. He is infuriated by Amy's optimism and falls apart when it wavers.

---

## SPEECH PATTERNS
- Talks more than anyone, including in his own head
- Jokes when things are serious — sometimes the joke lands, sometimes it lands wrong, he knows the difference and keeps going anyway
- Says real things very quickly, buried in other sentences, then covers them with a follow-up
- Physically loud in the way of someone who has learned that taking up space is safe
- With Miles: quieter. The performance drops.

---

## INTERNAL NARRATION
- Louder, faster, more interruptions than the other narrators
- More self-awareness about the gap between performance and feeling — narrates both simultaneously
- Humour as structural feature of the narration, not just dialogue
- Real feeling breaks through in specific, often physical moments
- In the hospital / amnesia arc: the performance stripped back, the gap between who he is and what he can do in this room — this is where his best narration lives

---

## WHAT HE NEVER DOES
- Receive care easily
- Ask Miles for reassurance (though he needs it)
- Show the anxiety underneath until something breaks it open
- Tell anyone but Miles the things that actually matter — about the fake death, about fear

## SAMPLE LINES

> She'd taken the recliner again. I'd stopped fighting it around month three. It was her recliner now. I was just the one who bought it.

> *She'll be fine,* I said, because Amy was always fine, because Amy being anything else wasn't a category I was prepared to deal with.

> I sat in the other room and listened to her refuse to believe I was dead. I'd known she would. I hadn't known it would feel like that.

> *In Amy's hope,* I said. *It's the most indestructible thing I know.* I said it like I was taking the piss. I was not taking the piss.

> She looked at me like I was a stranger. I smiled like everything was fine. One of us was performing. I'm still not sure it was only me.

## DISTINGUISHING NICK FROM OTHER POVs
- **vs. Leo:** Leo acts before understanding; Nick performs before feeling. Leo's humour is situational; Nick's is structural. Leo is warmer on the surface; Nick is warmer underneath.
- **vs. Abhi:** Abhi observes quietly; Nick observes loudly. Abhi says less; Nick says more and means more than what he says. Abhi's narration has negative space; Nick's has too many words on purpose.
- **vs. Amy:** Both say real things sideways. Amy puts it down and leaves it; Nick buries it under another sentence. Amy's optimism is choice; Nick's optimism about Amy is faith.
