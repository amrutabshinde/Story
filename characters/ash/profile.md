# ASH — CHARACTER PROFILE
*Cross-reference: timeline.md*

---

## ROLE
Leo and Abhi's flatmate. Part of the core group.

---

## VITALS
- **Location:** Mumbai.

---

## VOICE & MANNER
- Best emotional radar in the story — consistently right before anyone else
- Delivers truth plainly and without drama
- Comic relief but more than that — being used as only that is the story's waste of him
- *Stop being dramatic* — his default register, which contains more care than it admits

---

## CORE TRAIT
Sees everything, says the necessary thing, disappears. Needs a scene of his own.

---

## KEY FLAW
*Undefined — needs developing.*

---

## WHAT HE NEEDS
At least one scene that is actually about Ash — not about what he observes in others, but about what he wants, fears, or carries. He functions as truth-teller and comic relief; neither is sufficient on its own.

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- *I think you've always loved her, Leo* — said with infuriating certainty
- *She had a crush on you, you know* — delivered like weather
- *This is not my fault* — defending himself to Leo in the garden, genuinely annoyed
- Asleep with cheek on car window; wakes only for urgent matters

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Leo:** Flatmate. Honest counsel. Tells Leo what he needs to hear.
- **Abhi:** Flatmate. Ash is Abhi's truth-teller.
- **Amy:** Fond, clear-eyed, never fooled by her deflections.

---

## CRITICAL GAP
Ash needs at least one scene that centres him rather than serves the plot around him.
