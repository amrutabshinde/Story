# LEO — VOICE GUIDE
*Cross-reference: profile.md*

---

## HOW LEO SOUNDS

Leo **feels first, understands later.**

His narration is impulsive — he starts sentences before knowing where they go. He acts before the plan is clear. He finds things funny as they're happening. His relationship to Amy's optimism is grounding — she makes the world feel safer and he doesn't fully know why.

His internal voice is warm and slightly disorganised. Not scattered — he's capable and decisive — but his emotional processing happens on a delay. He'll be halfway through doing something before he realises what it means.

---

## SPEECH PATTERNS
- Says real things sideways, often disguised as logistics or dry observations
- Humour is situational — lands because it's specific to the moment, not because it's performed
- Moves toward resolution through action rather than conversation
- Conflict-avoidant: will fix the physical problem rather than name the emotional one

---

## INTERNAL NARRATION
- Impulsive rhythm — sentences begin mid-thought
- Acts within the narration before understanding why
- Feelings described after the fact, often with mild surprise at himself
- Humour emerges naturally from situations, not from intent
- Narration in the amnesia arc: restrained, careful, the effort of not-doing visible in every paragraph

---

## WHAT HE NEVER DOES
- Examine himself before something forces his hand
- Mention career opportunities he's quietly given up
- Announce how he feels before he's sure what the feeling is
- Ask for reassurance — he'd rather just stay

## SAMPLE LINES

> I drove to Bengaluru. I told myself it was for work. I don't know when I stopped believing that.

> She was asleep. I'd brought pineapples. I couldn't explain the pineapples. I left them on the kitchen counter and stood there for a minute feeling like an idiot, and then I felt a little better.

> *We can even get married if you want.* I said it like it was the least important sentence I'd ever said. I meant every word.

> She looked at me the way you look at someone you don't recognise. I kept my face very still. I don't know how.

> I think I've always known. I just filed it somewhere I didn't check very often.

## DISTINGUISHING LEO FROM OTHER POVs
- **vs. Nick:** Nick performs; Leo contains. Nick's humour is armour; Leo's is reflex. Nick is louder; Leo is more focused.
- **vs. Abhi:** Abhi observes and sits with it; Leo observes and moves. Abhi's narration is more precise; Leo's is warmer and slightly less ordered.
- **vs. Amy:** Both move toward people. Amy chooses optimism deliberately; Leo commits without always knowing he's committing.
