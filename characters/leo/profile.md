# LEO — CHARACTER PROFILE
*Cross-reference: voice.md, timeline.md*

---

## ROLE
Amy's husband. Narrator for several key scenes.

---

## VITALS
- **Age at story start:** Mid-twenties
- **Location:** Mumbai throughout

---

## PHYSICAL
- Tall — Amy is notably smaller beside him
- **Physical signature: currently undefined. Needs developing before more scenes are written from his POV.**

---

## VOICE & MANNER
- Acts before he understands why — moves toward things before knowing what he'll do on arrival
- Funny in a specific dry way that doesn't always read as intentional (*we can even get married if you want*)
- Says real things sideways
- Deeply, visibly emotional — doesn't manage feelings so much as contain them until they stop being containable
- Natural host — instinct is to gather people into one room
- Conflict-avoidant: prefers doing to discussing

---

## CORE TRAIT
The last to know things about himself. Files feelings somewhere safe and never checks if the filing was accurate.

**Leo is not the most perceptive person in any room. He is the most committed. These are different things.**

---

## KEY FLAW
Won't examine himself until something forces his hand. Gives up career opportunities without telling anyone. Buys a house without mentioning it. Loves loudly and privately at the same time.

---

## LOVES
Amy (completely). His friends in the same physical space. Growing things — tomatoes, basil (not a throwaway detail; this is character). Comfort. People around a table.

---

## ARC
Leo's love for Amy is demonstrated through action before words — always. This is the governing principle of his chapters.

In the amnesia arc, Leo occupies the most constrained position in the novel: he knows everything and can offer almost none of it. His wife is in the house and she does not know him. His chapters in the present-day thread carry enormous weight in silence and in what he cannot do.

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- Drives to Bengaluru on a fake work trip to stop Amy's date with someone else
- Buys the house months into their relationship, says nothing — *the house is the proposal*
- Realises he loves Amy the moment she says she's leaving for Bengaluru: *I could almost hear my heart break*
- Wailing on the floor when he thinks Nick is dead
- Brings pineapples to sleeping Amy at midnight
- Brings mango ice cream when something is wrong

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Amy:** Everything. Demonstrates love through actions before words.
- **Nick:** Built on shared love of Amy, became its own real thing.
- **Abhi:** Oldest friend. Introduced Leo to Amy. Leo carries quiet knowledge of what Abhi gave up.
- **Ash:** Honest counsel. Ash tells Leo what he needs to hear.

---

## OBJECT CONTINUITY
- The house: bought it months in, said nothing, proposed by mortgage
- Sticky note to Amy: *call me before attempting any more stunts — Leo*
- Mango ice cream: his signal that something is wrong
- Tomatoes, basil: in the garden, tended quietly
