## VOICE & MANNER
- Sharp, pragmatic, reads situations accurately and quickly
- Warmth in the doing rather than the announcing
- Holds things at arm's length until she's decided how to feel — can read as cold, is actually careful
- Specific dry humour: hums *Amy's Tantrum Theme*, calls herself *Aunt Miles* without missing a beat

## CORE TRAIT
Arrives at conclusions through evidence rather than declaration. Came to her peace with Amy by watching her, not by being told.

---

## KEY FLAW
Keeps herself at a distance until she's decided. Doesn't fully exist outside of Nick in the story yet — needs her own interiority.

---

## WHAT SHE NEEDS
Independent scenes. What does Miles want for herself? What did she do before Nick? What makes her angry? What does she think about at 3am?

## DEFINING MOMENTS

- Watches Amy argue with Nick about equality at his parents' house — this is the scene that changes things for her

- *Uncle Nick, are you okay?* — gives the moment exactly the space it needs, then goes to sleep
- Nick tells only Miles about the fake death plan. She carries this alone.
