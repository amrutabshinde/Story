# AMY — CHARACTER PROFILE
*Cross-reference: voice.md, timeline.md*

---

## ROLE
Central character. Every other character orbits her.

---

## VITALS
- **Age at story start:** Early-mid twenties
- **Location:** Mumbai → Bengaluru (work transfer) → Mumbai (returns, marries Leo)

---

## PHYSICAL
- Small — noticeably shorter than Nick and Leo
- Wears glasses
- Drawn to bright colours, flower prints, soft textures
- **Tell:** When frightened or in pain, she goes very still. This is the opposite of her default. It is immediately noticeable to anyone who knows her.

---

## VOICE & MANNER
- Talks constantly, listens completely — both fully true at the same time
- Cannot maintain a lie. Her face doesn't cooperate.
- Physically tactile: full hugs, touches the faces of people she's worried about, reaches for hands
- Says things once and puts them down if they're not picked up. Never presses.

---

## CORE TRAIT
Relentless optimism — not naivety, a **choice** made continuously in the face of evidence. It costs her more than anyone sees.

Write this carefully. Amy is never naive. Her optimism is work. It is the hardest thing she does.

---

## KEY FLAW
Doesn't ask for help until past the point where asking feels possible. So practised at not being a burden that people don't know what she actually needs.

---

## LOVES
Flowers (everywhere — never announced, but visible in every space she inhabits), Star Wars, LOTR, Harry Potter, Doctor Who, Game of Thrones (has read the books, has opinions), miniature crafting, painting, cooking for people, mango, chocolate, soft things.

---

## ARC
Amy's love for Leo is shown through behaviour, not declaration. This is a deliberate craft choice — the governing principle of her chapters.

**Scenes that demonstrate this (written or planned):**
- Late-night vigils
- Panic at his unreachability
- Cooking when everyone is falling apart (*people still need to eat*)
- The night before the wedding *(unwritten)*
- Quietly rearranging her life for Leo without telling him *(unwritten)*
- A monsoon night scene *(unwritten)*
- A domestic sticky-note gesture in return *(unwritten)*
- Amy's memory-return moment *(unwritten)*

**The amnesia arc** is the structural engine of the novel. Amy wakes having lost six years — her marriage, Nick, everything from Year 3 onward. She must rediscover what the reader already knows.

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- Keeps Leo's sticky note (*call me before attempting any more stunts — Leo*) for years
- Waits on the corner every morning hoping Nick will drive past
- Collapses hearing Nick is dead — then calls Miles to say *there's no proof*
- Wakes with amnesia; first major concern is that Abhi missed a flight because of her
- Cooks when everything is falling apart: *people still need to eat*
- Amy unconscious in Nick's arms after the burglar attack — he holds on

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Leo:** Her person. The life she chose. Loved him before she knew she did.
- **Nick:** The friend she prayed for. The Krishna to her Draupadi. The most complete platonic love in the story. Never romantic — do not write it with subtext.
- **Abhi:** The person who found her and decided she was worth protecting before she'd earned it. Tenderness older and quieter than the others. Complicated, resolved, irreducible.
- **Vic:** Loved genuinely and incompletely. Wrong architecture. The right person for a season.
- **Miles:** Hard-won. Solid. Miles came to her peace with Amy through evidence, not declaration.

---

## ILLNESS ARC — CRITICAL NOTE
Amy has **two separate medical arcs.** They must not be conflated.

1. **Early detection** — quiet, fully resolved, handled alone for too long. The fear is private. The register is muted.
2. **Later recurrence** — the real collapse. Different emotional register entirely. Nick screams on the floor when Amy calls.

These are distinct events. Maintain separation in all scenes and timeline references.
