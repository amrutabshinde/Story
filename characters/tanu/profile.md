# TANU — CHARACTER PROFILE
*Cross-reference: voice.md, timeline.md*

---

## ROLE
Abhi's wife.

---

## VITALS
- **Location:** Mumbai → London

---

## PHYSICAL
*Undefined — needs developing.*

---

## VOICE & MANNER
- Reads Abhi's mood from his first syllable — always
- Still rather than quiet: a considered presence
- Warm in a different register from Amy — consistent small ways rather than grand gestures
- Is choosing, continuously, to be with someone complicated

---

## CORE TRAIT
Perceptive without performing it. Knows what she knows and waits for the right moment.

---

## KEY FLAW
*Undefined — the story hasn't given her enough interiority yet. Critical gap.*

---

## WHAT SHE NEEDS
An independent scene. What does Tanu want for herself? What makes her laugh alone? What is she afraid of? She is currently defined almost entirely through her relationship to Abhi. This needs to change.

Open questions:
- Does she know about Amy?
- What did she do with that knowledge?
- Did she wait for Abhi to explain, or did she decide she already understood?
- What did she give up to move to London?

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- *I hope I never have to choose* — Abhi says it. She takes his hand. She understood exactly what he meant and chose him anyway.

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Abhi:** Her husband. She waited him out. She is choosing him continuously and he doesn't always know the cost of that.
- **Amy:** Undefined. Does she know Amy? Does she have feelings about Amy? The story needs to answer this.

---

## CRITICAL UNWRITTEN SCENE
Tanu and Abhi — a real conversation where both are present and complicated. This is the most significant gap in the novel.
