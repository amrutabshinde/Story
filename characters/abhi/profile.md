# ABHI — CHARACTER PROFILE
*Cross-reference: voice.md, timeline.md*

---

## ROLE
The one who found Amy first. Leo's oldest friend. Tanu's husband. Narrator for several key scenes.

---

## VITALS
- **Age at story start:** Mid-twenties
- **Location:** Mumbai → London (relocates with Tanu)

---

## PHYSICAL
- **Worried pose:** head in hands — recurs across years and scenes, almost a physical signature
- Goes very still when others escalate
- Face does less than everyone else's — not performance-oriented

---

## VOICE & MANNER
- Quiet in the way that comes from choosing words carefully
- Rare dry humour that lands harder because of its rarity
- Sees things — assembles details others miss into accurate pictures
- Loves with depth and almost no announcement
- Three steps ahead emotionally; almost never uses this as leverage

---

## CORE TRAIT
Pays such close attention to people that he ends up knowing them better than they know themselves. Built a box of Amy's preferences over months — things she never announced but which are visible in every choice she makes.

---

## KEY FLAW
Cannot metabolise conflict between what he feels and what he believes he should feel. Doesn't know how to be wrong out loud. Made the right choice (London) badly (the airport goodbye). Has never fully explained himself to Tanu, who deserved the explanation most.

---

## LOVES
Tanu — firstly and completely, predates his understanding of love. Amy — complicated, unresolved, irreducible. Order. Light. Things that are right for the space they're in.

---

## ARC
Abhi's feelings for Amy are real but **resolved**. He chose Tanu and he means it. His complexity comes from having made the right choice imperfectly — not from ongoing longing.

He gave Amy up so she could be happy. He knows Leo was the right architecture. He does not regret the choice. He has not entirely forgiven himself the way he made it.

The airport goodbye is the scene that defines his flaw: the right decision, executed coldly, with no explanation given to anyone who deserved one.

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- Collected months of gifts for Amy: curtains, earring box, scent, flowers, the impossibly soft teddy bear — assembled from things she never announced but made visible in every choice
- Adjusted Amy's fairy lights on the way out the door — her exact setting, in passing, without being asked
- Airport goodbye: cold pat, couldn't meet her eyes, didn't look back
- *What choice do I have* — Amy asks *and you?* at her bedside
- Flew from London when she texted two sentences
- *I hope I never have to choose* — to Tanu, on the deck
- Noticed the east-facing bedroom was wrong; they moved everything for Amy's birthday

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Tanu:** His home. Nearly damaged through inattention. She waited him out.
- **Amy:** The complication he didn't choose, couldn't resolve by feeling differently, gave up so she could be happy.
- **Leo:** Best friend. Introduced him to Amy. The architect of the whole story.
- **Ash:** His person for honesty.

---

## CRITICAL UNWRITTEN SCENE
Abhi and Tanu — a full scene from inside their relationship, not just glimpses. This is the most significant gap in the story. What did Tanu know? What did she do with that knowledge? Did she wait for Abhi to explain, or did she decide she already understood?
