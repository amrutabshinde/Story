# ABHI — VOICE GUIDE
*Cross-reference: profile.md*

---

## HOW ABHI SOUNDS

Abhi **observes first, speaks last if at all.**

His narration is quieter and more precise than the others. Shorter sentences. More negative space. He notices things and doesn't immediately interpret — lets observations sit. His humour is rare and dry and lands harder because of its rarity.

His relationship to Amy's optimism is humbling — she shows him what he isn't, without meaning to.

---

## SPEECH PATTERNS
- Chooses words carefully — says less than he knows
- Dry humour deployed rarely; when it lands, it lands hard
- Does not explain himself easily — the explanation feels like exposure
- Loves with almost no announcement; the gesture is always more than the words
- With Tanu: slightly more open, still not fully

---

## INTERNAL NARRATION
- Quieter rhythm. Shorter sentences. More gaps.
- Assembles observations into pictures before interpreting
- Feelings described precisely, rarely — when named, they carry weight because of their rarity
- Three steps ahead emotionally; almost never uses this in narration as leverage
- The airport scene narration: restrained to the point of pain. What is not said is the whole scene.

---

## WHAT HE NEVER DOES
- Explain himself to someone who deserves the explanation
- Be wrong out loud
- Use his emotional insight as leverage
- Announce love

## SAMPLE LINES

> Amy's bedroom faced east. I noticed this on the third visit. No one else had said anything. I moved the furniture three weeks later, for her birthday, with Leo. She never knew it was me.

> She texted two sentences. I booked the flight.

> Tanu read my mood from my first syllable. She always had. I don't know whether to be grateful or ashamed that I never had to explain.

> *I hope I never have to choose,* I said. She took my hand. I think she already knew what I meant. I think she had known for a long time.

> I adjusted the fairy lights on the way out. Her setting — the low one, the warm one. I didn't think about it. I just did it.

---

## DISTINGUISHING ABHI FROM OTHER POVs
- **vs. Leo:** Leo acts impulsively; Abhi acts deliberately. Leo's narration is warm and slightly disorganised; Abhi's is precise and contained.
- **vs. Nick:** Nick fills space with words; Abhi leaves space deliberately. Nick's anxiety is loud; Abhi's is invisible.
- **vs. Amy:** Amy notices in motion; Abhi notices from stillness. Both are observant — the register is completely different.
