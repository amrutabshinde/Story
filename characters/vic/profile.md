## VOICE & MANNER

## CORE TRAIT
Right person, wrong architecture. Loved Amy genuinely and incompletely at the same time.

---

## KEY FLAW
Made the decision to take the European contract alone, without discussion. Amy found out at dinner that he was leaving in hours. He chose ambush over negotiation — not cruelty, but avoidance of a hard conversation he didn't know how to have.

---

## DEFINING MOMENTS
- Hugs Amy at the airport with his eyes closed, like he would never let go
- Does not look back walking into departures — because he knows himself well enough to know he couldn't board if he did
- *Then I will too* — when Amy says she's going to be okay

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Amy:** Genuine love. Wrong architecture. The right person for a season.
- **Abhi:** From the moment Vic met Abhi, he'd decided Amy and Abhi shouldn't be together. Abhi had said it plainly. Vic knew Abhi was right.

## NOTE
Vic is not a villain. He is not written as the obstacle to Leo. He loved Amy genuinely. The relationship ended because of architecture, not character.
