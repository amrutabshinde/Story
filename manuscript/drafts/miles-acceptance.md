' it is such a big house. I am scared to sleep ' she was telling the cook.

That was pretty much her in a nutshell. Talkative and Clueless. I really did not understand what Nick saw in her. 

'hi Amy, ' I called out. ' Everything alright?'

' tell me Nick, why do  you think you should have protected me.
