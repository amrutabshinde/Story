# Leo - Finally

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 3*

---

On the evening I was flying back to Mumbai, Nick , Amy and me were having dinner at Amy's place. 
Nick was telling Amy about his friend who she had met at Nick's bday party. 
'He was asking about you, Amy ' he said smiling slyly.' Like if you would be interested to go out for a coffee with him'
'Oh.. ' said Amy looking confused. Oh, some guy wanted to ask Amy out on a date! I felt like my food was stuck in my throat. 
'Come on .. you need to start dating. '
'Nick, I am not sure.' she said sounding uncertain  as she bit her lips.
'You need to get back in the game' Nick said chewing loudly. 'What do you think , Leo, can she start dating again?' 
Of course I don't want her to go on a date, you big buffoon. why did he have to ask me! Both Amy and Nick were looking at me waiting for an answer. I cleared my throat and said 'Well, if Amy wants to go she can.'
Amy's eyes turned cold. Like she was angry at me for not supporting her.   
'See, even Leo thinks you should!' boomed Nick. 
'Well then, if the great Leo himself says so .. ' she gave me a dangerous look , 'Fine, I am free next weekend.' 
'Perfect!' said Nick and started typing something on his phone. 'Do you have something good to wear?... never mind... we can go shopping.'
Was Amy angry with me for not supporting her? She did not speak to me all evening except what was necessary. I hugged her goodbye when I left for my flight. Did she look sad?

All through the week I was worried about the date. What if Amy liked that guy! What if they start going out. What if they get married! I booked a flight to Bangalore for Saturday. I had to stop her. 
'I have some work over there on the weekend. Can I crash at your place?' I texted her. 
'yeah, sure.' she texted back. 
 
I reached her place late afternoon. Hoping I wasn't too late. she was getting ready as she opened the door. I felt my heart skip a beat when I saw her. She wore an off shoulder red dress and matching earrings. she let her hair down and it curled all over her shoulders. she had makeup on and lipstick... She looked beautiful. I almost forgot to breath. Oh boy, Nick's friend is going to ask her to marry him if I let her go!
'Hi' I managed to say. 'You look nice.'
'Thanks, I am meeting Nick's friend for dinner.'
'Oh yeah , I'd forgotten' I said like I had not travelled all the way from Mumbai to stop it from happening.
That seemed to strike a chord. She straightened her back like she does when she is angry and took a deep breath. I really did not know what I did wrong here.
She was struggling with her necklace I gestured to help. She stood quietly in front of me , her back towards me.She she held her hair to a side as i put it on her. God, she smelled like flowers! My heart was racing.
'Amy you don't have to go on a date if you dont want to' I said summing up my courage.
'Why wouldn't I want to.' she asked frostily her back still towards me as she set her hair back in place. 
'I don't want you to see any one else.' I said quietly.
 'What's it to you.' she said slightly pushing her chin up but still avoiding my eyes. 'Amy, Do I have to spell everything out to you!' I said frustrated and pulled her arm to make her face me. I pulled a bit harder than I intended to, pulling her very close to me. So close that I could feel her breath on my neck.
'How can I know for sure unless you do, Leo!' she said faking calm but her cheeks had gone all red and a little drops of sweat appeared on her powdered forehead. My heart was ponding in my chest. She was so close - could she hear it? She was breathing slowly, staring at me - challenging me to answer. She was so close that her perfume filled my head. So close that I could feel her warmth . I could see where her mascara was a bit smudged already. Where she missed a spot on her lip with the lipstick. And I leaned in and kissed her. Oh my God, I was kissing her. And she kissed me back. I was kissing Amy, finally! After all this time, I was finally kissing the girl I was in love with. I could feel her hand on my neck as I held her by her waist. The room had completely dissolved around us. We broke apart breathless my mind still reeling. 
'I .. that.. it was .. ' she stammered her cheek all red trying to hold back a smile.
'It was nice! ' I said amused at how cute she looked when she did not know what to say.
"I have a date" , she blurted out.
'Really?'  That is what she was thinking about right now? 'Right, right.. you do..' I replied.
'I am getting late' she said looking at her wrist which had no watch.
'yeah' I held back a laugh.
'Stop it, Leo,' she hit me playfully and I held her wrist.
' Go on on your date then ' I teased.
Then finally it occurred to her. 'I should call Nick and cancel' 
'Cancel , really!?' I said kissing her fingers and letting her hand go. She blushed even more. 'I'll gwt some water.' I said to give her some time to compose herself as I moved towards the kitchen.
I could hear her talking to Nick on the phone.
'Nick, I was getting ready for today. Leo was here and we were talking and one thing led to the other and we kissed... Yes yes, stop screaming..So I know I am sorry. Not sorry. But can I cancel the date. ..I owe you Nick. Yes, we did.. no.. it wasn't.. oh my god..hang up now...  bye Nick... bye.. am hanging up.. bye... bye.'
She sat on the sofa smiling to herself. As I sat next to her she put her head on my chest , I put my arm around her and kissed her forehead and sighed. 
'I love you, Amy!' 
'I have always loved you, Leo' she replied.
"We just never got the timing right '
'Until now' she smiled and moved closer.
'Finally!'
