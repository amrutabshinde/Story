# Abhi - an evening to remember

---

It is just the perfect evening; I think as I help Tanu put away the dinner dishes. We have just had an awesome dinner, Tanu, me and Amy. I got late till we actually sat down for eating, but the cooking part was most enjoyable. Amy kept suggesting weird ideas of making the dishes taste different, and Tanu actually tried some of them. I generally stay away from kitchen but this time I sat on the dinner table watching the two. Two of the most special people in my life- Tanu my wife and Amy my best friend.

Tanu is quietly humming to herself as I pass on the dishes to her. And Amy is outside lying on the couch, watching Pokemon. My pet dog, Goofy actually sits down next to the couch and watches cartoon with her. Guess all he cares for is to sit next to her as she keeps running her hands through his fur. Both of then unnaturally quite today, I thought to myself.

Just then Tanu calls me quietly, “Abhi, look at that.” She says as she points to the couch smiling. I couldn’t help smiling myself. Amy had dozed off to sleep. One hand on Goofy, who sat quietly, And other had the remote. And whatever household trick Tanu and Amy had used to make the hair shine had worked. Her hair shone beautifully as they fell across her face. Tanu got a blanket as i took the remote from Amy’s hand. I put placed her hands up in a comfortable position. And I sit down on the floor near her head. Tanu sits next to me as i switch the TV off. I wish I can stop time here. I put my arms around Tanu and pull her closer. She obliges but says “You’ll wake her up, Abhi.” And she looks at her and smiles places her head on my shoulder. This would have been a perfect scene. Had it not been for the door bell. Luckily she not wake up. Tanu gets up to get the door. It is Leo. “Hello, “he booms. And Tanu shushes him. “Amy is sleeping” There he enters his annoyingly smiley face. And untidy hair. He was supposed to pick her up from my place. “Oh , is it “ he said and rushed in. “Hey Abhi” he called as I shushed him too. He is annoying. But Amy loves him so much , I have no choice but to bear him. He stared at her for some time and sat on the floor next to me. And placed a kiss on her forehead. And again. And again. I was resisting my urge to kick him away. Let my little girl sleep. “Have you had dinner?” Tanu asked him. He looked up making sad face, “Nah.. n m hungry. You got something to eat?” Tanu smiled and went to the kitchen, the idiot followed.

Within a few minutes there was laughter in the kitchen. I could see Tanu and Leo sitting on the dining table and Tanu laughing with her head in her arms. I Couldn’t help smiling. This was my family completed. No matter how much i found leo irritating he i knew he was the one for Amy. He loved her just as i would have liked someone to. I put my head back on the sofa and i could almost hear her breath. I wish i have a daughter like her. On second thoughts, one is enough. Looks like Leo is done with his dinner. He asked Tanu to get a shawl and put it in Amy’s hand. She snuggled to the shawl. And he ran his hand across her hair. “Could you  keep some pillows on the floor. In case she rolls over”. Tanu just patted his arm in reply.  He stood there looking at her for a few moments and sighed.. “I’ll pick her up tomorrow then.” . “Cool, “ I said. “Come over for breakfast”. He gave me a quizzical smile and left.

Tanu and me got some pillows and placed them on the floor. I was in half mind to sleep on the carpet. She looked so cute snuggling to the shawl clutching her blanket and sleepy peacefully. I kissed her good night and she smiled in her sleep. Tanu switched off the lights and we made our way to sleep.
