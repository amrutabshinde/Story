# Leo - i hate to say goodbye

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 3*

---

When i was first informed i was going to NY for 2 months last month it did not seem as a long time.  Nor to me nor to Amy. We celebrated that eve, she did say she’d miss me but it did not bother me much until last week. Somewhere during the shopping and the packing my heart had started sinking. How was I to live without being able to hold her for next two months. Last whole week, she had taken up the job of cooking breakfast for me n my roommates, we were having dinners together too.

Here we sat in the back seat of Abhi’s car, Amy n me. Akki in the front n Abhi on the wheels. Amy was quite. She was holding my hand, head a bit bowed.  Abhi was trying to lighten up the mood.

“Leo,“  he said, ” m not sure if I’d miss you dude, but m going miss Amy’s breakfast when u r gone”

Well, at least she smiled. I patted her cheek with my free hand and she smiled back again. I was to leave in about 6 hours early morn. She was going to wait at our apartment and come to see me off. I did not know if i wanted her to. I couldn't bear to see her cry and live with it for next 2 months.

We nearly reached our place when akki said, “Leo, Abhi n me will grab quick ice cream. Why don’t u n Amy just check the luggage till then. “ Abhi was about to protest he did not want ice cream , when the look in Akki eye shut him up. Akki was trying to give Amy n me some time. She never generally shuts up even for a moment . her silence was unnerving to all of us.

We walked up still holding hands. That’s the way she loves it.

I just had to close the door to the flat when Amy did what she was wanting to do since so long. She ran into my arms and hugged me tight. For a moment I was almost lost in the smell of her hair.

“I’ll miss you Leo, she said.

I wanted to say things like don’t worry we’ll call and talk every day. But I knew the truth , it just couldn't replace the feeling of holding her so close to me, of feeling her breath against mine ,  listening to her heartbeat as she hugged me.

I hugged her back “Gonna miss u too babe”

I did not want to let her go. I kissed her on head, then eyes , then cheek and lips. And she kissed back. This was killing me. It was going to be so hard to say good bye. Same thought trouble her, she let head head fall on my chest, her hair falling on her face.

“Take care of yourself for me, wont you.”she squeaked

I nodded. And pulled her closer. Little droplets of tears stuck on her eyelashes. “Don’t cry , Amy. I'll cancel the trip” I said earnestly. She smiled weakly. That’s my brave girl. “And promise me , u’ll take care of yourself.”

She hugged back. Words just felt so hollow here. All I knew was what little time i have left with me before going I want to hold her tight close to me.
