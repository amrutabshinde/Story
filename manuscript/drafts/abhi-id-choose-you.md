# Abhi - I'd choose you

*POV: Abhi | Timeline: year 3*

---

The air in my house was a bit tense. We were five of us Leo, Ash, Tanu Amy and me. The background being that Ash had this girlfriend, Rach. She did not approve a lot of Ash’s friendship with Amy. Two days back Rach and Ash had a fight over Amy.  She walked out . Ash got all angry. Amy went and apologized . Ash fumed even more.

‘you should not have gone there’ Ash said as he walked across the hall, he did not raise his voice but we could hear his anger. Leo and Tanu were both standing silently leaning on the railing in the balcony just watching the drama. Me well i was hungry , i was digging stuff out of the fridge and eating as i sat on the dining table. We all kept our safe distance from the battlefield.

‘But  Ash should not we tell her the truth’ Amy argued.

‘It is her problem if she does not trust me, ’ Ash was actually getting angry , never seen him talk in that tone.

“Ash, she is just getting possessive, c'mon “  she squeaked.

“you are supposed to be my friend, not hers, you had no reason to go there “

“This girl is imp to you, I could never be the reason for u losing someone u love.”

“If she does not understand our friendship it is her problem. ”

“God Ash you have to learn to prioritise...” she was looking at me? “What would you choose if u had to choose between Tanu and me? “ damn why is she dragging me into this. I stuffed my mouth with some more food. Amy continued impatiently “Of course he would choose Tanu!” 

This was unfair argument , Ash was in a shock, Even Leo had his mouth hanging somewhat open. Tanu was staring at me. She had realized I had avoided answering.

Ash finally spoke , “you hurt me more than she did , Amy. ” he sounded hurt too.  He sat down on the sofa. ‘ What have i done to deserve this.”

Amy had lost all her fierce manner now. She sat next to Ash on the sofa. “Ash , I...” he cut her midway .” I love her and she doesn’t trust me, I love you and you don’t  seem to care either Amy.  “ He put his head in his hands. Poor guy. He loved Rach, she was the only girl he ever got involved with for so long. He loved Amy too, she was his best friend.

“Ash, i cant see you sad, and if i m the reason, it would kill me. “  

Ash just sighed.  “I guess i should go” he picked up his jacket and rose.

“What are you going to do "

“I don’t know”

“Just talk to her, will you.”

“Hmm.”

He left mumbling goodbyes to us all. Amy looked all distressed. Leo sat on the sofa next to her . I  moved out to the balcony. I was thinking about Amy question. What would i choose? Tanu moved closer. I knew what she would ask.

“Tanu, I hope i never have to choose.”
