# Leo - The beginning (jan 2008)

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 1*

---

Abhi, one of my roomates for almost a year,  was busy packing his bags for his 4 month long work trip to London while Ash , my other roommate, and I lazed around. He probably might get things done quicker if we weren't sitting on his ironed clothes or eating the snacks he was meant to packing. 
I was reaching out for the next packet of kurkure when Tanu - Abhi's girlfriend , smacked me hard on my wrist. ' Leo, get out' she screamed at me. My phone buzzed, I picked it up massaging my hand, it was a message from Amy. I hadn't known her long. She was Abhi's friend from office. More like his pet cat to be honest.Abhi found her in the office and decided to take her under his wings. I think when Abhi and Tanu marry they would probably adopt her. She is at our house every weekend. The sofa is her permanent bed. We dont even bother to move the comforter from the sofa on weekdays when she isn't around. 

' call me when Abhi isn't around' she wrote. That was weird. I moved out on the terrace and called her. Maybe she was planning a farewell party for Abhi ,I thought, amused. 
' Leo, I need you to come and pick me up from the train station. I fell on the stairs'
' oh my god, are you alright'
' I am ok, but I can't walk all the way up there. Pick me up please. Just get me any painkillers you have. I think I hurt my back. Don't tell Abhi. You know how he gets. ' she said in one breath. 
So I drove to the station. I took some water and painkillers for her. I picked up a few chocolate bars from Abhi's open bag for her.
She sat on bench out side the station clenching her hand and breathing slowly. Even from a distance I could see she was in pain. I rushed towards her and sat down next to her.
'Amy, are you alright?' I asked as I handed over the painkillers and water.
' Maybe not, ' she winced as she took them. ' I hurt my back when I fell on the stairs. It is killing me.' she took a deep a breath in. Her eyes were watering with the effort of talking.
'Can you walk?'
'No'  
' ok, let's sit a while. We should go to a doctor.' the medicine would start working in sometime.
' I will go tomorrow I promise. Abhi can't know. I dont want to ruin his trip. You know how worked up he can get.' she said miserably. It was true. He starts worrying if Amy doesn't answer her mobile for a few hours. Or he will pace the room from the time Amy leaves formour house late until she is home.to be fair to the guy, she is very clumsy ,she frequently burns her finger or scrapes her knees. And she give lift to perfect stargers on her way home!
' ok, ok. Calm down. We will think of a way.'
' it is only a matter of few hours'
' 12 hours to be exact. He leaves tomorrow morning.' I frowned. I did not like this a bit. She clearly needed to be checked out. We sat there as a little color returned to her face. Her breathing got better. 
'Feeling better?' I handed her some chocolates.
She tried to move then stand and did not wince. ' I think I can walk now.'
'Sure?'
'yeah, ' she said gobbling up the chocolate next. 
I had parked close so we did not have to walk long. But she was sweating from that effort as well. How we were going to keep this from Abhi - I had no clue. 

'thank god you are here , Amy, I am sure these guys keep unpacking everything I packed. Help me.' said Tanu as soon as we walked into the house.
' I am offended.' I said. 'Sit back and let me show you ladies how it is done.' I said as I started helping Tanu put things in one of the bags. Amy sat down on the sofa and gave me a grateful smile.
Abhi was weighing the other bag. He sat down beside Amy.
' you don't look so good.' he said.' Are you alright.' 
' Stomach cramps.' she answered. 'I am fine.' Good thinking, I thought,  at least that meant no one was asking her to make tea or pack or anything that required her to get up from her corner. 

That night I suggested ordering dinner in so Amy wouldn't have to go into trouble of going to a restaurant.
After dinner Abhi , Ash and Tanu went to bed early since we had to wake up at 3 am for Abhi to catch his flight. Amy had set her bed up on the sofa as usual. But around midnight I could hear shuffling in the living room. When I went out she was trying to get up. She was supporting herself up with one hand on the sofa and other on the coffee table. The coffee table was sliding away slowly, she was going to crash. I almost screamed as I rushed towards her , reaching just in time to break her fall.
' oh my God, Amy!' I exclaimed,' what the hell are you trying to do'
'I was trying to get up' she replied as if that was such an obvious answer.
As I helped her into sitting position she said. ' thanks! the painkiller has worn off. my back is throbbing again.' 
'We should see the doctor, Amy' I was getting worried. 
 She gulped down the medicines without water. ' few more hours , few more hours' she said more to herself than to me. 
'What do you think will happen?' I asked ,'if Abhi finds out. He needs to get on the flight no matter what.'
'He will have a lousy flight, Leo, he worries too much. ' 
 I was a bit worried for her as well. She should have gone to the doctor.
'I dont think I have broken any bones.' she said sensing my disapproval. ' I will go to the doctor first thing tomorrow morning' 
' Hmm' I replied unhappily.
' I am fine. ' she said trying to sound upbeat and failing miserably at it. 
' want to watch some TV.' I said as I sat down next to her. That would at least distract her from the pain. 
' yeah, iron man?' she asked. 
So there we were eating popcorn and ice cream and watching TV. She knew the movie by heart. She kept saying the dialogues along with the movie. Half way through the movie she fell asleep. I left her a sticky note on her mobile ' call me before trying any other stunts yourself. - Leo'  Years later she still has that note, but that is a story for a different time.

In the early morning ,we sleepily dropped Abhi to the airport. And Tanu to her bus for home. On the way to the doctor I told Ash about Amy's adventure the day before and we took her to a doctor. Luckily Amy just had sprained her back and would be good in a week or two. We got her the medicines doctor prescribed and dropped her home.
On my way to the house, I was restless. How was she going to manage on her own. I kept picturing how she almost fell again yesterday night. I couldn't in good conscience not help her out. 
' Ash, we should get her home.' 
' who..' said Ash, who had fallen asleep with his cheek on the window. 
' Amy, we cant let her live alone when she is this hurt. I am turning around.' I said.
' ok, just don't wake me up unless it is urgent.' he said and went back to sleep.
I was wondering what to say as I knocked on her door. She took long to open the door. When she did she sweating with he effort of getting up. 
' hi, ' she said. ' did you forget something?' 
' Amy, pack your bags and stay in Abhi's room for a few  weeks. I am sure he wont mind. I can't let you live alone in this condition.' 
As surprised as she was she agreed . I think she was relieved. Even little tasks like sitting and standing were painful for her. Although she could sit on a bar stool and cook for us for the next 3 weeks. For 3 whole weeks we had the privilege of hosting Amy at our house. And I did not know it then but I had just invited over the greatest adventure of my life.
