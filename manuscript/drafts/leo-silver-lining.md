# Leo-  silver lining

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 2*

---

I was driving back home from office at 2 am on a Friday night. I felt completely exhausted and all I wanted to do was just hit the bed. It had been the same situation everyday for the past few weeks. we were working on a new project and even after 4 weeks it looked like we were no where near to a even a good start. And it had started taking a toll on my relationship with my girlfriend , my roommates and friends. I had hardly spoken to  anyone since I started working these insane hours. Except a few messages I had no clue as too what was going on in the lives of people I loved.  It was all gloom and doom and with no sign of ending. So when i entered my apartment I was surprised to find the kitchen light on and Amy  - my roommate's friend , sleeping with her head down on the kitchen table. I would have just let her sleep there but I did not think it was a very good posture for her back. I gently nudged her and she woke up with a start. 
'Amy, why are you sleeping here?' I asked.
She look a bit confused at first and rubbed her eyes. Suddenly she smiled , 'I was waiting for you, Leo!' 
She pushed a glass bowl toward me. 'I made mango kalakand!' I realized how hungry i was and sat on the chair next to her and started eating it. She started explaining how she found he recipe and tried it out and it came out so well without me asking for details. 
'Are you hungry? I made biryani as well.'
'biryani? why did you give me dessert first?' I said in a mock anger.
She heated up another bowl while I got the plates. We sat down to eat.
'I dont see you anymore' she said eating a pea in my biryani.
'Work has been hectic. I hardly even see Abhi and Ash to be honest. In fact i dont remember the last time i had a decent meal with Nat ' and i continued to tell her how i have been in office almost 14 hours a day, and it has been so exhausting. How I dnt know what was going on Abhi and Ash's and her lives. How I feared I was going to lose Nat this way. We have already  been arguing on phone calls. It felt good to finally say it out loud. Like some small amount of weight was reduced from my shoulders. She listened. Surprisingly without any interruptions. I expected her to say that I should get my priorities straightened etc. but she surprised me.
' hmm, why don't you just tell Nat.' she shrugged.
