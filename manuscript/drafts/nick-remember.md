# Nick - Remember

*POV: Nick | Timeline: present day*

---

Miles and I were driving back home from dinner. She had been trying to keep me busy every weekend so that I don't miss Amy much. It was hard especially on Fridays. I always spend Friday nights at Amy and Leo's house. Even if I came back at 4 am after a party I would still crash at their place. Ever since Amy's amnesia I had no where else to go. No where else I wanted to go. 

'Fuck "I screamed as I realized I wrongly took the familiar turn to Amy's house. 'Fuck fuck fuck' I repeated as I pulled up on the curb. I wanted to scream at the injustice of it. I wanted to cry in despair. I wanted to drive my car right into her house and shake her till she remembered me.  
I sat there trying to focus on my breathing. That is what Amy would have asked me to do when I am angry or frustrated. 
'Just breath in and breath out' she would say.
'Nick , are you ok?' Miles asked , concern written all over her face.
'I am sorry, I am just so used to taking this exit. ' I sighed as I started to get back on the road.
She pressed my shoulder slightly, 'I can understand.'
'I miss her so much!' I said touching the pendant Amy had given me. I used to be so important to her.    
She doesn't even know me anymore.

I was woken up next morning by Leo banging on my door.
"Ssup dude" I asked yawing.
"Nick I need you to look after Amy for me today. I have to go out" he said
"What!" , how could he leave me alone with Amy. Miles must have talked to Leo about yesterday. 
"Get ready. And wear your batman T shirt"
"Leo, please , we are not trying to remind Amy who I am. It is dangerous. She went into a panic attack when we tried.  Did Miles talk to you? It was nothing honestly."
"Shut up and get ready', Leo said pushing me toward my wardrobe. "I need you to be with her. Just get ready quickly. she must have woken up."
I put on the first t shirt I found. It wasn't batman. Leo spilled water on it so that I would have to go and change again!
"Leo, wtf man.. " I called out as I changed into the batman shirt.

We reached their place to find Amy up and reading some book.
"Amy, You remember Nick"
"yep, we went to his party last week." she said
"Right, I have to go out today, nick would stay here with you. If there's anything you need just call me , ok?"
"Ok.. " she said a bit confused. "I don't need a baby sitter" 
'You forgot how to turn the gas off.' Leo said in an accusing tone. 'Dont let her cook anything', he told me.
'It had nothing to do with amnesia.' she said. 'The knobs at Abhi's turn the other way round' 
'No cooking ' Leo repeated firmly.
'Fine' she said grudgingly.
Then she turned to me ,"Isn't it a give away , if u wear a T-shirt that declares your secret identity?" she asked "Batman" she whispered.
Leo closed the door behind him. He was smiling still at the Amy's batman comment about my T-shirt.
That was the same thing she had said to me when she had first seen me wear this.
I wanted to shake Amy hard and remind her who I was. How could she forget me. After all we had been through. Amy sat on the sofa, the Game of Thrones fleece on her legs.
I gave that fleece to you , women. How can u forget! The voice inside my brain was screaming in despair.
Outside there was complete silence. Awkward silence.

We were strangers now. She smiled at me and I smiled back.
' so, it is you and me , would you like some coffee? '  I asked.
' I will make us some', said my hostess walking slowly towards the kitchen.
' no cooking , remember?'
She made a face and sat on the table in the kitchen. She was silent as I made coffee. Did she remember this, we have been in kitchen together for ages. Trying new dishes out. Putting out fire at times. This is us , Amy! You got me back from the dead, how can you forget us. I did not know what I could say to her without trying to make her remember me. I just silently beat the coffee. 

'I know you , don't I?" she said all of a sudden.
My heart started beating faster. Do nothing to cause panic , I told myself. I took a deep breath and turned to face her.
'It doesn't matter. you remember all things you should , rest will come eventually. Just don't stress yourself." , I said. 
'But I knew you? Like why cant I remember a whole person I knew.' , 
'Amy, chill. lets not rush", I kept the cup down. I reached for my mobile in the pocket in case i have to call Leo.
 "Nick, I promise I wont panic again. I have accepted I cant remember things from past few years. It will take time to come to me. " she said calmly. "I just have a feeling that we knew each other, in fact Leo must have trapped you here for the same reason."  she smiled.
"Leo's an idiot.' I said feeling a bit relived. " We have know each other for long now" , We are best of friends. I could take a bullet for you and you would do the same for me too. I wanted to say. Instead I said, "But in a way, I got another chance to make a first impression on you again!" 
Her face lit up. "Yeah.. And the impression is turning out to be pretty good too" she said.

I pulled out the BB-8 and R2-D2 coffee mugs we had ordered when Star Wars Episode 7 promotions were going on. I gave her the BB8 and took the other one for myself. Her smile suddenly vanished. she kept the cup on the table. It looked like she was struggling to speak next lines.
'Star Wars Episode 8!!.. " she screamed." Did I watch it!" 
"Yes you did" I said softly. Come on Amy you do not mind not remembering your best friend and you freak out about freaking Star Wars! 
"What else did I forget" she said tension written all over her face. "Winds of Winter, Star Trek, Dr. Strange... "
"Amy, it is ok. Calm down"
"It is not ok Nick, It is the most awesome thing that can happen!!" she jumped with joy. "I can watch these things again like the very first time!!"
I was stunned. Literally. Only Amy could see something good come out of her amnesia.
"But that means.. " she continued looking a bit worried now.
"You have to watch these all before you start remembering them" I said catching on.
"What if I remember these things tomorrow" she looked horror struck by the idea.
"Ok , Amy , just relax, "I held her arm." I have all these DVD's at my place. Do you want to go there now? We can do  marathon of all the things you want to see."
"Lets go then!" she said gulping down her cup.
 "Would you be comfortable to come to my place alone?" I asked seriously.
"I never came to your place before?" she asked though she was already wearing her jacket.
"You did , all the time"  
"come on then! I don't know how much time I have!" she raced to the door.
Trust Amy to trust strangers! I rolled my eyes. Isn't that how we had met first too. because she trusted a stranger with a batman t-shirt who was a GoT fan. 

But as we walked I could sense she was having doubts. Could she trust this stranger. Was is really wise to follow me to my house alone."Amy, why don't you call Leo and tell him you are at my place. In case he comes home and finds u missing.'
She spoke to Leo and looked more relaxed 

'here' said Amy handing me the mobile,' Leo wants to talk to you' she looked happy so Leo probably assured her that she could trust this stranger.

' Nick, I be around if you need me' 

' hmpf'

' u alright?'

'yep. Absolutely' Why wouldn't I be, my best friend just concluded that I was not going to kill her. 

Meanwhile, Amy without waiting for me to get off the call was busy talking to me.

' .. maybe I should watch the star wars movie first or do you think it should be GoT.. are they worth it?' she said and then continued without waiting for my answer..' or justice league! How was it!? Don't tell me. No spoilers.' 

She was still talking when I looked for my keys and opened the door, when I walked in she followed.. God, I had almost forgotten how much rubbish she could talk! 

' So this is my house' I said as she stepped in

' the Wayne mansion ' she said laughing.

I smiled back. ' make your self comfortable,"

I knew she wanted to sit in the recliner in front of the TV. She always loves that spot. In fact it is her spot in my living room. But today she wasn't sure if she should  choose the most comfortable place in an acquaintance's house. 

I know , she was ill and that I should try and understand. It wasn't her fault that she could not remember me. Yeah that is what kept me from lashing out!

She choose to watch wonder woman, even though I knew she was dying to watch after the cliff hanger in the last season GoT. She wasn't sure she would be comfortable watch GoT with me.

All throughout the movie she was distant and polite. Thanking me when ever I got her something to eat . Smiling politely when our eyes met.

I don't know why Leo did this to me. It is hard enough losing my best friend, why did he want to live with the proof for the entire day. It was taking all my restrain to not start snapping back at her thank you's and please's .

'It really bothers you  doesn't it, that I don't remember you?' 

'What do you want me to say , Amy' I asked a bit sarcastically. She withdrew sensing danger.

' I saw u in the shuttle a few times. And in the gym and sometimes on my way to office' 

'I wanted to make sure you were ok, specially when Leo wasn't around.'

' oh?' she asked , her eyes suddenly showing some emotion.

' I always watch out for you, Amy!' I said sincerely. "I always do" I repeated, 'We are supposed to be very good friends, in fact 'very good friends' is an understatement.'

'Really!' she said her eyes brightening. 'Am I cheating on Leo with you? ",she asked playfully.

'No! Obviously not!' I said shocked and then realized she was joking as she laughed and popped the 10th chocolate in her mouth.

' Show her what she gave you on your birthday ' Leo said. She has given me a lot of things over the course of so many years but I knew which gift he meant. It was a peacock feather pendant on a chain. So the story goes that ever since she was a little girl and read Palace of Illusions she had prayed to God for a friend like Krishna was to Drupadi. Not for a true love or prince charming. But a friend- nothing more nothing less. And when it was my birthday she hunted down the perfect peacock feather necklace for me. Cause according to her God had granted her wish when she met me. 
I have been wearing it ever since her accident. It gave me hope that we are going to be alright. 
She looked at it and then looked at Leo. 
'This was a year after you met him. You just had to give him this on his birthday. We spent months finding the perfect one. what I told you in the morning is what you told me once - If Nick tells to jump off a cliff, you will without a second thought.' Leo said.
Amy started at me digesting it all. 

' How could I forget you! ' Amy said her eyes glistening as she took my hand. 
All my anger and frustration just melted at those words. "I am so sad, but I am so grateful" she said pressing my hand. And I knew she was going to find something positive in this situation. I felt a familiar sense of irritation, she just couldn't give up! Leo shrugged as he smiled. We knew we were going to get a dose of Amy's optimism now.
"This is a miracle, isn't it. Of the millions of people on this planet, I managed to find you , forget you and still have you by my side! We are going to get through this.'
"You have gotten through a lot worse" Leo added.
'See!!' she beamed and slapped my arm. ' We still have a chance... can we eat now, we still have a lot of movies to finish.'
 Classic Amy. Hopelessly optimistic. How she drove me mad. How I loved her! Leo was smiling at me. He did not do all this so Amy could remember me, he did all this so I could remember Amy. Memories or not, she was still the same. and that is all that mattered.
