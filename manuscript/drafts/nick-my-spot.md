# Nick - my spot!

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 4*

---

I felt like a little kid right now.excited that it was my bday. Amy , leo urmilla n me were going to Amy's house for my bdy after party. We had just left the party that my dad threw for me. It was good , but it wasn't fun. These were the three people I wanted to spend m

' now for your birthday gift' amy said. The cake still smeared on her face. ' yeyeye' I said imitating her.  Leo and amy got up and walked towards their 2nd bedroom. Amy motioned me n miles to follow. Leo stood at the door and stopped. ' amy u sure u want to give his to him' he asked . my patience was now eating off. ' just get out of my way n give me my gift.' I pushed leo awy from the door and rushed into the room after opening it. 

The very first thing I noticed when I walked in was the big photo frame on the wall. It was a black n white photo of leo, amy miles n me with Mont. Given the garden in the background it had to be miles' house. None of us were looking at the lens. All of us were laughing.  I can't remember when this would have been clicked. But that photo kind of summarizes my life with these guys , sheer cheer. 

'Hows it' amy askedas she stood next to me. I smiled back in answer. Words seemed lame to express what I thought of it. 

' thanks' I said. 

'See , he did not even notice' leo yelled at amy. ' I slogged so hard to set this room up and all he sees is this photo!'

I did not know what he was talking about. I looked around the room . it looked cleaner than usual. There was a bed set up. The covers were new. And there were curtains. And the cupboard was clean and empty. Bookcase was new. The books on it were the ones amy borrowed from me. What was going on, I wondered.  

'Leo, cmon' amy started.

' ssup guys , what did I miss?' I asked.

' this is you bday gift.' Amy said. She handed me a key and said, ' this is your permanent room in my house , nick' 

Oh that's when it struck me. The decor was my favorite color - blue. The book case was for my books. The cupboards had the clothes I left at their place and forgot to carry back home. This was my room!
