# Leo - Forget me not (jan 2014)

*POV: Leo | Timeline: present day*

---

I was sitting on the chair in the hospital lobby outside the operating room. Praying. My wife,  Amy, had been rushed here after a car accident. She was out of danger but the doctors were still operating on her. My hands and feet trembling. Fear gripped me— I had come so close to losing her again.

Nick, Amy’s best friend, burst through the doors, worry written all over his face. Over the years, Nick and I had stood together through too many crises like this—Amy in the hospital, missing, or fighting for her life.

'She is out of danger now,' I said before he could ask. Some of the tension eased from his shoulders.

'How bad?'

'She has internal bleeding. They are fixing it,' I said as Nick hugged me. I cried. He cried.

'I swear to God, Leo,' he said, wiping his face with the back of his hand, 'someday I am going to purposefully end up in the hospital just so that she knows how this feels!' Nick managed a weak smile. But it wasn't reaching his eyes.

We waited for any updates from the doctors or nurses. Nick was pacing the floor while I sat on the chair.

Then Abhi arrived, breathing hard, his shirt damp with sweat. He had been my flatmate when we first moved to Mumbai, and the one who introduced me to Amy. He had always been fiercely protective of her.

'How is she?' he said, panting, and put a hand on my shoulder.

'She is out of danger but they are still operating for internal bleeding,' I said.

He sat on a chair in his familiar worried pose — his head in his hands.

'She is going to be fine,'  Nick said. Abhi nodded.

We waited.

---

The nurse called me into Amy's room when Amy was waking up. I walked in with Nick and Abhi behind me.

She was propped up against some pillows. Bruises all over her face and arm. But she was alive and awake.

'You have been in an accident. You are safe now. Your family is here,' the doctor was telling her.

'Hi,' she said softly as I walked in. Nick followed, and then Abhi.

Something was off. The way she looked at us. The way she looked at me.

'Oh, Abhi, I am so sorry. You had to miss your flight for me!'

*What flight?* I thought. Abhi looked confused as well. Nick ran out to get the doctor.

Soon the doctor was asking her a lot of simple questions. My heart was pounding hard against my chest as I stood at the foot of the bed and watched.

'Amy, do you know who these people are?'

'Abhi,' she looked at Abhi.

Then she looked at me. 'Leo. Abhi's roommate.' I felt a stone drop in my stomach. *Abhi's roommate.* She just glanced over at Nick and said nothing. Nick had his hand on my shoulder and pressed it hard, like he was trying to steady himself.

'Do you remember your accident?'

'I was walking on the overhead bridge and tripped. I fell on the stairs. That is the last thing I remember.'

*That was six years back. That happened six years back.*

The doctor was explaining to Amy that it was normal to get a bit disoriented after an accident. She was holding Abhi's hand tight. She kept glancing at Abhi and at the doctor as the doctor spoke to me. It was unnerving to have her look at me as a stranger. My Amy.

The doctor was talking about temporary amnesia and disorientation. She had lost her memories of  the last six years. It was all too much to take in. I kept looking at Amy.

'Will she be alright?' I asked.

'She will be. It could take a few weeks for everything to come back. Give her some time — she has been through a lot.'

Later in the room , she was opening the box of accessories and jewelry they had  given her back. Her glasses, her watch, her chain —she picked them one by one  and then she picked up her ring. She slid it onto her finger, then looked at Abhi's hand for a ring, then Nick's, then mine — trying to guess who she was married to.

'Abhi,' she said softly. 'Who?' She pointed to the ring on her hand.

Abhi looked at me uncertainly as I moved towards her.

'Leo,' he said.

'Leo!' she exclaimed, and she looked at me, trying to find something — anything — familiar.

'You have been married to Leo for a year now.'

'I am?' she said, breathing faster. Nick gestured toward her heart rate monitor. It had started registering red.

'Amy, it is alright,' Nick said, moving closer with his hands in the air.

'I don't even know who you are!' she cried at Nick.  'I don't remember marrying. Why can't I remember?' She looked at Abhi, panic rising in her voice.

The monitors were beeping loudly now. She was having a full panic attack. Soon the room filled with nurses and doctors. They sedated her and she fell asleep. She wouldn't let go of  Abhi's hand. Right now he was the only person she trusted. Nick and I were strangers to her. Both of us sat outside the room while Abhi sat inside with her.

---

'She doesn't remember me, Nick. My wife...' I sighed as I put my head in my hands. I just wanted to wake up and find out this was a nightmare. 'For her I am just Abhi's roommate.'

'For now, Leo,' Nick said. 'She is going to remember soon.'

'I don't know how I can survive till she does. Just a few minutes of this and I feel so broken. I don't know who I am without her.' I wanted to cry out loud. She was my whole world — my lifeline.

'Give her some time. She will remember.'

I pushed back a sob. I was scared. I was lost.

'What if she doesn't?' I spoke out my greatest fear.

'Then we will remind her. Every little detail,' he said firmly. 'We will get her back.'

I glanced at the window to her room. She was still asleep as Abhi sat next to her, keeping watch.

*Come back, Amy.* I thought. Please come back to me.
