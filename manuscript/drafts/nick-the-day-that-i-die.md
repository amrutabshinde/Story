# Nick - The day that I die.

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 5*

---

Ami , Mile and Ashu were glued to the TV since morning Any moment now the news would be out of my airplane crash announcing that I had died in the unfortunate event.

We were getting feeds of the video camera set up in my house. 'Let see , nick, who will cry when u die.' said Ashu rubbing his hands as sat in front of the TV. Frankly I found it disgusting, I was going to hurt the people I loved the most. True mom, dad, and Miles knew i wasnt dead. But Amy did not. The hardest decision I have ever made in my life was to keep the plan a secret form Amy. Some part of me wondered how she would react to my death. 'Tch , tch , nikhil, ' my conscious said ' how could you even think of testing Amy's friendship'

The news channel finally started showing the news of my crash. An hour or so later the camera in my parents house showed Sahil rushing in asking for my parents- he wasn't going to believe I died based on some news. My parent were supposed to be out of the country. He kept asking each of the staff at home as if hoping at least someone would deny that I was on the plane. He called my father to confirm. My father would have confirmed cause he fell into the chair next to him looking dazed.

A few minutes later Leo came running into the TV frame. 'Sahil', he asked ' The news , Nick's plane... , is it true.'

Sahil nodded.

' Is Nick ok?, I am trying to reach him.. I cant.. where is he .. is he fine?' asked Leo, shaking Sahil by his arms.

'I called his dad, Nick was in the plane that crashed.'

'No.. sahil.. it cant be true!' screamed Leo his eyes starting to well up.

Sahil just stared back at him. Sahil couldn't believe it too.

Leo fell down on his knees. crying.. no wailing. I was touched. I never knew my death would affect him so.

'Leo!' I heard Amy's voice. She rushed towards Leo.

'Sahil, where is Nick!' she demanded. 'Leo, what has happened'

Leo was still crying on the floor.

Sahil stood up and held Amy by her arms.

'Amy, I spoke to Nick'd dad- Nick was on the plane that crashed.'

'No no no..' Amy said tears forming in her eyes.

'Amy , Nick is dead.. he is gone , Amy ' Sahil hugged her and wept.

'No no no; Amy kept chanting.

'No' she screamed and pushed Sahil back.'He cant be dead'

'Nick is gone ..' Sahil said amongst tears.

Amy still had the look of Hope in her eyes. The look I always teased her for . The undying optimism, even if it were stupid. How many time I had teased her for it. How many time I had tried to even to break her insane belief in things. ' Well, ' said the voice in my head,' looks like you might succeed this time' 

Amy looked at Sahil then at Leo still crying on the floor.

' He can't die.. ' she said softy. ' he can't she pleaded. Was she giving up. The light in her eyes was gone. She closed her eyes and rocked a bit and collapsed on the floor! ' Amy!' called Sahil.

' Amy! ' I yelled at the screen. Leo just looked at the fainted Amy on the floor and cried even louder. 

That evening when Leo spoke to Miles on the phone, Miles inquired how Amy was and put the call on speaker.

' she is still in shock.. ' said Leo hoarsely, ' it is hard to believe he died' 

' miles' Amy was heard on the phone. I moved closer. ' Miles , listen to me.. Nick is not dead..'

Ashu, Ami looked at each other worried. Had we missed something? 

' they haven't found him as yet. There is no proof he died, Do you understand! We have to find him, Miles' she said.' he might be out there somewhere in need of our help. I know he is alive ,Miles, he can't be dead.  ' 

Well then after all, her optimism still wins. This was one battle I was happy to lose.

' If you were to keep you life in something secure, where would you keep it?' someone had asked me.' why , I would keep it in Amy's hope. It is the most indestructible thing I know'
