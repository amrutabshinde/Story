# leo- finally again

*POV: Leo | Timeline: present day (year 3)*

---

She was wearing these multiple chains and necklaces around her neck. They had gotten tangled with each other and her hair. And she was struggling to take them off. 

' stop pulling at them, you'll break the chains and probably have a bald patch at the back of you head' I said as I took my coat off. 
' arrgh ' she said. 'I am going to have to live in these forever now' she was still trying to pull all stand apart.
' let me help' I said and walked up to her from behind. 
As I worked on each chain at a time my heart started beating faster. This was our moment. Every time my finger touched her neck my stomach fluttered. How I wanted to kiss her. How i wanted her to love me again. But did not remember us as yet. 

 She had become unnaturally still as well. I could see her shoulder rise and fall as she breathed. 

I was aware of everything. The three inches between my mouth and her shoulder. The particular way her hair smelled, the same way it had always smelled — flowers, something warm underneath. The fact that she was breathing carefully, measured, like she was counting it.

*Don't*, I told myself. *Don't make this something it isn't. She doesn't remember. She doesn't know what she doesn't remember. Don't you dare.*

' What took you so long , Leo?' she asked quietly.
At first I though she was talking about the necklace I was working on. But they way she said it suggested she meant something else .My hands stopped.
' Amy?' I asked , too scared to get my hopes up. She turned around slowly and looked at me with pain in her eyes.
'I waited so long for you! You kept hurting me with all your new girlfriends. I waited and waited.'  . 
' I am sorry , Amy! I was a fool!' she remembered us! 
Why did you take so long to love me back!' she said
 I pulled her in before I could think of the right words, because there weren't any. My arms went around her and she let me, and I held on with everything I had, and I pressed my mouth into her hair and I thought: *she remembers. She remembers us. She's in there and she's coming back.*
