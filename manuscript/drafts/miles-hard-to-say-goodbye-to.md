# Miles- hard to say goodbye to

*POV: Miles | Timeline: year 5*

---

Ami and I were sitting around the swimming pool in ashu's villa. Nick was swimming at the far end of the pool, almost like he was avoiding us. He had become very moody in the last week. He did not like being holed up here. He hated not being able to contact folks back home. He hated that most people considered him to be dead now. We expected that now that nick is dead for the world , whosoever wanted him killed would try to retrieve the ammunition plans he had been working on. We had surveillance cameras fitted around his house to catch them red handed. Ashu had his police force station out side nikhil's house. Ashu was sitting on the sofa next to the pool, lazily watching the feeds from nikhils house.  

' nick' he called out, ' you have a visitor' 

Ami and I ran to see who it was . nick swum towards the laptop.

' who is it ? ' Ami asked with excitement as we sat next to ashu.

' Amy .. ' nick whispered when he saw the silhouette on the screen. 

' what is she doing there' Ami said disappointed. 

' who is she?' Asked ashu. I knew that tone . he thought she was a suspect.

' she is Nikhil's friend. We know her for 3 years now , ashu, she's clean.'  I said firmly. I did not want Nikhil fighting with ashu .

' she has key to your apartment' ashu exclaimed as we saw Amy open the front door and walk into the empty house.

Nikhil was ignoring us all. He stared intently at the screen. Amy mean while switched on the light in Nikhil's house. She fumbled through the big bag she was carrying and got out a painting.

' she got a painting ' I said out loud. Oh Amy.. She always gave the paintings she painted to Nikhil. By the looks of it she was here to keep  at his place. One of the hardest things of our plan was to keep it from Amy and Leo. Amy was devastated when she was told Nikhil's crashed into the sea. For the first few hours she kept fainting , her blood pressure going low. Leo had to struggle with the tragic lose of losing nick and Amy's health. 

I had called Leo a few days later, Nikhil wanted to know how they were. I asked him to video chat with me so that Nikhil could see Amy. Leo looked tired. His eyes were red from lack of sleep. 

'How are you doing, Leo, are you OK?' I had asked.

he said ' I can't afford to grieve , miles. I have to stand strong for Amy. How are you?' He asked. His eyes filled with concern.

I did not know what to say. I couldn't imagine a life without nick. I had told Leo I am going to look for whosoever killed nick. 

' I can't grieve either , Leo' I said. 

' I understand' he replied. 

' where is Amy? ' I asked Leo. 

' she is at abhi's place, we are living there since a couple of days. In fact I just came home to collect stuff....we will probably shift in some other place in a few days.' He said.

I just raised my eyebrows to question what he meant.

' there is just too much of him in this house ' he replied. His eyes were welling up with tears. ' we can't .. Just can't stay here... ' he wiped his tears. Nikhil had sulked the whole day following that call. 

Meanwhile in Nick's house,Amy placed  the painting on the table in the living room. She arranged the flowers in the flower vase. Arranged the pillows on the sofa. Almost like she expected Nikhil; to come in any moment and wanted the house tidy for him.  Nikhil had left his fur coat on the teapoy. She picked it up. And sat at the foot of the sofa hugging the coat. It was heartbreaking to see her. Nikhil looked sad too. It was hard to tell who looked more forlorn, Amy or nick. She sat there for quiet sometime. Nikhil did not take his eyes off the screen.

There was another clicking noise and the door opened again. It was Leo.  ' Amy, what are you doing here!' He asked. 

' I told you, I just came here to keep nikhil's painting ' she said quietly. 

' Amy, he won't be needing it anymore now' Leo said sadly. He sat next to Amy. ' try to understand' he pleaded.

' he is not dead, Leo. He will come back. They did not find him.. There is no proof he died in the crash.'  It looked like they had had this argument before. 

' Amy, listen to me.' He turned Amy to face him. ' his plane blew off when he was flying in the middle of the ocean. Even if he survived the blast, there is just no way he could have survived in the water.'he bowed his head ' I have lost him already, Amy.. Miles won't come back either. I can't afford to lose you to false hope , Amy' his voice cracked. ' we just have to let him go now' 

Amy looked like she wanted to argue, but she did not know what to say. Accepting Leo was right meant she had lost nick forever. She just hugged the coat and cried, Leo holding her. Nikhil looked close to tears himself. 

' Amy , sweetheart, Let's go home, I'll wait for you in the parking' Leo said and walked out of the door. Amy gave nikhil's coat one last hug and placed it on the table. She sat on the chair with her head in her hands. There was a book on the table. Nikhil must have left it there.She picked it up and stared at it for long. Nikhil moved closer to screen now, muttering something under his breath. Was he smiling? he certainly looked like he was expecting something to happen.

Amy walked to the kitchen. We could hear her open and close some drawers. She came out with a red rose in her hand. She placed the rose in front of one of Nikhil's photos. She ran in Nikhil's library and came out with a sticky note and a book. She scribbled something on the note holding the book for support . she kept the book next to the rose with the note stuck on it. She left quietly closing the door behind her. 

I looked at Nikhil and was surprised to see him smiling.' Oh she is clever' he said

' Nikhil , what is it' I asked.

' she knows I am not dead now.' 

' a week before I left I made a fuss over the packets of foodstuff expiring when I'd be gone. So Amy had them all sorted. And kept them in drawers according to expiry.  I emptied the drawer with 6 month expiry date' 

' how did she know how to look in the drawers' 

' the cornflakes from my table were gone. Instead I placed 1984 there to tell her that she could be watched.'

' 1984- big brother is watching' 

' exactly , and the I put a rose in the empty drawers so she knows it a top secret.'

' sub rose!' 

' correct, it took me weeks to come up with all the clues  and plant them into her head, but it took her 5 min to tell me she knows' 

' how, the only thing she left was a note saying missing you nick'

' the book she place it on is ' return of Sherlock Holmes'.

' I forgive you ashu for suspecting her, she is frighteningly clever'
