# Nick - Worrying starts

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 3*

---

I was working late that day. I was about to exit the building when I saw her hurrying up the stairs. It was almost 2 a.m. I wondered what she was doing in the office so late. We had met just a few days back so I did not know her well. But I knew she almost always took the bus back home at 6 p.m.
' Amy ' I called out.
She looked at me and stopped as I caught up to her on the stairs. She looked tired. Her hair was untidy. Her clothes were soiled. Wait was that blood!
' Amy, is that blood!?' I asked horrorstruck. ' Are you alright?!'
' It's not mine.  The security guard.. he collapsed when I was in the elevator with him... He vomited blood..' 
She looked shaken. I held her shoulders and made her sit down on the steps.
' calm down' I said. 'Tell me what happened.' 
' I .. i was going down in the elavator. One moment I was talking to him and the next moment he is lying in a pool of blood.' she shuddered.' I called the ambulance, but he kept spitting out more blood till they came.'
'how is he now?'
' stable, and his family arrived so I left from the hospital. ' she rubbed her eyes , her stomach rumbled and she stood up.' I feel a bit dizzy..  I need to get something to eat.' she said. 
We walked up the stairs together. She stood in front of the vending machine clicking almost every button and came back with a lots of chocolates and chips in her hand. 
After she had her first energy bar , I asked her if she wanted to change her shirt.
'I have a few in my office from some events.'
' That will be really helpful' 
There were still some people working on the floor as I walked towards my office. She clamed down considerably after eating chocolate. 
' why are you here this late, working?' she asked biting off a big chunk of chocolate bar.
'Yeah. Why did you come back?' 
' Too late to take a cab home , I'll just spend the night here  and leave in the morning.'
'That is your plan?' I asked incredulously. 
'Yeah, It is dangerous to go back by train or cab at this time. I could be mugged, just waiting for the cab.'  she sounded offended.
' I can drive you home.' I offered.
'It is ok, I am anyways planning to binge watch Witcher season 2', she said her eyes shining with excitement.
' Is it out today? oh yes!  I have been waiting for it for a year now!'
'I know, right!?' she echoed.
' I can keep you company.' I said , she had been helping others out. I felt like I should extend some support. 
'Dont worry,  honestly . I will just wait for a few hours and take cab home in the morning.'
It is this next moment that became the defining moment of my life to come. If I had chosen anything  else my life and Amy's too would have turned out to be completely different. I could have insisted I drop her. I could have just said good bye and left since she was safe in the office . But this is what I said,
' I have a projector in my office, we can watch it there!' 
'Sure?'
'Yeah, absolutely!'
'Awesome ' she said.. 
And that is how we spent that night- watching the series , eating whatever chips and trails mixes we found in the vending machines. After about a dozen coffees and 4 hours we were at the penultimate episode of the season. And I was having fun. It was good to watch with someone who watched the series with so much devotion. And she had read the books! Finally someone who is true fan. 
'Look at the time' Amy said looking at her watch.  'It is almost 6.30. Do you want to finish the season?' 
'Yeah , of course' I said. 
'We should get breakfast after this, I am starving' she said' 
I looked at the pile of wrappers in front of her.
'Stop judging me!' she said in a mock anger. 

' Thank you for staying with me today!' Amy said as we walked towards a café across the street.
' I have to admit I did it only to look chivalrous, but I ended up enjoying' I smiled back.
'We should do this again. Lets try to get like an even bigger screen and watch the new Star Trek movie!' she said excited.
'You mean like watch the movie in a theatre?' I asked amused.
'Yes, yes.. that is what I meant to say'  she said trying hard not to laugh at her own mistake. She blushed a shade of red as I laughed.

'I always wanted to try this place. Breakfast on me.' she said.
'Come on, let's split it.' 
'I owe you that much at least' she smiled as we waited for our turn.  After giving the order I turned around and walked towards a table near the window. I sat down on a chair when I realized Amy hadn't followed me . She was talking to the barista and laughing. I saw her put a generous tip in the tip jar.

' The lady at the counter is raising money for her college l.' she said as she sat down. That explained the tip.
After we had our coffees and pancakes and eggs I took a piece of paper napkin and wrote my number on it. I folded it and handed it to her 'Drop me a line when you get home safe.'
'I'll be fine!' she said rolling her eyes.
'I just want to make sure you reach home safely without emptying out you bank account for the taxi driver's son's wedding or something.'  
'I am not that stupid, you know' she said  
'You are not stupid , but you are naïve. You helped an unknown man and I bet you would have given him money if he had asked for it. You spent the night with me who you barely know."
'I know you..' she protested. 
 'We had met for a whole of 5 min before this! That's now knowing.' I exclaimed. 'How did you survive this long without getting into trouble!'
'How does one live your whole life on high alert.'
'Better safe than sorry'
'There aren't that many bad people in the world. And the probability that we will end up meeting some of the rare bad ones is so low. ' she answered. 
I couldn't help smiling. I disagreed, but I did not have the heart to burst her happy bubble. 
'Just let me know when you reach, ok ' 
'Ok, dad' she said as she got into her cab and greeted the driver. She waived me goodbye. As I watched her car leave, I felt a bit .. what was it - Sad? Worried? . 
As I drove home I got a text from her - ' Home safe. Paid the cab driver the fare only.;) - Amy'

Years later I still have that screen shot of the message saved. I did not know then that I was meeting my friend for life. A friend who I could willing die for. A friend to whom some years later I would admit, ' I always worry about you, Amy!'
