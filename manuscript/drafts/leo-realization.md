# Leo- realization

*POV: Leo | Timeline: year 2*

---

' so we are picking Amy up on our way?' I asked Abhi as I loaded my bags in the car. We were all going for a long awaited road trip. Generally Amy comes over at night so it is easier to leave early morning without having to drive over to her place. But she did not come last night. 
'dude, she isn't coming' Abhi said as Ash looked at me like I had lost my mind.
'What! Why?' I said not trying to sound as sad as I was feeling. 
'She had to travel to Bengaluru for work. She has been talking non stop about it for the past month now! ' said Ash ' how could you not know! I think I even know the hotel they are putting her in.'
I thought it was some other week. I felt like a heavy weight was dropped in my stomach. Why would Abhi and Ash plan this trip if she couldn't make it! What was the point of going if we all couldn't go together. Given so many ups and downs of the past few months this was one trip I was counting on cheer everyone up.  
  This day was doomed. I was feeling so resentful towards these guys I took the first turn to drive just so I could avoid talking to them. Why did no one tell me. I could have convinced everyone to work some other dates out. She needed this break more than anyone. She had become quieter after her break up with Vic. I had imagined some how this trip would get us some part of our old Amy back.
Finally Ash asked, 'Are you ok, Leo. '
I wasn't okay. And it was confusing. If Abhi wasn't coming would I feel this low. I would miss him . But I was more than missing Amy. I was miserable. 

'yeah, why, what happened ' I said breaking out of my thoughts.
'I asked you if you wanted to take a break.' Abhi said. ' almost 5 times now!' 
' are you high' teased Ash. 
Abhi and Ash now sat in the front letting me brood in the back.
The road was beautiful, there were waterfalls everywhere due to the rains. Amy would have loved it I thought angrily. Stupid beautiful weather. It was just perfect. why did it have to been so nice! Why did she have to miss it! 
I sent her a few photos out of spite to show her what she missed. 
'It is so beautiful! I should have been there!' she messaged back.
' you should have! Why did you not come!?'
' I had to come to Bangalore for a project. There were no other weekends available for this trip.:(. But lets go for a drive next weekend to see the waterfalls? Send me lots of pictures for now. Have fun!'
She was nauseatingly optimistic. But it rubs off on you too. In my mind I was already planning a drive back here next weekend.

She was away the whole of next week. Next Saturday morning she arrived in Mumbai and took a cab straight to our house. She got us donuts from some place she had loved there.
She sat at the table and gestured for me to sit there as well. Abhi got us all coffees and sat next to her. 
'Ash, come here and sit with us' she said to Ash who was sitting on the sofa. ' There is something I need to tell you guys' 
Ash promptly got up as Abhi and I exchanged confused looks.
'So' she began as all of were huddled around coffee and donuts. ' I got offered a new position in Bengaluru.' 
'What?' Abhi asked as he stopped chewing his food. 
'Are you taking it.? ' Ash asked as he too stopped sipping his coffee. 
' I asked for it' she said as all 3 of us stared at her. 
This was a joke , I thought. Why would she ask for a job in Bangalore. Why would she want to leave Mumbai. Leave us.
' hear me out' she continued.' I a

She was overreacting, this was no reason to uproot her life and move to an unknown place.
'Amy, stop being impulsive.' i said angrily slightly banging my hand on the table. 
She put her hand on mine and looked into my eyes ' Leo, please. ' she pleaded, ' I have thought about this a lot. I have to try make things better. I need this.'
I grazed her hand with my thumb. Feeling her warm skin on my cold hands. She was leaving us, leaving me. I could almost hear my heart break- shatter like glass into a million pieces. I couldn't let her go. There was no home without her. There was no life without her. I needed her. I loved her. The realization hit me like a heavy punch in the stomach. I pulled my hand from under hers with a jerk. 
' I need some air.' I said as I walked out.
' Leo, come on, don't be like that.' she called out behind me but I continued walking till I reached a bench in the garden. 
My throat was dry. How could I have not known this before, I thought. Ash came up and sat next to me.
' stop being so dramatic ' he said.
' Ash, I love her. I cant let her go' 
' I know. ' he said. 
'you dont get it, I love her like I want to spend the rest of my life with her. Like I dont think I want to live without her' 
'I know.' Ash repeated smugly.
I just started at him in disbelief, either was really being stupid and not getting a word of what I was saying or he was teasing me.
'I think you have always loved her , Leo.' he said
How could he know it. I did not know it until now.
'Ever since she came to our house to stay and left.' he continued. 'I think you have liked her even when you were dating Nat. You just never accepted it , even to yourself.' 
I wanted to strangle him. If he was so smart to have realized what I had failed to , why did he just not tell me. 
'I have to stop her.' I said.
'You have to let her go, Leo', Ash said. 'She needs time.'
'But that means she will leave.' 
'That just means we will have to get her back. She is still not over Vic as yet, Leo.  I dont know if she still has a crush on you.' 
' what?'
' She had a crush on you ever since you brought her home when she hurt her back. '
I wanted to strangle Ash now. What was even the point of him.
' Why did you not say anything!' i lashed out angrily.
' So now this is my fault!?.You started dating Nat. And she Vic. What was I supposed to do! ' ash said angrily. 
I ran my hand through my hair. I wanted to pull them out! 
' I am going back home. ' Ash said getting up and started walking back. ' you pull your act together and try acting supportive' 
I walked around a bit kicking stones and getting my toes stubbed.
