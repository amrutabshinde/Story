# Leo - end of an era.

*POV: Leo | Timeline: Unknown*

---

Abhi has just informed us this morning that he was flying to London, tomorrow -a week before that he had initially planned. He was moving there for good. Amy was supposed to fly later this week to bid him farewell.  His postponing the plan meant Amy would not get to meet him. She has been trying to get flight tickets to come to Mumbai from Bangalore. But it was a very short notice. 
' But Amy is coming this weekend to meet you.' I couldn't believe he had forgotten that.
' yeah but I need to be there sooner for a cousin's wedding. I wish I could wait till she was here.' he gave an empty excuse.    
I stared at him in disbelief.
' when did some cousins wedding become more important to you than Amy' I screamed.
He just stared back at me. I knew he was avoiding  saying goodbye to Amy. It was the hardest for him. 
   Ever since I have known Abhi he has been with Tanu. I don't think he even remembers a time before her. To say he loved her would be undermining what Tanu meant to him. He adores her. He worshipped her. She was in every fiber of his body. Even though they were in different cities for their jobs, she was in every breath he took. And his perfectly rosy love life was hit by the tornado called Amy- Unpredictable, unruly and wild - she was completely  opposite to the clam and peaceful Tanu.  Abhi instantly gravitated towards her. He felt protective towards Amy. He got her into our group. And then I,  his best friend , started dating Amy and he realized he did not want to share Amy with anyone.  Abhi weighed  the stakes and decided to cut his losses and leave. I understand he had to weed Amy out to protect his life but why did he have to be so cruel.
' you choose to leave Abhi.  You choose to move away from her. Why do you have to punish her! ' I said angrily .' she couldn't get any tickets to come here. She has been sobbing non stop since this morning. She was thinking about driving here from Bangalore. It is a 19 hour drive!. She won't let you leave without saying goodbye.' 
' Tell her not to make a big deal out of this..' he said.
I could have punched him. But Nick was calling me. He was Amy's friend in Bangalore.
' what the f , Leo!' he shouted. ' why couldn't Abhi just tell us his plans before! And if he did not care about Amy making it there why the hell is Amy ready to risk driving all the way there!'
' you can't let her do it' 
' of course I won't  , but she is desperate, I am trying to see if we can get a bus.'
' you are coming too?' I asked and felt a wave of gratitude towards him. Bless him.
' the state she is in , I can't let her come alone. You'd think this Abhi guy is dying the way she is crying'
Next day Nick and Amy just barely made it to the airport in time. Abhi has checked in his bags. He had hugged Ash nd me. Last I heard from Amy they had taken a cab to the airport from the bus stop and were on their way. Abhi kept glancing around to see if she was her and then at his watch. Every time he looked at his watch I want to beg him to please just please wait till she comes. Abhi has been Amy's rock. She trusted and depended on him more than she did with me. She did not understand why Abhi was suddenly behaving so aloof. But that never changed how strongly she believed in him. 

She ran to Abhi and hugged him and cried. Abhi has one hand on his cabin bag. He just patted her back with the other hand. He should have hugged her tight and cried. He was going to regret saying a proper goodbye. 
Amy looked at him confused and hurt. She touched his cheek and wished him luck as she let him go.  He walked towards security as Amy watched him go and she hugged me and cried.
