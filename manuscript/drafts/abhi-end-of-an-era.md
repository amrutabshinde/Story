# Abhi- end of an era

*POV: Abhi | Timeline: year 4*

---

' Abhi, are you sure you want to come here early' Tanu asked over the phone.
' yeah, will be good to attend the wedding and get to know people.' I lied to my fiancé.
'Hmm' she said not believing me. ' see you then' she said as she cut the call. Tanu had been in London for the past month. We were moving there. I was to join her next week. Except I decided on flying out there tomorrow. Amy was going to come over the weekend to bid me farewell. I wasn't sure I could take the drama. She already had been tearing up every time we met since I informed her that I was moving. And this morning when I told her I had to go early' she had gone proper ballistics on me. She was trying to fly in to Mumbai from Bangalore to see me. I wasn't sure if I wanted her to come or not. 
Leo walked in my room .' But Amy is coming this weekend to meet you. Why are you leaving tomorrow?' he asked
' I need to be there sooner for a cousin's wedding. I wish I could wait till she was here.' I said.
He stared at me, shocked. 
' When did some cousins wedding become more important to you than Amy!' he asked.
I had no answer. Or maybe I did have the answers but he wouldn't like them. He wouldn't like it if I told I couldn't say good bye to Amy , that she was part of the reason I did not want to stay here any longer. That I was running away because I couldn't be happy for him and Amy like I wanted to. And Tanu - I had been toxic for past few months, every since Leo and Amy got together. She deserved better. 
    I have been in love with Tanu ever since I remember. Ever since I understood love. She was in every breath I took. Even though we ended up in different cities after school she was with me all the time. I would desperately wait for weekends when I would get to see her , hold her. She was my home , my happy place. And then Amy came into my life. Unpredictable, unruly and wild - she was completely  opposite to the clam and peaceful Tanu.  I would worry what new trouble Amy had gotten herself into every since the first day I met her. I wanted her to be around so I knew she was safe. And not until Amy started dating my best friend- Leo , did I realize how deep in trouble I was. I hated sharing Amy's  attention. I should have been happy for my friends instead I was starting to feel bitter. And it had started affecting my relationship with Tanu too. I had become a ticking time bomb. If I lost control for one moment I could take down 3 lives - of people I love the most. And I couldn't have that. I had to get away from Leo and Amy. So when this opportunity to move to London came I was more than happy to move. 
 'She couldn't get any flight tickets to come here on such a short notice.. She was thinking about driving here from Bangalore. It is a 19 hour drive! She won't let you leave without saying goodbye.' he said raising his voice.
Leo had  never given me a hard time about my plan to move until now. Maybe he was secretly relieved I was leaving I thought bitterly. Amy had almost turned me against my best friend. I had to weed her out of my mind. It wasn't my job to keep Amy safe. I owed her nothing I kept telling my self even as my heart was beating faster at the thought of her trying to drive all night. 
' Leo, tell her to stop making a big deal out of this.' I said as coolly as I could. 
Leo looked like he could punch me. He might have but his mobile rang and  I was saved. 
'Nick' he said. Nick was Amy new friend in Bangalore. I could hear him over Leo's call.
' what the hell, Leo!' Nick shouted into the phone. ' Why couldn't Abhi just tell us his plans before! And if he did not care about Amy making it there why the hell is Amy ready to risk driving all the way there!'
' You can't let her , Nick.' Leo pleaded.
' Of course not , even if we started now we'd have to drive all night to get there in time!'
' It is not safe...' Leo said.
' But she is desperate ,' Nick cut him off,' I am trying to see if we can get a train or a bus.'
' You are coming too?' Leo looked relieved.
' The state she is in , I can't let her come alone. You'd think this guy is dying the way she is crying'
Leo smiled weakly. He was a calmer now that Nick was on the case. He let me get back to my packing. 

Next day Nick and Amy just barely made it to the airport in time. I had checked in my bags- hugged Ash and Leo. Both of whom were in tears. Last I heard from Amy she had taken a cab to the airport from the bus stop and was on her way. I tried not to look as anxious as I was feeling. Not sure if I wanted her to come. 
I saw her running towards me. Disheveled and red eyed she was running as fast as she could. She slowed down as she saw me. And gave me a tight hug as she reached. I patted her back with one free hand. She looked up at me sad and confused at my lack of affection - now and even when deciding to leave early. But she did not confront me. She just touched my cheek with her sweaty palms and smiled tearfully 'Safe travels'.  I couldn't meet her eye.  I looked around Leo looking at me with sadness in his eyes. Or was it pity- like he knew how I would regret not holding Amy one last time. I wanted to get away from them. I tried to focus on Tanu - she was my compass. I had to get to her as soon as possible.
