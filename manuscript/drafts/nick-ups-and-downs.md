# Nick - ups and downs.

*POV: Nick | Timeline: year 4*

---

' Leo broke up with me' said Amy on the phone. I was driving back home. 
' what!?' 
' Leo broke up with me ' she repeated. 
' Are you home?'
'Yes'
' I am coming' I said taking the road to her house.

I called Leo and got his voice mail.
' what the f, Leo!' 

'Right,' I said eventually. 'We're going hiking.' 

She looked up. 

'That should help,' I said. 

'Nick—' 

'And when you fall off a cliff and die, Leo will really regret this.' 

She almost smiled. It was small and exhausted and not quite real, but it was something.

I choose the hardest trail we knew. Between climbing rocks and stopping for breath I though it would take her mind off Leo for some time.  We hiked in silence mostly. Her eyes kept brimming with tears from time to time. She wiped them and climbed ahead. Finally at the top we sat there shoulder to shoulder watching the birds fly over the river. She rested her head on my shoulder. 
' what is wrong with me , Nick? ' she said softly. 
'Where do I start' I said trying to lighten up the mood. But it did not help much.
'Why cant I just find the one. I thought Leo was the one.'
'He is the one , Amy. He is just the idiot one' she smiled as I continued,' you know he broke up only because he loves you , don't you?' 
' that is so illogical ' she said.
' I know , but tell me you know he loves you enough to let you go.'
She teared up again and nodded.
' in his mind and in his infinite stupidity, he thinks he is holding you back. he will realize his mistake and he will beg you to take him back, I promise you! And I hope you make him really beg.' 

'Amy is here to meet you. ' my secretary Maya said over the phone. ' it could be urgent, she did not even go over to check out the new coffee machine.' she added in a whisper. Amy ,unannounced? I wondered why. To be honest , with Amy, it could be anything from her showing me her new dress to her moving to moon.
' ok , what do I have next?' 
' don't worry I will cancel it. Sent her up already.' 
' thanks ' I said ,'what would I do without you. ' 

She walked in and asked quietly, ' Nick, do you have sometime.?'
.
' hmm, can you wait , I have a very important meeting right now. It will be over in an hour.'
' right, ' she said. ' I will wait outside and she turn towards the door.'
' Amy, idiot. I was kidding!' I said.they way her eyes were almost close to tears, it filled my heart with an unknown fear. What was wrong! I called Maya and asked her to clear my day as she sat on the couch.
' what's wrong?' I asked as I sat next to Amy. 
' I need help , Nick'
'of course , Amy. Anything..' I said and I kissed her forehead. 
' you remember when I fell in the snow last week while hiking in the storm' she said . ' there was a moment as I grew numb from the cold I thought all I had to do was not move from here. And it will all be over.' 
'Amy..' I gasped. ' wh..what are you sayin'
' I am not happy , Nick. It hurts to get up in the morning and go about my day. All I had to do for this pain to end was just lie there ' she continued tears rolling down from her eyes. ' but I couldn't do it.. I couldn't do it to Leo , to you, to my family.. if I die .. like that.. it would break so many hearts!' She wiped her tear and I hugged her. Ever since she broke up with Leo I had been worried for her. I knew she was hurting but I did not know it was this bad. I felt so helpless.  
' I am scared , Nick! I am trying so hard to be happy. But I am losing this battle!' She cried in my arms. 
I let her cry for some time. I needed to choose what I said next carefully. 
' Amy, you won't kill yourself. You value everyone in your life too much to do that to us. If you did not come back last weekend you know how devastated we would be. Leo would probably go mad with grief... I can't even imagine how I would wake up in a world without you.. and you know it. It is a good reason to not die. But not good enough to live. '
She looked up at me.
' You can't live hoping Leo will come back someday. You have to either get Leo back or get over him. I don't really have any advice for you Amy. That has always been your department. You have always been the hope in any dire situation, the light when things are pitch dark. Imagine if I were to be in your situation, would you want me just jump of a cliff and end it all.'
'of course not! If you died I would have brought you back and killed you!' She said smiling.
' then take your own advice. Life is too precious, specially ours! And we have to do whatever it takes to make it a happy one.' 

It is only after she left that I allowed my self to be weak. I sat down on the sofa with my head in my hands. How could I not have seen this. I drove her to the ski trip. I drove her back home from it a few days later. And I failed to see this. I could have lost her , I shuddered. 

' how is she ?' He asked instead of hello. 
' why do u care now! after u dumped her. What is she to you now!'  I said angrily.
' Nick, please. You know I love her. '
' you don't you , Leo. At least not as much as you love yourself.' I spat out.
' Nick..' he cried. .
' you did not leave to protect her! You ran off to protect your self. To save your self from having to worry for her! You don't care if she is dead as long as it isn't on your watch. ' I was practically screaming now.' I trusted you with my best friend. And you broke her heart. I want to hurt you so bad , Leo!'
' Nick, to be honest, I'd welcome it. ' he said sadly. ' any thing to numb this heart ache .'
' I don't care if you died of heart ache, Leo! I don't want to hear your sob story. I cant believe I was actually thinking of begging you to take her back...you selfish, cell centered bastard.. if anything happens to her...'
 'what is wrong,' Leo sobbed 
'..I will kill you.. ' and i cut the call over his frantic questions. He deserved it. I was shaking with anger.
