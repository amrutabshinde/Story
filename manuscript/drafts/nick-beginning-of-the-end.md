# beginning of the end

*POV: Unknown | Timeline: Unknown*

---

Nick was pacing up and down the living room anxiously waiting for Amy to call. She was going to get her reports for stomach cancer today. It had come back again after they had treated her for ovarian cancer a few years back. 
Finally his cellphone rang. 
' hey'
' hi Nick ' , she said. ' it is not good news. It is back. Nothing much they can do. they are giving me a few more months... umm.. to.. to..', she let out a long breath and left it at that. She said all that in go like she had rehearsed it multiple times in her head. 
' Amy..' Nick tried to get the words out but his throat was dry. 
' we are going home now. See you later' she said and cut the call.
Nick sat down on the floor and let out a scream of despair. The injustice of it was too much to bear. Amy was his most dear friend in the whole world. They had been through so much together. He would give away anything without a thought to keep her safe. But there was nothing he could do to help now. He was going to lose her and there was nothing he could do! He cried helplessly. 
Nick did not know how long it was that he was sitting there when his phone rang. Amy was calling again. He took a few ragged breaths and answered.
'Amy..? ' he croaked.
'Nick, I need your help.' she said. It brought him back to reality. Amy needed his help. ' It is Leo, Could you come and talk to him. He isn't talking to me. He is just sitting upstairs in our bedroom. It is just too much to deal with.. I don't know what to do..' She sounded so distressed that Nick forgot all about his sorrows. 
'Amy, Amy .. don't worry.. I am on my way' Nick said , like he had assured her a million times before , ahead of driving to her house. 

Amy looked solemn as she opened the door. Almost gave him a smile. She put her hands on his shoulders and asked quietly , 'Are you ok? ' 
He nodded stifling a sob as she pulled him into a hug.  She let him go and studied his face. 
'Leo is upstairs. He hasn't spoken since we came back' she said sounding worried. 
Nick nodded and went upstairs, unable to speak.
Leo sat at the edge of the bed. Tears running down his face.  
Nick sat next to him. Leo moved his head slightly to look at him and continued to cry. 
' What are we going to do!' he croaked. 
Nick was silent , he had no words of consolation. Both of them sat quietly for a while. Until Nick spoke, ' The thing that scares me the most , Leo, is that there is going to be a day when I wake up and she will not. ' and Nick's voice cracked.' and I don't know how I am going to live after that. I don't know what I am going to do then...i have always needed her to hold me together ... I was in pieces when she told me this morning. I sat in the empty house crying.  And then she called. She said she needed my help to look after you. When I came I realized she did not need my help. She just wanted to make sure both of us were under her roof so she could take care of us.... After all she is going through- she is worrying about us, Leo. We are such a mess!'
Leo sobbed. 
'We can't do that to her. We have to care for her not the other way round. We have eternity to cry about our lose. She has a few months. We have to be stronger than this.' 
Leo nodded. 
' Go talk to her.' Nick said.' she needs you' 
Leo stood up wiping his tears. Nick sat there as Leo walked downstairs. 
' What are you doing?' he heard Leo exclaim in surprise. 
' Cooking.' Amy replied. ' we still need to eat.' she said in her matter of fact voice. 
Despite the situation Nick couldn't help a weak smile. She  hasn't given up as yet. And so neither would he.
