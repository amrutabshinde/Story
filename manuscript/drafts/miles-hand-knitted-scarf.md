# Miles- hand knitted scarf

*POV: Miles | Timeline: present day*

---

It was Nicks birthday the next day. His family was throwing him a big party just as usual. It might do him good. Could even cheer up his mood to meet with his friends and cousins. Heavens know he needs some thing go cheer him up.

He was strolling in the lawn alone when Ami and I arrived.

Ami gave him a hug and I kissed his cheek.

 "Is is good you guys come" he said hugging me

"Miles wouldn't miss your birthday for the world", Ami said smiling. "I'll let you two catch up. I need to get something to eat"

Ami ran indoors.

"How are you doing, Nick?" I asked Nick as  walked across the lawn with him. His voice always sounded sad these days. But up close in person he looked devastated. I felt sorry for leaving him alone hear. With no Amy or Leo, he would be feeling lonely.

"I am trying hard to be happy", he said earnestly.

"Would you want to come and live in Pune for a few days. You can work from there " I suggested.

"Maybe I will, " he said ." It is difficult living so close to Amy and not being able to see her"

"I know it must hurt" I said ," Things will improve"

"That is what i though when I came back. Things are just going from bad to worse. I am being punished for causing Amy so much pain. It is like life is trying to throw me out of her life so that she can be safe."

"You cant keep thinking like that Nick. That is so not true. "

" Amy would have been better off without me in her life. I have almost gotten her killed multiple times. She would have been a lot safer if she hadn't met me"

" I am sure Amy would have chosen you over being safe. And you know it too" 

" good for her that she doesn't have to make the choice now. I am already out of her life. It is like I never was. Probably it is better this way" 

 " what are you waiting for so anxiously!? " Nick finally asked.

" your gift" I replied, " it is on its way, I want to give it to you before you cut the cake"

he gave me a puzzled expression.

"You will love it, i promise" i said.

Nick just smiled and nodded.

Finally I saw Leo' s car come in through the gate. Nick did not realize Leo was here until Leo was standing right behind him.

" happy birthday , Nick" Leo exclaimed.

Nick spun around to face him. He just stared at Leo for some seconds before he found his voice. 

" Leo, thanks man! I can't believe you are here" 

" how could I miss your birthday party, even though u did not invite me" did leo in a mock angry voice.

" oh cut the crap" Nick said and hugged Leo. " I am glad you are here.. Where is ..."Nick began to say when he saw Amy silently standing behind Leo. She was just smiling looking around. It was good to see her in person. The last i had seen her was on the bed in the hospital sleeping. And then she did not remember us when she woke up. 

 Nick let Leo go and stood in front of Amy.

"Amy, that's Nick, he is our friend " Leo said.

" Hi " he said nervously.

" Hi ," Amy said cheerfully shaking his hand, " happy birthday!!"

" thanks" said Nick not letting go of Amy's hand, " thanks for coming. How are you doing! " 

"I am fine, thank you, " Amy said smiling. "Thank you for having us here.'

"yeah, absolutely.You are safe and sound, that's all that matters really." said nick seriously.

"And that is Miles. We know her too" Leo interrupted before the conversation could turn sad.

"Hi , Miles. I am sorry. I just cant remember" Amy said.

"Amy, it is alright , don't stress yourself. Miles doesn't mind" Leo said placing his hands on her shoulder.

"Of course, sweetie we don't mind. We are just happy you are well."  I said and hugged her.  

" We got Nick a gift. " said Leo.

" Oh yes the gift" , Amy pulled a wrapped box out of her purse and handed it to Nick. " here you go"

Nick held the box with both his hand like a child holding on to a precious toy.

"Open it" Amy said rubbing her hands. 

It was a scarf. Amy had been knitting it for Nick for ages.

" Finished it last week, Leo says I was knitting this for you" she said. "It was so pretty I wanted to keep it to myself!" 

" You can , Amy. " said Nick politely. " You can keep it if you love it so much"

" nah, I was just kidding , it was meant for you, Leo says" she smiled. 

" thanks " said Nick smiling. His first true smile I had seen all day. " it is so nice of you to knit this for me. Specially after you are still recovering."

" nah , m just fine. It was not much effort. Specially as I don't remember knitting most of it.",  Amy said laughing and shrugging here shoulder..

"I think we should move."Leo said, looking at all the people standing around waiting to talk to nick.

"I'll see you around" Nick said reluctantly but the smile still intact on his face.

"That was the best gift ever!" nick screamed as he hugged me. "I cant thank you enough Miles!"

He was visibly happier as her met other people.

Leo and Amy sat on the table as Nick and me waltzed. Nick was grinning from ear to ear. The shine in his eyes was back. Oh, how i had missed those awesome dimples! He kept stealing glance at the table where Amy sat.

"I am sorry" he said still grinning like a mad man, "I cant help but imagine if this is a dream."

"I understand" I said . I didn't mind. His happiness was infectious. May be things will start getting back to normal now I thought happily.
