# Miles-At the cost of danger.

*POV: Miles | Timeline: year 5*

---

The three of us sat in the same place for quite sometime. I was relieve they had lied to Nick, but then there was a guilt that we were keeping Nick here at the cost of the danger Amy and Leo were in. Every time I looked at them I felt a rush of gratitude toward Amy, no wonder Nick loves her so. Leo and Amy loved Nick back selflessly. 

After a while Nick came running through the open door. The tension clearly showing on his face.Beads of sweat shining on his brow. "Nick" , Amy said before Nick could say anything, "You scared these guys so much for nothing"

"How are you ? Did anyone hurt you "Nick asked ignoring her statement.

"Of course not. No one was here. Except these two banging on my door. I am perfectly fine. Stop acting like a paranoid" Amy said as Nick walked towards her. He pulled her into a hug and stood there with his eyes shut. He held her long like that. 

Only when Amy screamed "Nick, you are strangling me" did he finally let he go.  

Leo made us all some coffee. Nick sat on the sofa sipping his coffee quietly. What was he thinking. Even Amy kept glancing at him nervously.

"This is really getting out of hand." Nick said finally.

"But Nick, they are trying to scare you." Amy said.

"Yeah, they cant probably come here. We have guards and all." said Leo.

Given all the trauma these guys had just been through, I admire the brav faces they were putting up.

"They could have" said Nick looking at Leo.

"But they did not , Nick" , said Amy with desperation in her voice. "They are trying to scare you to go back Nick. Just don't give in. " she got up and started pacing wringing her hands "Stop overreacting , Nick. you don't have to take the blame for every little incident. Running away just isn't the solution. That's what they want you to do." she looked at Nick who looked back silently. " You are not going back" she said with even more agony, "I just wont let you go. I almost died trying to get you back here. You are not leaving me again!"

Nikhil's had an amused look on his face now. Honestly Amy did look funny walking around like that ranting away. Leo was rolling his eyes.

"Amy, Relax"said nick smiling." I never said I was going back."

"Oh" said Amy looking a bit embarrassed "OK" she said trying to hold back a smile. She went inside the kitchen.

The rest of us in the living room burst out laughing.  Nikhil followed her into the kitchen. Imitating her tone. 

Nikhil was quiet while driving me back home. I was having my own thought battle to deal with. Did Nikhil know we were lying.Was he thinking of returning back. I felt guilty of wanting Amy to lie to him to keep him here. I only realized we had reached my house when Nikhil said "Good night, Miles"

"Want to come in for dinner" I asked as I took off the seat belt.

"Meeting Dad for dinner" he said.

"Cool, Good night then" I got out of the car and walked towards the gate.

Should I tell him the truth. But he will definitely want to go back to Germany then. But Amy could have been hurt. I looked back. Nikhil was still waiting for me to get inside the house. He waved towards me and smiled.

I walked back and got into the car.

"Nick, there is something you need to know. They were there at Amy;s place tonight"

He did not look surprised. Instead he let out a sigh and said "I know"

He pulled out a bunch of keys from his pocket. His bunch of keys to Amy's house. But he had lost them last week. He had made Amy change all her locks when he couldn't find them.

"Found them outside Amy's door"

"that means.. "

"That means someone stole it from me and tried to use it to get in"

"If you wouldn't have made change the locks.."

"Locks are not going to keep them out , Miles. I need to find a way of stopping this. Can you tell me what Amy told you" he said.

I narrated all I knew. How they had cut off the power to her house. and then the phones. And how they had banged on the door. How Amy had hoarded up furniture against the door and locked herself up in the bedroom. Nick tried to keep his face calm. But his hands gave him away. He was clutching the steering wheel so tightly that his knuckles had turned white.

"So?"I asked. Did he want to go back again.

"I have to find a way. I can't ..just can't see people getting hurt because of me"he said earnestly.

"I understand"  I said. my heart sinking. He is going to go back.

Nikhil's mobile rang. He read the message and smiled. "Miles I am not going back. We need to find another way out. Living away from you is very painful ..." he paused and  said "And besides Amy just wont let me " he said as he passed me his phone. It was a message from Amy, It said "I will find you. I will hunt you. And I will make you eat the chicken sandwiches I made today.;)"
