# ...

*POV: Unknown | Timeline: Unknown*

---

'yoo hoo' she screamed over the phone into my ear. I am sure even my wife and my kids sitting in the next room heard her.
She was attending a conference in NY next month. Turns our my firm was sending me for the same one as well. She was so excited to be able to meet me. We have been friends for over 2 decades now. She is one of my best friends. We moved to different towns , had our own families and kids , got old now but we have kept in touch. I was looking forward to meet her in person as well .Her excitement was infectious. I went on smiling from ear to ear all week after the call.

On the day before the conference she reached the hotel before me. She was so impatient to see me  that she was pacing the lobby when i arrived. 
' dont spend time checking in just keep your bags in my room for now ' she said rushing me. She gave me 5 whole min to freshen up and then pulled me out of the hotelShe had already chosen a place to have dinner and researched options for desert after. We had ramen and walked a few blocks to have a cheese cake. It was nice to be here without any responsibilities just the two of us after a very long time. Last 15 years or so we have always met with the kids and spouses and that left very little time for us to catch up one to one. This was a pleasant change. She was just like i remembered her - trying to get a lot of things done at once. Making sure things went exactly as she had planned. I had to keep reminding her to breathe. It felt like we had time -travelled decades in the past. 
We were on our way back when it started raining heavily. We got completely drenched as we ran towards our hotel. I followed her up to her room to change since my luggage was still there. She changed in the shower while I put on dry clothes in the room.  She walked out of the shower with a towel in her hand which she flung over a chair and she sat down on the little sofa in the room. 
'Dry your hair or you are going to catch a cold ' I warned seeing her wet hair dripping water all over the floor. 
' I already did' she lied switching on the tv and flicking through the channels.
I picked up the towel she had kept on the chair and sat next to her. 
' c'mon, look at me, let me get the water out. You are ruining the sofa as well.'
She made a face but obliged.
I put the towel around her head and rubbed it. 
' stay still'  i said as she wriggled.
' you are going to pull my hair out' she protested trying to get out of the towel.
' stop being a baby' i laughed.
It happed in a split of a second. One moment I had her head in my hands and the next moment I leaned in and was kissing her. What was more bizarre was that she kissed me back. 'What was i doing!' i thought as i felt her cold hands on my neck. We have families back home. This was irresponsible . I forced my self to stop.
' stop.. stop .. stop..' I said as I pushed her away.
She first had a confused look on her face which immediately turned to horror when she suddenly realized what we had done. I turned away and put my face in my hands. How could I lose control like this! This little fragile co existence we had carved for us couldn't survive anything like this. Somewhere a little voice in my mind was saying to kiss her back. I was having a hard time restraining it. I heard her sniffle next to me. Then I felt her forehead touch my arm. She was sobbing now. I put my arm around her and she cried. I know how she felt. We have always toed the line of being more than friends but never dared to move ahead. Cursed to always walk together but still never be together. It was a cruel fate. Even if I tried I could not get rid of the spark for her in my heart and neither could she for me. We were happy where we were . I loved my wife and she loved her husband. But there was a vaccuum which no one else could fill. And how it hurt! I found tears in my eyes too. I hugged her tight and wept. 
We must have fallen asleep like that cause I woke up in the early morning hours on the sofa . She was sleeping with her head on my chest. I sat there for sometime just drinking in the peace of the moment as I hugged her. Once she was up we would have to go along the day like nothing happened. 
It had become chilly. I tried to move her so I could get her a blanket.
' dont move ' she said eyes still closed.
' you are awake? ' i asked , surprised.
' just pretend i am sleeping and dont move'  she said as she dug her head in my chest.
' ok,' i smiled as i moved her hair back from her face and kissed her forehead.
' sometimes i wish we would have never met.' she said softly, eyes still closed. 
' yeah?' i asked a bit hurt. 
' we would just have avoided so much trouble. We would be so much happier.' she said looking up at me.
' We would be so much lonelier.' I added. 
'But not heart broken atleast.' she said sadly.
I held her arms and moved her to face me, ' No amount of heart ache is going to make me wish you away. Do you understand.' 
She nodded and sighed  and said, 'Besides can't change much now, it is what it is' she got up from the sofa towards the coffee machine. 
' it is what it is ' i repeated and followed.
