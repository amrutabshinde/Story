# Leo - last

*POV: Leo | Timeline: Unknown*

---

As Leo entered the escalator to his apartment, he thought about the last time he had been there. It was 5 months back.... seemed like ages ago. Last time he had been here with Amy. She had come from some trip with her friends and had come straight here. The entire day they spent enjoying themselves playing cards , watching movies and cooking. While returning to Pune he had finally dared to propose to her. She replied just as he had expected. She wouldn't go against her parent’s wishes. That was a relief. Leo’s family and Amy’s had been friends for long. There would not be a problem as far as he could tell.

Leo could never understand how he fell in love with Amy. True he always cared for her, but it was because she was so naive. It was almost compulsive feeling protective towards her. “Princess Amy” he would joke when she cribbed about the sun troubling her. When her job took her to a different city, Leo was worried. Not that she was a fool. But she trusted people a lot. And was hurt at slightest possible thing. As Amy’s friend put it, ‘ she was born in the wrong era. She should have been born in the age of Gods. ‘ She could at times be clumsy and forgetful. But you couldn't help loving her.

Leo couldn't help noticing how much he missed her. When he moved to Mumbai she was the only family he had here. When Amy had an accident where she hurt her leg, Leo had been there to help her recover. Sometime around this time he realized he could not care for anyone else as he did for her.

When their marriage was finally approved by their family members, Leo had had this apartment renovated. He got a cane swing just as she loved. He got shelfs where she could keep her “Harry Potter”s and Famous fives. He searched for the lamp she had once loved and put it in what would be their room. And he got her a ring , just like the one she always wanted . The one in LOTR but with a small diamond in it.

It was on the day he was to give her the ring that his world came crumbling down. She said they couldn't marry. She had been diagnosed with heart disease.

He could never forget the last time they met. She died in his arms, as if she was keeping death away just to fall into his arms. “Love ya loads” she whispered in his ears before breathing her last.

He entered his apartment. As he kept his bag on the floor he saw the cards they had played last time still scattered on the floor. The chocolate wrappers still lying on the sofa. There in his kitchen was Amy’s scarf, she’d forgotten last time. And he fell to the floor and wept.
