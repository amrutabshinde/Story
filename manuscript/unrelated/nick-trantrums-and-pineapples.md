# Nick - trantrums and pineapples

*POV: Nick | Timeline: Unknown*

---

I was taking to Miles on Skype. With our wedding next year there were a lot of details discuss. We were thinking of wedding someplace north and reception in Pune when Amy walked in through my front door unannounced. 

' I am not going back home tonight. I am mad at Leo, I am staying here tonight' she declared.  

I must have looked shocked , cause miles looked at me with an amused expression on her face. She shrugged and smiled. Another of Amy's tantrums were coming along.  She started humming the tune to our song 'Amy's tantrum'. 

'Miles you have to support me. I am not throwing a tantrum this time. ' Amy said.

' you always say that ' I said. 

' what is it amy, what has leo done this time. ' Miles asked her.

' I can't tell you that , ' she said and Miles raised an eyebrow,' you both will support him then. I just need a place to stay tonight, stop asking me questions like the police. I am hungry . get off my back ' she went to the kitchen probably to eat but mostly to avoid us questioning her.

' tell Leo she is here' Miles said, ' he'd be wondering where she stormed off to.' 

' yeah right. Will drop her home once she cools down. I said and dialed Leo.

' Leo ,dude, your woman is here. '

' oh , yeah, like she would go anywhere else.' he said 'Just let her stay there tonight, make sure she has her dinner. I will explain later. ' 

I could have asked what was wrong but Amy came out with a bowl and fruits she had dug out from the fridge. 

' fruit salad ' she said. ' I wish you had pineapples I feel like eating pineapples.'

She sat on the dining table cutting them , me and miles that is the laptop sat next to her. 

' Amy,' I said,' isn't it stupid running off from your house every time you guys have a fight.' 

' I did not want to stay there and fight , nick, and I was hungry' she said as she tossed a piece of apple in her mouth. 

' nick, get her a proper meal' Miles said, ' Amy, eat something more than just fruits....  Leo must be worried about you. Did you tell him you are coming here. ' trying to make Amy feel guilty 

' i'm not talking to him. Anyways where else am I going to go!' She said eating a piece of an apple. 

' Amy stop being a kid.' I said.

' m very hungry nick, let's talk after dinner. You sure you don't have pineapples, I feel like eating some.' She called as I went in the kitchen to set up dinner.

I found pineapple juice in the fridge for her. Hopefully that would suffice her. There was briyani I had made in the afternoon. I served it out for her. And I added some chocolates. She devoured everything in a few minutes. She kept talking about some movie during the dinner with her mouth full of food. When is she ever going to grow up I wondered. 

She sat on the sofa watching TV. I continued talking to Miles as Amy played  some Disney movie with loads of songs. After about half an hour she yawned and said ' I am sleepy' and dropped on the sofa. ' Amy, go sleep in the bed room. ' I said smiling. She dragged her feet to the bedroom. She was asleep by the time I went in to check 10 mins later. 

I called Leo to tell him that his wife was finally asleep.

'Thanks dude. ' he replied

' would you be kind enough to tell me what do I owe the pleasure of having your wife at my place.'

' hmm... Can you conference Miles,  there is something I need to tell you guys.'

' We have some news .Amy is pregnant , we are having a baby.' He said in one breath. I was trying to digest what he had just said.
' oh my god , that's great news ' Miles chirped. My voice was stuck in my throat. Amy was with a baby! I was feeling a bit dizzy now. Little Amy having a little kid! It was a bit hard to imagine.
 Your wedding would  when she would be 7 to 8 months pregnant. I told her we might not be able to travel for your wedding. And she said she did not want to discuss and stormed out.' he continued. ' nick ' Leo called out . ' nick, u okay? ' miles said . ' I m just... ', I began feeling overwhelmed. ' it is great' I managed to say softly. 
'I wanted to tell you face to face but given the circumstances...' Leo was saying.   
'She wanted to eat pineapples today' I remembered,.' Let me try n get some before she wakes' 
Miles was smiling at me, Leo sounded annoyed, ' Nick, it is close to midnight. Just stay where you are. She' d anyways might want something else tomorrow.' 

' miles what options do we have ' I asked . 

' We can push the wedding out a few months. '

' yeah that might be doable ' I said 
' you are not...' Leo began

' yes we are ' miles and me said together. ' I am not getting married without Amy.' I added.
' We haven't finalized anything anyways. '  Miles said.

' You guys are crazy' said Leo.  

' like Amy , like friends ' said miles .

Leo dropped off. Miles stared at me from the laptop screen smiling.,

' we are going to uncle and aunt' she screamed. ' oh my god!! Amy is having a baby!'  I just kept smiling stupidly. I couldn't  just find words to say anything. ' uncle nick , are you ok?' 

I nodded, 'I m just so happy' I said. 

She smiled' good night nick'

'Good night , aunt miles' I said.

I sat on the sofa unable to sleep. This news called for a celebration. Maybe I should call Leo and call him over. 

' Leo, dude were you sleeping' 

' is Amy OK?' He asked.

' she is fine , ' I could hear car honking on the phone.' Where are you?' 

'I am right outside your main gate , coming to your place in a few minutes'

And he hung up. 

' what do think my house is , a bnb, both husband and wife drop in any time' I taunted as Leo walked in. 

He walked straight to the bed room ignoring me. I followed.

He knelt down next to her and running his hands over her head. He put a bag of sliced pineapples on the table next to her bed.

 Leo rose and said looking up at me.' nick dude, I am going be a dad!'

I smiled and hugged him. ' and you, an uncle' he added.
