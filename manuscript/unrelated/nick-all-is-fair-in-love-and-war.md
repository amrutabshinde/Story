Nikhil was on his way to office. It would be his first day in the new office in Pune. He was excited. He had worked hard for this to come true. For him to be able to work with urmilla. He smiled to himself as he thought of her. He checked his reflection in the mirror. He had worn the shirt Amy had bought for him. Blue to match his eyes. She always said no girl could resist falling for him with this look. This time he hoped it were true at least for this one girl. He had been enchanted by her the very first time he met her. Was it her looks or her cool attitude, he couldn't say. But he knew he was head over heels in love with her. He had spent month coming up with proposal for a new office site in pune. Delicately suggested his father to have urmilla work with him. He even bought a house close to urmilla's . Finally here he was . he checked his reflection in the mirror as he parked his car. 'All the best'  he wished his reflection.
